# AI Security Startup Upwind Raises $400M, Valuation Soars to $3.8B

**Severity:** informational | **Category:** Cloud Security,Other | **Updated:** 2026-10-28 | **Reading time:** 3 min

Upwind Security, a startup specializing in cloud and AI application security, has closed a $400 million Series C funding round, pushing its valuation to $3.8 billion. The round, led by Bessemer Venture Partners, reflects surging investor interest and confidence in platforms designed to secure complex cloud environments and the rapidly growing deployment of AI models and agents.

## Executive Summary
On October 28, 2026, **Upwind Security**, a San Francisco-based startup focused on cloud and AI security, announced the closure of a $400 million Series C funding round. The investment, led by **[Bessemer Venture Partners](https://www.bvp.com/)**, more than doubles the company's valuation to $3.8 billion, up from $1.5 billion in January 2026. This substantial funding round underscores the immense market demand and investor confidence in solutions that address the security challenges of modern cloud infrastructure and the increasing use of AI agents in enterprise environments. The new capital will be used to accelerate product development and expand go-to-market efforts.

---

## Funding Details
- **Funding Round:** $400 million, Series C.
- **Lead Investor:** Bessemer Venture Partners.
- **Other Participants:** Canapi Ventures, and existing investors Greylock Partners, Craft Ventures, and TCV.
- **Total Raised to Date:** $830 million.
- **Valuation:** $3.8 billion.

This funding event is particularly notable as it comes shortly after reports that **Upwind** rejected a nearly $4 billion acquisition offer from **Datadog**, signaling the company's confidence in its independent growth trajectory and the high value the market places on its specialized technology.

---

## Company and Technology Overview
Founded in 2022 by the entrepreneurial team behind Spot.io (which was acquired by NetApp for $450 million), **Upwind Security** has rapidly emerged as a key player in the cloud security space. The company's platform provides runtime-native security, which involves monitoring cloud infrastructure, APIs, and AI agents as they operate to detect active threats.

Key features of the platform include:
- **Cloud Native Application Protection Platform (CNAPP):** Offers comprehensive security across the cloud application lifecycle.
- **Runtime Security:** Detects threats like malware, zero-day exploits, and suspicious activity in real-time within live cloud environments.
- **AI Security:** The platform was recently expanded to specifically address risks associated with the deployment of large language models (LLMs) and autonomous AI agents.

Upwind's CEO, Amiram Shachar, highlighted the growing threat landscape, noting that AI tools have lowered the barrier to entry for malicious actors, making robust, AI-aware security platforms more critical than ever.

---

## Market Context and Business Impact
The massive funding round and valuation surge reflect several key market trends:
- **Explosive Growth:** Upwind reports its revenue has quadrupled in 2026 and is approaching a $100 million annual recurring revenue (ARR) projection, indicating rapid customer adoption.
- **High Demand for AI Security:** As enterprises increasingly adopt AI, they are confronting new security risks. Investors are betting heavily on companies like Upwind that are positioned to secure these new workloads.
- **Consolidation and High Valuations:** The cybersecurity market, particularly in cloud and AI security, is seeing intense competition and high valuations, as evidenced by the rejected acquisition offer from Datadog.

Upwind's customer base includes hundreds of organizations such as **Siemens**, **Roku**, and **Carvana**. The platform integrates with all major cloud providers, including **AWS**, **Microsoft Azure**, **Google Cloud**, and **Oracle Cloud**, giving it a broad addressable market. The new capital will enable Upwind to further invest in engineering to enhance its product and to scale its sales and marketing teams to capture more of this growing market.
