# Arizona's IMS Care Center Discloses Breach of Patient Data

**Severity:** medium | **Category:** Data Breach,Phishing,Regulatory | **Updated:** 2026-10-21 | **Reading time:** 3 min

IMS Care Center, a large physician-owned medical group in Arizona, has disclosed a data breach affecting its patients. The incident, which occurred in June 2026, involved unauthorized access to the company's corporate email system. An investigation completed in late September determined that sensitive patient information, including names, addresses, Social Security numbers, and medical data, was potentially exposed. The healthcare provider is now notifying affected individuals and offering complimentary credit monitoring services.

## Executive Summary
**[IMS Care Center, LLC](https://www.imsaz.com/)**, a large multispecialty medical group based in Phoenix, Arizona, has reported a data breach that exposed the personal and protected health information (PHI) of its patients. According to a notification filed with the New Hampshire Attorney General on October 16, 2026, the breach resulted from an unauthorized party gaining access to the company's corporate email environment. The incident occurred in June 2026, and a subsequent investigation confirmed that sensitive data, including Social Security numbers and medical information, may have been compromised. IMS Care Center is now in the process of notifying affected individuals and has begun offering identity theft protection services.

## Incident Overview
The timeline of the incident is as follows:
-   **June 17-18, 2026:** An unauthorized party gained access to certain accounts within IMS Care Center's corporate email system.
-   **June 18, 2026:** The company detected the unusual activity and immediately launched an investigation with the assistance of external cybersecurity experts.
-   **September 30, 2026:** A lengthy and detailed review of the affected email accounts was completed. This review identified the specific types of data that were potentially exposed.
-   **October 16, 2026:** IMS Care Center began mailing notification letters to individuals whose information was identified in the compromised accounts.

The attack vector appears to be a compromise of the email environment, which is a common target in the healthcare industry, often resulting from successful phishing campaigns or credential theft.

## Data Exposed
The investigation determined that the compromised email accounts contained a wide range of sensitive patient information. The types of data exposed could include:
-   **Personal Identifiers:** Full Name, Address
-   **Social Security Numbers (SSNs)**
-   **Protected Health Information (PHI):** Medical information, which could include diagnoses, treatment details, and patient history.

The exposure of both SSNs and PHI makes this a particularly serious breach, as it puts affected individuals at risk of both financial identity theft and other malicious activities.

## Response Actions
In response to the breach, IMS Care Center has taken several steps:
-   **Investigation:** Engaged third-party cybersecurity professionals to investigate the scope and nature of the incident.
-   **Security Enhancements:** The company reports that it is reviewing its existing policies and procedures and implementing additional security measures to prevent future incidents.
-   **Notification:** Notifying relevant regulatory bodies (such as state Attorneys General) and affected individuals as required by law.
-   **Support for Victims:** Offering 12 months of complimentary credit monitoring and identity theft restoration services through Cyberscout (a TransUnion service).

## Impact on Individuals
Individuals affected by this data breach face a significant risk of harm. With their names, SSNs, and medical information exposed, they are vulnerable to:
-   **Identity Theft:** Criminals can use SSNs to open new lines of credit, file fraudulent tax returns, or apply for benefits.
-   **Medical Identity Theft:** Malicious actors could use stolen medical information to receive medical care, purchase prescription drugs, or submit fraudulent claims to insurance providers.
-   **Targeted Phishing:** The stolen data could be used to craft highly convincing phishing scams targeting the victims.

Due to these risks, law firms specializing in class-action lawsuits have already announced investigations into the incident.

## Recommendations for Affected Individuals
IMS Care Center and cybersecurity best practices recommend that affected individuals take the following steps:
1.  **Enroll in Credit Monitoring:** Take advantage of the free credit monitoring service offered by IMS Care Center to receive alerts on new activity.
2.  **Place a Fraud Alert or Credit Freeze:** Contact the major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) to place a fraud alert or, for stronger protection, a credit freeze on your files.
3.  **Review Financial and Medical Statements:** Carefully review all bank, credit card, and explanation of benefits (EOB) statements for any suspicious activity.
4.  **Be Wary of Phishing:** Be extra cautious of unsolicited emails, text messages, or phone calls that ask for personal information, as they may be attempts to leverage the stolen data.
