# TigerByte Cyber Launches with $3M to Secure AI and Edge Devices

**Severity:** informational | **Category:** Policy and Compliance,Threat Intelligence,Other | **Updated:** 2026-09-20 | **Reading time:** 3 min

TigerByte Cyber, a cybersecurity startup specializing in hardening AI and mission-critical edge devices, has emerged from stealth with $3 million in seed funding. The New Hampshire-based firm has already secured over $7 million in contracts with U.S. government agencies, including the Space Force and Navy. TigerByte's Cyber Protection Suite (CPS) is a compact, hardware-enforced solution designed to bring advanced security features like post-quantum encryption to legacy systems.

## Executive Summary
On September 19, 2026, **TigerByte Cyber**, a cybersecurity startup based in Hanover, New Hampshire, officially launched its public operations after securing $3 million in a seed funding round. The company specializes in providing advanced cyber hardening for legacy systems, with a particular focus on mission-critical edge devices and Artificial Intelligence (AI) platforms. Despite just emerging from stealth, TigerByte has already built significant momentum, having been awarded over $7 million in contracts from U.S. government and defense agencies, including the **[U.S. Space Force](https://www.spaceforce.mil/)**, the **[U.S. Navy](https://www.navy.mil/)**, and **[DARPA](https://www.darpa.mil/)**. The funding round was led by Hale Capital Partners and Tenon VC.

## Company Overview
- **Company:** TigerByte Cyber
- **Focus Area:** Cyber hardening for AI and edge devices, particularly legacy systems in critical environments.
- **Flagship Product:** Cyber Protection Suite (CPS)
- **Key Technology:** The CPS is described as an "ultra-compact, plug-and-play" solution that provides hardware-enforced security. This suggests it may be a hardware module or 'bump-in-the-wire' device that can be added to existing systems without requiring software modification.
- **Capabilities:** The suite reportedly delivers data validation, deep packet inspection, network segmentation, and post-quantum encryption.

## Market Significance
The launch of TigerByte Cyber is significant for several reasons:
1.  **Addressing a Critical Gap:** Many mission-critical systems in defense, aerospace, and industrial sectors are legacy systems that are difficult to patch or upgrade. A plug-and-play hardware solution that adds modern security capabilities without re-architecting the underlying system is a highly attractive proposition.
2.  **Focus on AI and Edge:** As AI and edge computing become more prevalent in military and industrial applications, securing these devices becomes paramount. TigerByte is positioning itself at the intersection of these key technology trends.
3.  **Post-Quantum Encryption:** The inclusion of post-quantum encryption (PQC) is forward-looking, addressing the future threat that quantum computers will pose to current encryption standards. This is particularly important for systems with long lifecycles, such as those in defense and critical infrastructure.
4.  **Government Trust:** The fact that TigerByte has already secured $7 million in contracts from demanding clients like the Space Force and DARPA before its public launch serves as a strong validation of its technology and team.

## Impact Assessment
- **Business Impact:** The $3 million in seed funding will likely be used to scale operations, expand sales and marketing efforts, and further develop the Cyber Protection Suite. The strong government backing provides a stable revenue base and a high-profile reference customer set.
- **Industry Impact:** If successful, TigerByte's approach could influence how legacy systems are secured. It represents a shift from software-centric solutions to hardware-enforced security, which can offer stronger guarantees of isolation and integrity.
- **For Defenders:** The availability of such a product could provide a new tool for securing operational technology (OT) and other hard-to-protect environments. Security leaders in manufacturing, energy, and defense should monitor the development of this technology as a potential solution for their own legacy system challenges.

## Compliance Guidance
While not a compliance article, the technology offered by TigerByte could help organizations in the Defense Industrial Base (DIB) meet evolving cybersecurity requirements, such as the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC). The hardware-enforced segmentation and advanced encryption capabilities align with many of the controls required for protecting Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

## Mitigation
TigerByte's product itself is a mitigation. It aims to provide:
- **Network Segmentation:** [`M1030 - Network Segmentation`](https://attack.mitre.org/mitigations/M1030/) enforced at a hardware level to isolate critical legacy devices.
- **Data Encryption:** Protection against data interception, including future threats from quantum computing.
- **Data Validation:** A form of deep packet inspection to filter malicious or malformed commands sent to a device, preventing exploitation.
