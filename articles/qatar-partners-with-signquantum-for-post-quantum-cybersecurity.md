# Qatar Partners with SignQuantum for Post-Quantum Cybersecurity

**Severity:** informational | **Category:** Policy and Compliance,Regulatory,Other | **Updated:** 2026-11-16 | **Reading time:** 1 min

The government of Qatar is taking proactive steps to prepare for the threat of quantum computing by partnering with the cybersecurity firm SignQuantum. The collaboration, led by Qatar's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT), will focus on exploring applications for post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and developing quantum-resistant digital signatures to secure government services and data against future threats.

## Executive Summary
The State of Qatar, through its **[Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT)](https://www.mcit.gov.qa/en)**, has signed a collaboration agreement with cybersecurity firm **SignQuantum** to bolster the nation's defenses against the future threat of quantum computing. Announced on November 10, 2026, the partnership aims to proactively explore and implement post-quantum cryptography (PQC). The focus will be on developing use cases for quantum-resistant digital signatures and other cryptographic technologies to ensure the long-term security and integrity of Qatar's digital infrastructure and government services, in line with its Digital Agenda 2030.
