# AI Fuels Cyberattacks, Critical Flaws & Record Ransomware Emerge

**Published:** 2026-11-25 | **Articles:** 10

**AI-Driven Threats Accelerate and Expand:**

*   **AI-Powered Hacking Tools Like CyberStrikeAI Used in Japan Attacks:** The UK's NCSC warns of a surge in AI-assisted cyberattacks since September 2026, with threat actors using frameworks like ARTEX to automate exploitation of common weaknesses like exposed credentials and misconfigurations.
*   **FBI Warns of ARTEX, an AI-Powered Tool for Autonomous Cyberattacks:** Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 confirms over ten global campaigns in Q3 2026 using autonomous AI agents that compress attack timelines to hours, identifying unique detection indicators such as inhuman command velocity and structured command headers.
*   **AI-Assisted Campaign by GreyNoise13 Hits 22 Orgs via Check Point Flaws:** A threat actor, GreyNoise13, has compromised at least 22 organizations globally using an AI-assisted campaign targeting Check Point Security Management servers, exploiting vulnerabilities in Check Point, Citrix, and Ivanti products.

**Critical Vulnerabilities Under Active Exploitation:**

*   **VMware Patches Critical 9.8-Rated vCenter Auth Bypass Flaw:** Broadcom has released an advisory for CVE-2026-123903, a critical vCenter authentication bypass flaw with a network attack vector and low complexity, urging immediate patching due to the high likelihood of exploit development.
*   **CISA Adds Actively Exploited Drupal, Optimizely, and AntiCsrf Flaws to KEV:** CISA has added three vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog: a Drupal Core RCE flaw (CVE-2026-132165), an Idunno.AntiCsrf deserialization flaw (CVE-2026-131914), and an Optimizely Web Experimentation state control issue (CVE-2026-132224).
*   **Two Critical GeoServer Flaws Actively Exploited for RCE:** Threat actors are actively exploiting two critical GeoServer vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129243) to achieve unauthenticated remote code execution, with exploitation observed shortly after patch releases.
*   **NGINX HTTP/2 Use-After-Free Flaw (CVE-2026-123633) Allows DoS, RCE:** A critical use-after-free vulnerability in NGINX HTTP/2 (CVE-2026-123633) allows for denial of service and potential remote code execution, particularly when chained with a memory disclosure bug.
*   **CISA Adds 10 More Exploited Flaws to KEV from Danfoss, HPE, Mitel, Vates:** CISA has added ten more vulnerabilities impacting products from Danfoss, HPE, Mitel, and Vates to its KEV catalog, indicating active exploitation and mandating remediation for federal agencies.

**Ransomware and Data Exposure Trends:**

*   **Ransomware Attacks Shatter Records in Q3 2026:** Check Point Research reports record-breaking ransomware activity in Q3 2026, highlighting high-profile victims and a fragmented threat landscape with 117 active ransomware brands, where the top 10 gangs now account for only 49% of victims.
*   **Massive Data Leak Exposes Two Billion Credentials from Infostealer Ops:** A publicly accessible, non-password-protected database has been discovered containing approximately two billion credentials, believed to be aggregated from infostealer malware campaigns, posing a significant risk of account takeovers.

## Articles in this publication
- [AI-Powered Hacking Tools Like CyberStrikeAI Used in Japan Attacks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ai-powered-hacking-tools-like-cyberstrikeai-used-in-attacks-on-japan/) (high)

One notable campaign leveraged an open-source AI penetration testing framework named CyberStrikeAI, which used a swarm of 44 AI agents to automate the entire attack lifecycle, from reconnaissance to data exfiltration, against at least 19 organizations.
- [FBI Warns of ARTEX, an AI-Powered Tool for Autonomous Cyberattacks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/fbi-warns-of-artex-ai-powered-autonomous-hacking-tool/) (high)
  The FBI has issued a FLASH advisory about ARTEX, an open-source, AI-driven penetration testing framework being weaponized by threat actors. The tool automates complex cyberattacks by translating natural language goals into machine-executable actions. It has been used in attacks against finance, government, and healthcare sectors globally, with some intrusions achieving privileged access in under three hours. The advisory signals a new era of autonomous, machine-speed attacks, lowering the barrier for less-skilled actors and accelerating campaigns.
- [VMware Patches Critical 9.8-Rated vCenter Auth Bypass Flaw](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/vmware-vcenter-hit-by-critical-authentication-bypass-flaw-cve-2026-123903/) (critical)
  Broadcom has patched a critical authentication bypass vulnerability (CVE-2026-123903) in VMware vCenter Server, rated 9.8 on the CVSS scale. The flaw in the VMware Directory Service (vmdir) could allow an unauthenticated attacker with network access to gain unauthorized control. This follows the recent exploitation of a similar zero-day (CVE-2026-123890) by the Armory Castle threat actor, highlighting the urgent need for patching.
- [Ransomware Attacks Shatter Records in Q3 2026](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ransomware-attacks-hit-record-high-in-q3-2026-manufacturing-sector-most-targeted/) (high)
  Ransomware attacks surged to an all-time high in the third quarter of 2026, with security firms Check Point and Dragos reporting unprecedented numbers. Check Point recorded 2,830 victims on leak sites, while Dragos noted an 11% increase in attacks on industrial organizations. The manufacturing sector was the most heavily impacted, accounting for 69% of industrial attacks. The threat landscape has fragmented, with the Qilin and The Gentlemen groups leading the activity.
- [CISA Adds Actively Exploited Drupal, Optimizely, and AntiCsrf Flaws to KEV](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-adds-drupal-optimizely-idunno-vulnerabilities-to-kev-catalog/) (critical)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added three vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, citing evidence of active exploitation. The flaws include a remote code execution vulnerability in Drupal Core (CVE-2026-132165), a critical deserialization flaw in Idunno.AntiCsrf (CVE-2026-131914), and a state control issue in Optimizely Web Experimentation (CVE-2026-132224). Federal agencies are now under a strict deadline to remediate these high-risk vulnerabilities.
- [AI-Assisted Campaign by GreyNoise13 Hits 22 Orgs via Check Point Flaws](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ai-assisted-hacking-campaign-compromises-check-point-servers/) (high)
  A threat actor tracked as GreyNoise13 (GT13) has compromised at least 22 organizations globally in a sophisticated, AI-assisted hacking campaign targeting Check Point Security Management servers since mid-October 2026. The attackers are leveraging a chain of critical vulnerabilities for initial access, including flaws in Check Point, Citrix, and Ivanti products, impacting critical sectors like energy, finance, and government across eight countries.
- [Massive Data Leak Exposes Two Billion Credentials from Infostealer Ops](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/two-billion-credentials-exposed-in-massive-infostealer-leak/) (high)
  A cybersecurity researcher has discovered a massive, non-password-protected database on the public internet containing approximately two billion credentials. The discovery, made by Jeremiah Fowler and reported by ExpressVPN, is believed to be an aggregation of data harvested from widespread infostealer malware campaigns. The exposed data includes a vast collection of usernames and passwords in plaintext, posing a severe risk of account takeovers and credential stuffing attacks.
- [Two Critical GeoServer Flaws Actively Exploited for RCE](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/geoserver-vulnerabilities-actively-exploited-for-rce/) (critical)
  Threat actors are actively exploiting two critical vulnerabilities in the open-source GeoServer platform (CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129243), chaining them together to achieve unauthenticated remote code execution. The exploitation was observed in the wild by security firm watchTowr just one day after patches were released, prompting urgent warnings for administrators to update their instances or restrict access immediately.
- [CISA Adds 10 More Exploited Flaws to KEV from Danfoss, HPE, Mitel, Vates](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-adds-ten-more-vulnerabilities-to-kev-catalog-danfoss-hpe-mitel-vates/) (high)
  On November 25, 2026, CISA added ten more vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, signaling active exploitation in the wild. The newly added flaws impact a diverse range of products from vendors including Danfoss, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), Mitel, and Vates. Federal agencies are mandated under Binding Operational Directive (BOD) 26-04 to prioritize patching these high-risk vulnerabilities.
- [NGINX HTTP/2 Use-After-Free Flaw (CVE-2026-123633) Allows DoS, RCE](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/nginx-http2-use-after-free-vulnerability-cve-2026-123633/) (critical)
  A critical use-after-free vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-123633, has been discovered in the NGINX HTTP/2 implementation. The flaw can be triggered by an unauthenticated attacker sending a specially crafted, fragmented HTTP/2 request to a vulnerable server configured as a reverse proxy. Successful exploitation can cause a denial of service (DoS) by crashing the worker process. Researchers have also demonstrated that remote code execution (RCE) is possible by chaining the vulnerability with a separate memory disclosure bug, particularly when Address Space Layout Randomization (ASLR) is disabled or bypassed. NGINX has released patches, and administrators are urged to upgrade affected systems immediately.
