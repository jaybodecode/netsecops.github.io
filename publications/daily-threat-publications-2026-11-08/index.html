Microsoft Patches Paint RCE Flaw via Microsoft Store Update

HIGH

Windows 11 Paint RCE Flaw CVE-2026-83844 Patched

Microsoft has patched an important-rated remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability, CVE-2026-83844, in the Windows 11 Paint application. The flaw, an out-of-bounds read with a CVSS score of 8.1, could allow an unauthenticated attacker to execute code over a network without user interaction. Unusually, the patch is delivered via a Microsoft Store update, not through the standard Windows Update process. Users must manually update the Paint app to version 11.2609.401.0 or later to be protected.

U.S. Agencies Issue Urgent Warning on Industrial Refrigeration Cyberattacks

HIGH

CISA, FBI Warn of Attacks on Industrial Refrigeration Systems

Update:

A coordinated wave of cyberattacks on November 2nd and 3rd, 2026, has been confirmed across Europe, impacting supermarkets and a French blood bank. Threat actors exploited Danfoss controllers, manipulating temperatures to 50°C, causing significant spoilage. Research by Lupovis indicates a two-week reconnaissance period (Oct 20-30) by 91 distinct threat actors preceded the attacks, suggesting a planned operation. Health-ISAC and FBI have issued warnings, and Unit 42 found over 5,000 similar exposed devices globally.

CISA Adds Actively Exploited SonicWall MFA Bypass Flaw to KEV Catalog

CRITICAL

CISA Warns of Exploited SonicWall MFA Bypass Flaw

Update:

SonicWall has issued emergency patches for the actively exploited CVE-2026-19868 authentication bypass vulnerability. The update specifies affected SonicOS versions, including Gen 7 (7.3.3-7015 and older) and Gen 8 (8.2.2-8015 and older) devices. Organizations are urged to upgrade to fixed versions: Gen 7 (7.3.4-7013 or newer) and Gen 8 (8.2.3-8015 or newer) immediately to mitigate the risk of exploitation, which bypasses SAML and MFA.
