# Maersk Breached by Ransomware, Microsoft Issues Record Patch for Outlook Zero-Day

**Published:** 2026-11-12 | **Articles:** 9

In the last 48 hours, the cybersecurity landscape has been dominated by significant breaches and critical vulnerabilities. Shipping giant Maersk confirmed a data breach by the WhiteArmor ransomware group, who are demanding a $5 million ransom. Microsoft released its largest-ever Patch Tuesday, fixing 1,235 flaws, including an actively exploited Outlook zero-day. Concurrently, a massive supply chain attack on GitHub is compromising thousands of repositories, and numerous Philippine government agencies are investigating extensive data breaches. These events highlight persistent threats from ransomware, supply chain attacks, and unpatched zero-day vulnerabilities.

## Articles in this publication
- ['Black October' Attacks Steal API Keys from Nine AI Dev Platforms](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/black-october-attacks-hit-nine-ai-developer-platforms/) (high)
  A coordinated wave of attacks dubbed "Black October" has compromised at least nine AI developer platforms, including Northflank, Railway, and Lightning AI. Security researchers at Wiz, who identified the campaign, report that attackers targeted environment variables to steal API keys and other secrets. This technique, termed "LLMJacking," allows threat actors to hijack expensive AI model services for their own use, leading to significant fraudulent charges for victims. Northflank confirmed the breach after being alerted by Wiz, noting that multiple customers had reported unauthorized use of their AI API keys. The campaign highlights a critical credential crisis in AI infrastructure, with some attacks employing "agent swarm" tactics and resulting in at least one platform, Zeabur, shutting down permanently.
- [Email Service Breach Leaks 77 Million Addresses, Sparks Phishing Wave](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/arara-message-service-breach-leaks-77-million-emails-impacts-380-companies/) (high)
  Paycloud Holdings, parent company of the Arara Message email distribution service in Japan, has disclosed a data breach exposing approximately 77 million email addresses. Information from 380 corporate clients was also compromised, though no credit card data was lost. Following the breach, attackers launched a large-scale phishing campaign, sending over 7 million emails impersonating a credit card company to the leaked addresses. Users are warned to be vigilant against suspicious communications.
- [Ten Philippine Government Agencies Report Data Breaches](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ten-philippine-government-agencies-report-data-breaches/) (high)
  The Philippines' National Privacy Commission (NPC) has confirmed that ten government agencies, including the Department of Justice and the Department of Information and Communications Technology, have filed data breach notifications. The NPC is now assessing the scope and nature of the incidents, which raise serious concerns about the security of sensitive government-held data. The full extent of the breaches and the number of affected individuals have not yet been disclosed.
- [Microsoft Patches Actively Exploited Outlook Zero-Day in Massive Update](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/microsoft-patches-outlook-zero-day-cve-2026-124194-in-november-update/) (high)
  Microsoft's November 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed over 1,200 vulnerabilities, including a critical zero-day in Outlook, CVE-2026-124194, which is being actively exploited. The flaw allows attackers to steal NTLM authentication hashes by tricking users into opening a malicious message file. The vulnerability does not trigger in the Preview Pane, requiring direct user interaction. In response, CISA has added CVE-2026-124194 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, mandating federal agencies to patch by November 24, 2026.
- [Vanderbilt University Hit by BYOD Ransomware; Attackers Claim Massive Data Theft](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/vanderbilt-university-hit-by-byod-ransomware-data-theft-claimed/) (high)
  Vanderbilt University is recovering from a major ransomware attack claimed by the 'BYOD' group, which began on November 9, 2026. The incident caused widespread system disruptions, forcing the university to shut down devices and network access. The BYOD gang alleges it exfiltrated a massive trove of data, including over one million Social Security numbers, student records, and research data. While Vanderbilt has begun restoring services, it has not confirmed the data theft claims. The attack follows a pattern of the BYOD group targeting higher education and healthcare.
- [Maersk Confirms Breach; WhiteArmor Ransomware Demands $5M Ransom](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/maersk-confirms-breach-whitearmor-ransomware-demands-5-million/) (high)
  Global shipping firm Maersk has confirmed a data breach affecting a legacy IT system from a previous acquisition. The WhiteArmor ransomware group claims to have stolen 100GB of sensitive logistics data, including plaintext passwords and customer records, and is demanding a $5 million ransom. Maersk states the incident is isolated and does not anticipate disruption to customer operations, though the attackers have published detailed lists of allegedly stolen data to apply pressure.
- [Massive GitHub Supply Chain Attack Injects 'wfx' Stealer into Repos](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/github-supply-chain-attack-wfx-stealer-targets-ci-cd-secrets/) (critical)
  A large-scale software supply chain attack is actively targeting the GitHub ecosystem. Attackers are using compromised developer accounts to inject a sophisticated information stealer, dubbed 'wfx', into tens of thousands of code repositories. The malware is designed to run within GitHub Actions to exfiltrate sensitive CI/CD secrets, including repository secrets, cloud credentials, and API keys, posing a severe threat to software development and deployment pipelines.
- [Microsoft Reveals 'Lazarus,' a Stealthy AI Supply Chain Attack](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/microsoft-unveils-lazarus-stealthy-ai-attack-backdoors-models/) (high)
  Microsoft researchers have disclosed 'Lazarus,' a novel and highly stealthy AI supply-chain attack technique. The method allows an attacker to surgically backdoor open-weight large language models (LLMs) by modifying fewer than ten dormant neurons. This nearly undetectable edit, which requires no model retraining, can be activated by a secret trigger in a prompt, forcing the AI agent to execute arbitrary commands using its assigned privileges. The research highlights a critical new threat vector for the rapidly growing field of agentic AI.
- [Falcon C2 Framework Compromises Browsers to Target Governments](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/the-xg-files-falcon-c2-flying-under-the-browser-radar/) (high)
  Unit 42 has identified a browser-based command and control (C2) framework named Falcon (also known as XG or Xiàng Gē). The threat actor leverages this framework to compromise government webmail portals and applications by injecting malicious JavaScript. Operating entirely within the browser, Falcon can sideload malicious Chrome extensions and exfiltrate sensitive information. The campaign, observed between March and May 2026, targeted government ministries in the Middle East and Southeast Asia. The framework also serves as a delivery mechanism for a secondary implant called Antino, which uses Microsoft OneDrive for C2 communications. Analysis reveals a high-confidence link between Falcon and the PeckBirdy framework previously reported by Trend Micro.
