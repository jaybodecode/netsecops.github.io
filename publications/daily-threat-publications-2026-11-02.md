# Active Exploits, Data Leaks, and Critical Flaws Dominate Cybersecurity News

**Published:** 2026-11-02 | **Articles:** 7

**Critical Vulnerabilities and Active Exploitation:**

*   **[UPDATE] CISA Warns of Actively Exploited Ivanti Connect Secure Flaws:** CISA has updated its warning regarding Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day exploitation, identifying threat actor UTA0576 and specific affected and patched versions. The attack chain leverages CVE-2026-104935 for authentication bypass, leading to credential theft and reconnaissance. Notably, the attack evades Ivanti's Integrity Checker Tool, requiring deeper forensic analysis.
*   **[NEW] Critical RCE Flaw in Linux Kernel's TCP Module Affects Servers:** A critical remote code execution vulnerability (CVE-2026-98432) has been found in the Linux kernel's TCP networking memory management. With a CVSS score of 9.8, it allows unauthenticated remote attackers to gain root privileges, particularly impacting systems with exposed Multipath TCP listeners.
*   **[NEW] Android 'SMS of Death' Flaw Allows Zero-Click Device Crash:** A proof-of-concept exploit is available for CVE-2026-58882, an Android vulnerability allowing remote attackers to crash devices or disrupt cellular service via a crafted, invisible text message. This zero-click attack affects Android 14-17 and, while patched by Google in October 2026, poses a risk to unpatched devices.

**Data Breaches and Emerging Threats:**

*   **[NEW] Over 41,000 DHS Employee and Contractor Records Leaked Online:** Distributed Denial of Secrets has published personal and operational data for over 41,000 U.S. Department of Homeland Security employees and contractors. The leak includes sensitive information about surveillance programs and undercover operations, impacting agencies like ICE, CBP, and CISA.
*   **[NEW] Email Service Breach Leaks 77 Million Addresses, Sparks Phishing Wave:** Paycloud Holdings, operator of Japan's Arara Message email service, disclosed a breach exposing approximately 77 million email addresses and corporate client data. This has led to a large-scale phishing campaign impersonating a credit card company.
*   **[NEW] Ransomware Targets Synology NAS Devices Running Obsolete Software:** A ransomware campaign is actively targeting Synology NAS devices running end-of-life DiskStation Manager (DSM) versions that no longer receive security updates. Attacks exploit vulnerabilities patched in 2024, urging users to upgrade or replace legacy hardware.

**Industry Initiatives and Awareness:**

*   **[NEW] CISA Launches 2026 Critical Infrastructure Security & Resilience Month:** CISA has initiated its annual Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month for November 2026. The campaign focuses on the human element of security and preparing for evolving threats, including AI, urging partners to identify critical systems and maintain response plans.

## Articles in this publication
- [CISA Warns of Actively Exploited Ivanti Connect Secure Flaws](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-adds-actively-exploited-ivanti-connect-secure-flaws-to-kev/) (critical)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added two vulnerabilities in Ivanti Connect Secure (CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. Citing evidence of active exploitation, CISA warns the flaws can be chained by an authenticated attacker to escalate privileges and upload malicious files. Federal agencies are mandated to remediate the vulnerabilities to defend against threats targeting network perimeter devices.
- [Over 41,000 DHS Employee and Contractor Records Leaked Online](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/data-of-over-41000-dhs-employees-leaked-by-activist-group/) (high)
  The activist group Distributed Denial of Secrets has published the personal and operational data of over 41,000 U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) employees and contractors. The leak includes names, phone numbers, and email addresses, as well as sensitive details about surveillance programs and undercover operations. The data affects personnel from agencies like ICE, CBP, and CISA, posing a significant risk to personal safety and national security.
- [Email Service Breach Leaks 77 Million Addresses, Sparks Phishing Wave](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/arara-message-service-breach-leaks-77-million-emails-impacts-380-companies/) (high)
  Paycloud Holdings, parent company of the Arara Message email distribution service in Japan, has disclosed a data breach exposing approximately 77 million email addresses. Information from 380 corporate clients was also compromised, though no credit card data was lost. Following the breach, attackers launched a large-scale phishing campaign, sending over 7 million emails impersonating a credit card company to the leaked addresses. Users are warned to be vigilant against suspicious communications.
- [Critical RCE Flaw in Linux Kernel's TCP Module Affects Servers](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/critical-rce-flaw-cve-2026-98432-disclosed-in-linux-kernels-tcp-module/) (critical)
  A critical remote code execution vulnerability, CVE-2026-98432, has been discovered in the Linux kernel's memory management subsystem for TCP networking. The flaw carries a CVSS score of 9.8 and can be exploited by a remote, unauthenticated attacker to gain root privileges. Systems with exposed Multipath TCP (MPTCP) listeners are specifically at risk. While there are no reports of active exploitation, the vulnerability's severity and the widespread use of Linux demand immediate attention from administrators.
- [Android 'SMS of Death' Flaw Allows Zero-Click Remote Device Crash](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/android-sms-of-death-flaw-cve-2026-58882-allows-remote-zero-click-dos/) (medium)
  A proof-of-concept exploit has been released for CVE-2026-58882, an Android vulnerability dubbed "SMS of Death." The flaw allows a remote attacker to crash a target device or disrupt its cellular service using a specially crafted, invisible text message. This zero-click denial-of-service attack requires no user interaction and affects Android versions 14 through 17. Google patched the flaw in its October 2026 security updates, but unpatched devices remain vulnerable, with the PoC release increasing the risk of exploitation.
- [Ransomware Targets Synology NAS Devices Running Obsolete Software](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ransomware-wave-hits-synology-nas-devices-running-end-of-life-software/) (high)
  The Hong Kong CERT has issued an alert about a ransomware campaign actively targeting Synology Network-Attached Storage (NAS) devices. The attacks succeed by exploiting a vulnerability that was patched in 2024. Threat actors are specifically targeting devices running unsupported, end-of-life (EOL) versions of DiskStation Manager (DSM), which no longer receive security updates. Users are urged to upgrade to a supported DSM version or replace legacy hardware to avoid data encryption and ransom demands.
- [CISA Launches 2026 Critical Infrastructure Security & Resilience Month](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-launches-2026-critical-infrastructure-security-and-resilience-month/) (informational)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has launched its annual Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month for November 2026. The initiative aims to raise awareness and promote best practices for securing the nation's essential systems. This year's focus is on the human element of security and preparing for evolving threats, including those from artificial intelligence. CISA is urging public and private sector partners to focus on identifying critical systems, understanding risks, and maintaining actionable response plans.
