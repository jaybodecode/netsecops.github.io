Today New Articles

Google Patches Critical Chrome Flaw Allowing Sandbox Escape

Google has released an emergency security update for a critical vulnerability, CVE-2026-134401, affecting its Chrome web browser. The flaw, rated 9.6, is in the Variations component and could allow a remote attacker to execute arbitrary code, bypassing the browser's sandbox. The update is available for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android. While no active exploitation has been confirmed, users are urged to update immediately.

VMware Patches Critical 9.8-Rated vCenter Auth Bypass Flaw

Broadcom has patched a critical authentication bypass vulnerability (CVE-2026-123903) in VMware vCenter Server, rated 9.8 on the CVSS scale. The flaw in the VMware Directory Service (vmdir) could allow an unauthenticated attacker with network access to gain unauthorized control. This follows the recent exploitation of a similar zero-day (CVE-2026-123890) by the Armory Castle threat actor, highlighting the urgent need for patching.

Elecbrakes Flaws Allow Remote Hijacking of Trailer Brakes

Researchers have found three critical vulnerabilities in Elecbrakes trailer brake controllers (EB2, VM1, ELBC 2000). The flaws, primarily in the Bluetooth communication protocol, could allow a nearby attacker to remotely engage or disengage a trailer's brakes, creating a risk of catastrophic 'jackknife' accidents. The issues stem from missing authentication and protection against replay attacks.

European Commission Confirms Personal Data Stolen in Cyberattack

The European Commission has confirmed that a cyberattack first detected in October led to the theft of personal data. The breach targeted the EU Academy, the Commission's online learning platform. The attack, which reportedly involved AI tools, also caused wider disruptions. The EU Academy platform has been taken offline and will not be restored until January 2027 to allow for security upgrades.

Australia and Singapore Partner on AI and Cybersecurity Governance

Australia and Singapore have announced a formal agreement to strengthen cooperation on artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity governance. The partnership aims to protect critical digital infrastructure, promote international standards for responsible AI, and ensure advanced AI systems remain under human control. The collaboration will include knowledge sharing on AI model evaluation and joint risk mitigation efforts.

N-able Confirms Server Breach Caused Email Security Service Outage

Update:CISA has added CVE-2026-134624, a critical 9.3 CVSS vulnerability in N-able's Mail Assure and SpamExperts, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. This flaw was actively exploited by threat actors, leading to the previously reported server breach and service disruptions. CISA has issued a directive requiring all U.S. federal agencies to patch this vulnerability by Thanksgiving. N-able has confirmed it has deployed a fix for the exploited flaw and is continuing its investigation with an incident response firm. This update confirms the root cause of the earlier reported outage and highlights the critical nature of the vulnerability.

AI Agents Autonomously Find and Exploit Zero-Day Vulnerability

Update:The critical path traversal vulnerability, CVE-2026-133968, which was autonomously exploited by AI agents in MONAI Label, has now been added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. This inclusion signifies that the vulnerability is actively being exploited in the wild and poses a significant risk, requiring federal agencies to remediate it within a specified timeframe. This update underscores the heightened urgency for all organizations utilizing MONAI Label to apply available patches immediately to mitigate the threat of sophisticated AI-driven exploitation.

Industrial Ransomware Attacks Surge 11% in Q3 2026, Dragos Reports

Update:New reports confirm Q3 2026 as a record quarter for ransomware, with Check Point recording 2,830 victims on leak sites, the highest ever. This broader view complements Dragos' findings of an 11% surge in industrial attacks. The threat landscape is increasingly fragmented, with 117 active ransomware brands and smaller groups now responsible for the majority of incidents. Qilin and The Gentlemen continue to be leading actors, while the US remains the most targeted nation. This indicates a significant escalation in the overall ransomware threat.
