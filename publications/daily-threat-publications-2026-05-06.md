# Palo Alto Networks Zero-Day Under Active Attack; DAEMON Tools Hit by Major Supply Chain Compromise

**Published:** 2026-05-06 | **Articles:** 10

A critical zero-day vulnerability (CVE-2026-0300) in Palo Alto Networks' PAN-OS is being actively exploited for remote code execution, forcing urgent mitigation as patches are pending. Concurrently, a sophisticated supply chain attack has trojanized official DAEMON Tools installers, deploying backdoors and advanced RATs against targeted government and scientific entities. This period also saw disclosures of new malware campaigns from Chinese and North Korean APTs, a critical RCE flaw in Apache HTTP Server, and a large-scale phishing attack detailed by Microsoft, highlighting a landscape dominated by high-impact vulnerabilities and state-sponsored threats.

## Articles in this publication
- [Anthropic's 'Mythos' AI Model Triggers Global Cybersecurity Overhaul](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/anthropics-mythos-ai-model-forces-global-cybersecurity-rethink/) (informational)
  The announcement of 'Claude Mythos', a frontier AI model from Anthropic, is causing a seismic shift in global cybersecurity strategy. Anthropic revealed that the model, which will not be publicly released due to its potential for misuse, is capable of autonomously discovering and exploiting unknown zero-day vulnerabilities in major software. The UK's AI Safety Institute independently confirmed these capabilities, calling it a 'watershed moment'. In response, governments and major corporations are scrambling to reassess their security postures against this new class of AI-driven threat. India's finance ministry and China's cybersecurity industry are already reacting, while Anthropic has launched 'Project Glasswing' to give select partners like Apple and Goldman Sachs controlled access to test their defenses.
- [China-Based Silver Fox APT Expands Espionage Campaign Across Asia with Fake Tax Audits](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/china-based-silver-fox-apt-expands-espionage-in-asia/) (high)
  The China-based threat group Silver Fox has launched a new wave of attacks targeting businesses and individuals across Asia. The group, which has been active since at least 2022, uses fake tax audit notifications and counterfeit software update alerts to deliver malware. According to analysis by S2W, Silver Fox has evolved from financially motivated attacks within China to a dual-purpose operation involving both espionage and profit-driven campaigns. The group's focus has expanded from Taiwan and Japan to include targets in Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, and the Philippines, with a recent focus on medical institutions and financial companies.
- [New 'CloudZ' Malware Abuses Microsoft Phone Link to Steal Mobile Data](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cloudz-malware-abuses-microsoft-phone-link-to-steal-mobile-data/) (high)
  A novel malware campaign is leveraging a remote access trojan (RAT) named CloudZ and a custom plugin, Pheno, to abuse the Microsoft Phone Link feature in Windows. This attack, identified by Cisco Talos, allows threat actors to steal sensitive data from a user's smartphone, including SMS messages and one-time passwords (OTPs), by accessing the Phone Link database on a compromised PC. This innovative technique bypasses mobile device security entirely, shifting the attack vector to enterprise-managed Windows systems and undermining the security of SMS-based multi-factor authentication.
- [Microsoft Details Phishing Campaign Targeting 35,000 Users](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/microsoft-details-large-scale-phishing-campaign-targeting-35000-users/) (high)
  Microsoft has detailed a large-scale and sophisticated phishing campaign that targeted over 35,000 users in 26 countries between April 14 and 16, 2026. The attack used polished 'code of conduct' lures and an adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) phishing kit to steal session tokens and bypass multi-factor authentication. The campaign primarily targeted organizations in the United States, with the healthcare, financial services, and professional services sectors being the most affected. The report also highlights a 146% surge in QR code phishing attacks in Q1 2026.
- [Critical Palo Alto Networks Zero-Day (CVE-2026-0300) Actively Exploited for RCE](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/palo-alto-networks-pan-os-zero-day-cve-2026-0300-under-active-exploitation/) (critical)
  Palo Alto Networks has issued an urgent advisory for a critical, unpatched zero-day vulnerability, CVE-2026-0300, in its PAN-OS software. The flaw, a buffer overflow in the User-ID Authentication Portal, allows for unauthenticated remote code execution with root privileges. The company has confirmed limited but active exploitation in the wild, targeting internet-exposed firewalls. With a CVSS score of 9.3, the vulnerability affects multiple PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls. Patches are not yet available, with the first wave expected around May 13, 2026. Palo Alto Networks and global cybersecurity agencies are urging immediate implementation of mitigation measures, which involve restricting access to the vulnerable portal.
- [China-Nexus APT 'UAT-8302' Uses Shared Malware Toolkit to Target Governments](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/china-linked-apt-uat-8302-targets-governments-with-shared-malware/) (high)
  Cisco Talos has identified a sophisticated China-linked APT group, dubbed UAT-8302, that has been conducting espionage campaigns against government entities in South America and Southeastern Europe since late 2024. The group's primary objective is intelligence gathering through persistent access. A key finding is UAT-8302's use of a diverse malware toolkit, including NetDraft and an updated version of CloudSorcerer, which is shared among several other China-nexus threat actors like LongNosedGoblin and Earth Estries. This shared infrastructure suggests a high degree of collaboration within China's state-sponsored cyberespionage ecosystem, complicating attribution.
- [Critical RCE Flaw in Apache HTTP Server's HTTP/2 Module Patched](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/apache-http-server-critical-rce-flaw-cve-2026-23918-in-http2-module/) (high)
  The Apache Software Foundation has patched a critical vulnerability, CVE-2026-23918, in the Apache HTTP Server. The flaw is a 'double free' memory corruption bug in the `mod_http2` module affecting version 2.4.66. With a CVSS score of 8.8, the vulnerability can be trivially triggered by a remote, unauthenticated attacker to cause a denial-of-service (DoS) by crashing a worker process. More significantly, security researchers have developed a working proof-of-concept (PoC) demonstrating that the flaw can be exploited for remote code execution (RCE). Users are urgently advised to upgrade to the patched version, 2.4.67.
- [North Korean APT ScarCruft Hits Gaming Platform in Supply-Chain Attack](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/north-korean-apt-scarcruft-targets-gamers-in-supply-chain-attack/) (high)
  The North Korea-aligned APT group ScarCruft (APT37) has executed a multi-platform supply-chain attack by compromising a regional gaming platform, `sqgame[.]net`. Active since late 2024, the campaign targets the ethnic Korean community in China's Yanbian region. The attackers distributed trojanized Windows and Android game installers embedded with a new Android version of the 'BirdCall' backdoor. This powerful spyware harvests contacts, call logs, messages, and can record ambient audio, exfiltrating the data via legitimate cloud services like pCloud and Yandex Disk. The campaign's goal is likely surveillance of individuals of interest to the North Korean regime.
- [High-Severity Redis Flaw (CVE-2026-25243) Allows for Potential RCE](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/redis-restore-command-vulnerability-cve-2026-25243-may-lead-to-rce/) (high)
  A high-severity vulnerability, CVE-2026-25243, has been discovered in Redis, a popular in-memory data store. The flaw is a heap-based buffer overflow within the `RESTORE` command, which fails to properly validate serialized data. An authenticated attacker with permission to use the `RESTORE` command can supply a crafted payload to trigger invalid memory access, leading to a server crash or potential remote code execution. The vulnerability, rated CVSS 7.7, affects all Redis server versions up to 8.6.3. Users are advised to upgrade immediately or restrict access to the `RESTORE` command using ACLs.
- [89% of IT Leaders Struggle with Identity Sprawl Amid AI Expansion: Report](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/keeper-security-report-highlights-identity-sprawl-and-ai-risks/) (informational)
  A new global report from Keeper Security, "Identity Security at Machine Speed," reveals that 89% of IT leaders find it difficult to manage their organization's expanding identity footprint. The rapid adoption of AI is a key driver, multiplying the number of non-human identities (NHIs) that require governance. The survey of 3,200 IT decision-makers highlights major security gaps, with 96% citing disconnected tools as a problem and 72% admitting that credential misuse is not detected in real-time. The report also points to the risks of "shadow AI" and employees inadvertently exposing data to AI systems.
