# VECT 2.0 Ransomware Flaw Wipes Data; DAEMON Tools Hit by Supply Chain Attack; Critical Linux Flaw Grants Root Access

**Published:** 2026-05-05 | **Articles:** 10

This edition covers a critical flaw in the VECT 2.0 ransomware that turns it into a data wiper, a month-long supply chain attack compromising DAEMON Tools installers, and the discovery of 'CloudZ' malware abusing Microsoft Phone Link. Additionally, we report on the active exploitation of critical vulnerabilities in MetInfo CMS, the Linux kernel ('Copy Fail'), and cPanel, alongside data breaches at Trellix and Vimeo, and a large-scale phishing campaign detailed by Microsoft. The period of May 4-5, 2026, has been marked by severe supply chain compromises and the weaponization of newly disclosed vulnerabilities.

## Articles in this publication
- [CISA Warns of 'ShadowProxy' Phishing-as-a-Service that Bypasses MFA](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-warns-of-shadowproxy-phishing-as-a-service-platform/) (high)
  The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) and the FBI have issued a joint advisory about the growing threat from 'ShadowProxy,' a sophisticated Phishing-as-a-Service (PhaaS) platform. Sold on dark web forums, ShadowProxy enables even low-skilled criminals to launch large-scale phishing campaigns capable of defeating multi-factor authentication (MFA). The platform uses an adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) technique, acting as a reverse proxy to intercept credentials and, crucially, the session cookie generated after a successful MFA login. This allows attackers to hijack active sessions and gain full access to accounts like Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.
- [Instructure Confirms Massive Breach; ShinyHunters Claims 275 Million User Records from Canvas LMS](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/instructure-canvas-breach-shinyhunters-claims-275-million-records/) (high)
  Educational technology firm Instructure has confirmed a significant data breach affecting its widely used Canvas Learning Management System (LMS). The incident, which caused service disruptions starting May 1, 2026, has been claimed by the notorious extortion group ShinyHunters. The threat actor alleges the theft of 3.65 terabytes of data, including the personal information and private messages of 275 million students and educators from nearly 9,000 institutions globally. Instructure has acknowledged the exposure of names, email addresses, and user messages but stated that more sensitive data like passwords and financial information was not compromised. The company is working with law enforcement and has implemented remedial actions, including rotating API keys.
- [Critical cPanel Zero-Day (CVE-2026-41940) Actively Exploited, Over 40,000 Servers Compromised](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/critical-cpanel-zero-day-cve-2026-41940-actively-exploited/) (critical)
  A critical authentication bypass vulnerability in cPanel & WebHost Manager (WHM), tracked as CVE-2026-41940, is under active and widespread exploitation. The zero-day flaw, with a CVSS score of 9.8, allows unauthenticated attackers to gain complete administrative control over servers. Threat intelligence from The Shadowserver Foundation indicates that over 40,000 unique IP addresses associated with compromised servers have been observed. The vulnerability, added to CISA's KEV catalog, is being used to deploy 'Sorry' ransomware. Attackers are exploiting the flaw by injecting malicious parameters into session files. All users of cPanel & WHM are urged to patch immediately to mitigate this severe threat.
- [Cybersecurity Vendor Trellix Confirms Breach of Source Code Repository](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cybersecurity-firm-trellix-confirms-source-code-repository-breach/) (high)
  Cybersecurity vendor Trellix, formed from the merger of McAfee Enterprise and FireEye, has confirmed a security breach involving unauthorized access to a portion of its internal source code repository. The company stated it has engaged forensic experts and notified law enforcement. While the investigation is ongoing, Trellix has found no evidence that its source code release process was compromised or that the code has been altered or exploited. This incident places Trellix in a growing list of security companies, including Okta and LastPass, that have suffered source code breaches, highlighting the significant threat of supply chain attacks targeting the security industry itself.
- [Vimeo Data Exposed in Supply-Chain Attack on Vendor Anodot; ShinyHunters Implicated](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/vimeo-user-data-exposed-in-breach-at-third-party-vendor-anodot/) (high)
  Video hosting platform Vimeo has confirmed a data breach that exposed user and customer data as a result of a supply-chain attack on its analytics vendor, Anodot. The attackers reportedly stole authentication tokens from Anodot and used them to access customer environments, including Vimeo's Snowflake and BigQuery instances. The exposed Vimeo data includes technical information, video titles, metadata, and some customer email addresses. Vimeo has stressed that no video content, login credentials, or payment information was compromised. The notorious extortion group ShinyHunters has been linked to the broader campaign, also listing Rockstar Games as a victim of the Anodot compromise.
- [DAEMON Tools Installers Compromised in Ongoing Supply-Chain Attack](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/daemon-tools-installers-compromised-in-ongoing-supply-chain-attack/) (critical)
  An ongoing supply chain attack, active since April 8, 2026, has been compromising the official installers for DAEMON Tools, a popular disk emulation utility. Researchers at Kaspersky discovered that installers downloaded from the company's legitimate website were bundled with malware. These trojanized files, including versions 12.5.0.2421 to 12.5.0.2434, are signed with a valid digital certificate from the developer, AVB Disc Soft, allowing them to evade detection. The malware establishes a backdoor, collects system information, and in targeted cases, deploys an advanced implant called QUIC RAT on machines in Russia, Belarus, and Thailand.
- [New 'CloudZ' Malware Abuses Microsoft Phone Link to Steal Mobile Data](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cloudz-malware-abuses-microsoft-phone-link-to-steal-mobile-data/) (high)
  A novel malware campaign is leveraging a remote access trojan (RAT) named CloudZ and a custom plugin, Pheno, to abuse the Microsoft Phone Link feature in Windows. This attack, identified by Cisco Talos, allows threat actors to steal sensitive data from a user's smartphone, including SMS messages and one-time passwords (OTPs), by accessing the Phone Link database on a compromised PC. This innovative technique bypasses mobile device security entirely, shifting the attack vector to enterprise-managed Windows systems and undermining the security of SMS-based multi-factor authentication.
- [Critical MetInfo CMS Vulnerability Under Active Exploitation](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/attackers-actively-exploit-critical-rce-flaw-in-metinfo-cms/) (critical)
  A critical unauthenticated remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability in the MetInfo Content Management System, tracked as CVE-2026-29014, is being actively exploited in the wild. The flaw, which carries a CVSS score of 9.8, allows attackers to inject and execute arbitrary PHP code by sending crafted requests to a WeChat integration script. Exploitation has surged since May 1, 2026, primarily targeting servers in China and Hong Kong. With exploit code publicly available and around 2,000 MetInfo instances online, there is a high risk of widespread server compromise.
- [SANS and SERC Partner to Bolster Grid Cybersecurity Training](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/sans-and-serc-partner-for-critical-infrastructure-cybersecurity-training/) (informational)
  The SANS Institute and the SERC Reliability Corporation have formed a strategic partnership to deliver advanced cybersecurity training to electric utilities across the United States. This initiative aims to strengthen the reliability of the bulk electric system, improve readiness for NERC CIP compliance, and accelerate workforce development. The partnership will begin with SANS teaching its ICS456: NERC CIP course in August 2026, preparing professionals for the GIAC Critical Infrastructure Protection (GCIP) certification.
- [CISA Launches 'CI Fortify' to Bolster Critical Infrastructure Resilience](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-launches-ci-fortify-to-strengthen-critical-infrastructure-resilience/) (informational)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has launched a new initiative, 'CI Fortify,' to help critical infrastructure (CI) entities enhance their resilience against cyberattacks. The guidance focuses on preparing organizations to maintain operations during a crisis or conflict by developing proactive isolation and recovery capabilities. CISA is urging CI entities to review the guidance and implement its recommendations to ensure the continuity of vital services.
