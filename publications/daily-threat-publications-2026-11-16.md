# Kroll Report: 69% Orgs Breached; CISA Warns of UnrealIRCd Flaw

**Published:** 2026-11-16 | **Articles:** 8

**Critical Vulnerabilities and Active Exploitation:**

*   **[UPDATE] CISA Warns of Actively Exploited Critical Flaw in UnrealIRCd:** CISA has issued a warning regarding CVE-2026-114713, a critical flaw in UnrealIRCd that allows for remote command execution with low complexity and no user interaction. Older versions, including End-of-Life versions like UnrealIRCd 5, are urged to upgrade, with a hot patch now available as an alternative to a full upgrade to version 6.2.7.1.

**New Threats and Advisories:**

*   **[NEW] BYOD Ransomware Group Claims Breach of Michigan State University:** The 'BYOD' ransomware group claims to have exfiltrated 1.03 million records containing PII and passwords from Michigan State University, alleging the university's incident response team failed to maintain communication.
*   **[NEW] 'n0n' Extortion Group Claims Breach of Visa, Mastercard, JPMorgan:** The data extortion group 'n0n' has unverifiedly claimed to have stolen customer and marketing data from Visa, Mastercard, and JPMorgan Chase, including cardholder profiles and booking information, without providing proof.
*   **[NEW] Coles Group Investigates Cyber Incident, No Data Loss Confirmed:** Australian supermarket giant Coles Group is investigating a cybersecurity incident, but its investigation has so far found no evidence of customer, employee, or supplier data compromise or exfiltration.
*   **[NEW] German Drone Maker Quantum Systems Hit by Cyberattack:** German defense company Quantum Systems confirmed a cyberattack in late October 2026, which was contained and poses no risk to customers, though reports suggest a potential malicious software update.

**Industry Trends and Policy Updates:**

*   **[UPDATE] Kroll Report: 69% of Orgs Breached in Past Year, Most via Third Parties:** The Kroll 2026 Data Breach Outlook report indicates 69% of organizations experienced breaches in the past year, with a 45% increase in breaches for the professional services sector. The report emphasizes an 'assume breach' mentality and highlights the systemic risk of supply chain compromises.
*   **[NEW] Google Project Zero Ends 30-Day Grace Period for Disclosures:** Google's Project Zero is eliminating its 30-day grace period for patch adoption, with technical details of vulnerabilities now to be made public 90 days after reporting, regardless of fix availability, with a progressive shortening to 30 days by 2028.
*   **[NEW] Qatar Partners with SignQuantum for Post-Quantum Cybersecurity:** The government of Qatar is collaborating with SignQuantum to explore post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and develop quantum-resistant digital signatures to secure government services against future quantum computing threats.

## Articles in this publication
- [Kroll Report: 69% of Orgs Breached in Past Year, Most via Third Parties](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/kroll-report-finds-69-percent-of-orgs-breached-in-past-year/) (informational)
  Kroll's 2026 Data Breach Outlook report reveals that 69% of organizations experienced at least one data breach in the past year, with 34% suffering multiple incidents. The report highlights the growing threat of supply chain risk, finding that breaches originating from third-party technology providers had the widest impact, affecting 69% of all individuals impacted in Kroll's incident response cases. A notable disparity exists in risk perception, with IT leaders more concerned about vendor breaches than C-suite executives.
- [CISA Warns of Actively Exploited Critical Flaw in UnrealIRCd](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-warns-of-actively-exploited-unrealircd-flaw-cve-2026-114713/) (high)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added a critical command injection vulnerability in the UnrealIRCd server, CVE-2026-114713, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. The flaw allows unprivileged users on linked servers to execute commands with server-level authority. Exploitation was observed in the wild just one day after a patch was released, prompting an urgent call to update.
- [BYOD Ransomware Group Claims Breach of Michigan State University](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/byod-ransomware-group-claims-michigan-state-university-breach/) (high)
  The 'BYOD' ransomware group has claimed responsibility for a major data breach at Michigan State University (MSU), alleging the exfiltration of 1.03 million records containing personal identifiable information (PII) and passwords for current and former students and staff. The group publicly stated that the university's incident response team failed to maintain communication after an initial contact, leading the attackers to leak the data.
- ['n0n' Extortion Group Claims Breach of Visa, Mastercard, JPMorgan](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/extortion-group-n0n-claims-breach-of-visa-mastercard-jpmorgan/) (high)
  The data extortion group 'n0n' has made an unverified claim of stealing a trove of customer and marketing data from financial giants Visa, Mastercard, and JPMorgan Chase. The group, which operates by stealing data and threatening to leak it without deploying ransomware, has not provided proof. The allegedly stolen data includes cardholder profiles and booking information. None of the named companies have confirmed a breach.
- [Coles Group Investigates Cyber Incident, No Data Loss Confirmed](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/coles-group-investigates-cyber-incident-no-data-loss-confirmed/) (medium)
  Australian supermarket giant Coles Group has announced it is investigating a cybersecurity incident. In a statement to the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) on November 16, 2026, the company confirmed its retail and online operations remain unaffected. Crucially, Coles stated that its investigation has so far found no evidence that customer, employee, or supplier data has been compromised or exfiltrated. The company is working with the Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC).
- [Google Project Zero Ends 30-Day Grace Period for Disclosures](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/google-project-zero-ends-30-day-grace-period-for-vulnerability-disclosure/) (informational)
  Google's Project Zero, an elite vulnerability research team, has announced a significant change to its disclosure policy. Effective immediately, the team is eliminating the 30-day grace period previously granted for patch adoption. Under the new "Full X" policy, technical details of a vulnerability will be made public 90 days after reporting to a vendor, regardless of whether a fix is available. The deadline will be progressively shortened to 30 days by 2028 to pressure vendors into faster patching cycles.
- [German Drone Maker Quantum Systems Hit by Cyberattack](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/german-drone-maker-quantum-systems-hit-by-cyberattack/) (high)
  German defense and aerospace company Quantum Systems, a manufacturer of drones, confirmed it was the target of a cyberattack in late October 2026. While the company states the attack was contained and poses no risk to customers, reports suggest the incident may have involved a malicious software update, raising serious supply chain security concerns. German federal authorities, including military counterintelligence, are investigating the attack.
- [Qatar Partners with SignQuantum for Post-Quantum Cybersecurity](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/qatar-partners-with-signquantum-for-post-quantum-cybersecurity/) (informational)
  The government of Qatar is taking proactive steps to prepare for the threat of quantum computing by partnering with the cybersecurity firm SignQuantum. The collaboration, led by Qatar's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT), will focus on exploring applications for post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and developing quantum-resistant digital signatures to secure government services and data against future threats.
