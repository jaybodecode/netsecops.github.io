# UnrealIRCd Flaw, AI Supply Chain Attack, and PostgreSQL RCE Highlight Cybersecurity Risks

**Published:** 2026-11-15 | **Articles:** 7

**Critical Vulnerabilities and Exploitation:**

*   **[UPDATE] CISA Warns of Actively Exploited Critical Flaw in UnrealIRCd:** CISA has updated guidance for detecting and mitigating CVE-2026-114713, an Improper Neutralization of Delimiters in UnrealIRCd. New hunting hints focus on monitoring the `unrealircd` process for unusual child processes, outbound connections, and suspicious command strings, with detection emphasizing EDR process monitoring and remediation recommending low-privilege user execution and network segmentation.
*   **[NEW] PostgreSQL Patches 52 Flaws, Including 22-Year-Old RCE Bug:** The PostgreSQL project has released a minor version update addressing 52 vulnerabilities, including a critical 22-year-old remote code execution (RCE) flaw. While requiring authentication, this vulnerability poses a significant risk in environments with compromised credentials, underscoring the importance of diligent patch management for mature open-source projects.

**Supply Chain and Data Breach Incidents:**

*   **[NEW] Stolen AI Key at Fireworks AI Triggers Supply Chain Attack on Cursor:** A supply chain attack originated with a stolen API key from Fireworks AI, leading to a breach of developer tool company Cursor. Attackers injected a trojanized VS Code extension into Cursor's marketplace, which was downloaded by approximately 5,000 developers and installed a persistent SSH backdoor.
*   **[NEW] ConnectWise Probes ScreenConnect Partner Account Compromise:** ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its partners' ScreenConnect accounts, treating it as a supply chain attack. Threat actors have used this access to deploy unauthorized remote-access software into downstream customer environments, potentially granting administrative access to client networks.
*   **[NEW] Momentum Telecom Breach Claim Alleges 7.5M User Records for Sale:** A threat actor named 'Torax2020' has claimed a data breach at Momentum Telecom, a cloud voice services provider, and is offering over 7.5 million user PII records for sale. While unconfirmed, this claim highlights the ongoing trend of large-scale data breaches targeting telecommunications providers.
*   **[NEW] Hacker Group Claims Breach of Brazilian University, Affecting 53,000:** The BLCKORDER hacker group claims responsibility for a data breach at Celso Lisboa University in Brazil, with a database allegedly containing records of 51,000 students and 2,000 employees advertised for sale. This incident underscores the continued targeting of the education sector for large-scale personal data theft.

**Unusual Cybercrime Motives:**

*   **[NEW] Taiwan Funeral Operator Hacked Fire Dept to Illegally Solicit Clients:** A funeral service operator in Taiwan has been arrested for allegedly hiring hackers to breach emergency dispatch systems of the Taoyuan Fire Department and National Fire Agency. The group stole personal data from emergency records to illegally solicit business from grieving families, leading to arrests for cybersecurity and data protection law violations.

## Articles in this publication
- [CISA Warns of Actively Exploited Critical Flaw in UnrealIRCd](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-warns-of-actively-exploited-unrealircd-flaw-cve-2026-114713/) (high)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added a critical command injection vulnerability in the UnrealIRCd server, CVE-2026-114713, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. The flaw allows unprivileged users on linked servers to execute commands with server-level authority. Exploitation was observed in the wild just one day after a patch was released, prompting an urgent call to update.
- [Stolen AI Key at Fireworks AI Triggers Supply Chain Attack on Cursor](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/stolen-ai-key-leads-to-trojanized-vs-code-extension-supply-chain-attack/) (high)
  A sophisticated supply chain attack began with a stolen API key from AI provider Fireworks AI, allowing attackers to breach developer tool company Cursor. The threat actors then injected a trojanized version of the popular 'Claude Code' VS Code extension into Cursor's marketplace. For 11 hours, approximately 5,000 developers downloaded the malicious extension, which installed a persistent SSH backdoor on their systems, highlighting the cascading risks in AI-powered development workflows.
- [PostgreSQL Patches 52 Flaws, Including 22-Year-Old RCE Bug](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/postgresql-patches-22-year-old-remote-code-execution-vulnerability/) (high)
  The PostgreSQL project has issued a significant minor version update that remediates 52 vulnerabilities. Most notably, the patch addresses a critical, 22-year-old remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability. Although the flaw requires authentication to exploit, it poses a substantial risk in many environments where credentials may be compromised. The discovery highlights the persistent danger of legacy bugs in mature, widely-trusted open-source projects and reinforces the need for diligent patch management.
- [ConnectWise Probes ScreenConnect Partner Account Compromise](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/connectwise-investigates-screenconnect-partner-account-compromise/) (high)
  ConnectWise is actively investigating reports of unauthorized access to its partners' ScreenConnect accounts. The incident is being treated as a supply chain attack, as threat actors have successfully used their access to deploy unauthorized remote-access software into the environments of downstream customers. The compromise of a powerful RMM tool like ScreenConnect represents a significant threat, potentially giving attackers administrative access to a multitude of client networks.
- [Taiwan Funeral Operator Hacked Fire Dept to Illegally Solicit Clients](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/taiwan-funeral-operator-hacked-fire-department-for-client-data/) (medium)
  A funeral service operator in Taiwan has been arrested for allegedly hiring hackers to breach the emergency dispatch systems of the Taoyuan Fire Department and National Fire Agency. The group stole personal data from emergency records, allowing the operator to illegally solicit business from the families of deceased individuals by falsely claiming to be government-assigned. The case highlights a unique and predatory motive for cybercrime, leading to three arrests for violations of cybersecurity and data protection laws.
- [Momentum Telecom Breach Claim Alleges 7.5M User Records for Sale](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/momentum-telecom-breach-claim-alleges-7-5m-users-exposed/) (medium)
  A threat actor, 'Torax2020,' has posted an unverified claim on a criminal forum alleging a data breach at Momentum Telecom, a provider of cloud voice services. The actor is offering for sale two data files said to contain the personally identifiable information (PII) of over 7.5 million users. The data was allegedly exfiltrated earlier in 2026. While the claim has not been confirmed by the company, it highlights the ongoing trend of large-scale data breaches targeting telecommunications providers.
- [Hacker Group Claims Breach of Brazilian University, Affecting 53,000](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/brazilian-university-celso-lisboa-data-breach-claim/) (medium)
  The threat actor group BLCKORDER has claimed responsibility for a data breach at Celso Lisboa University in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. A user on a dark web forum is advertising a database for sale, allegedly containing the records of 51,000 students and 2,000 employees. The claim, while unverified, highlights the continued targeting of the education sector by cybercriminals seeking to steal large volumes of personal data.
