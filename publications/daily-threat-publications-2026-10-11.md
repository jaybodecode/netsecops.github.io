# Data Breaches & Exploited Vulnerabilities Dominate Cybersecurity News

**Published:** 2026-10-11 | **Articles:** 7

**Major Data Breaches and Legal Repercussions**

*   **IDScan.net Breach Escalates:** IDScan.net faces 19 class-action lawsuits following a breach that exposed up to 150 million driver's licenses. The lawsuits cite negligence and a failure to implement standard security practices, increasing the company's legal and financial exposure.
*   **Danish National Register Compromised:** Denmark's National Population Register was breached due to extreme negligence by third-party IT provider Pays ApS. Weak passwords, including '123456' on privileged accounts, allowed attackers to exfiltrate sensitive citizen data for 21 days.

**Ransomware Trends and Threat Actor Disruption**

*   **Ransomware Data Exfiltration Surges:** Zscaler reports a 275% surge in ransomware data exfiltration, detailing technical aspects of large-scale theft by groups like Babuk and INC Ransom. The report offers actionable intelligence for detecting suspicious activity and mitigating AI-generated phishing.
*   **Suspected ShinyHunters Hacker Arrested:** Edward Dubrovsky, a cybersecurity executive, was arrested in connection with the ShinyHunters group and the FBIJobs.gov breach. The arrest raises questions about the integrity of the incident response industry.
*   **Chinese Hacking Contractor Disrupted:** Allies exposed Chinese hacking contractor 'Integrity Technology Group' (Flax Typhoon), seizing domains for Microscan and FishHub tools. Microscan used an IoT botnet for scanning, while FishHub was a spearphishing platform. CISA added exploited vulnerabilities to its KEV catalog.

**Critical Vulnerabilities Under Active Exploit**

*   **Cisco and SonicWall Flaws Actively Exploited:** Cisco and SonicWall are patching critical, actively exploited vulnerabilities. Cisco's Catalyst SD-WAN Manager has an authentication bypass (CVE-2026-76504), and SonicWall's SMA 1000 series has a severe SSRF bug (CVE-2026-102255). Both are on CISA's KEV list.
*   **OT Security Digest Highlights Device Flaws:** This OT security roundup details critical vulnerabilities in Cisco Nexus switches (CVE-2026-76465), ASUS routers, TP-Link Tapo cameras, and Satel industrial dataloggers, indicating widespread risks in connected devices.

## Articles in this publication
- [IDScan.net Confirms Breach; 153M Driver's Licenses for Sale Online](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/idscan-confirms-breach-after-153m-drivers-licenses-appear-on-dark-web/) (critical)
  Identity verification firm IDScan.net has confirmed a massive data breach after a database containing scans of over 153 million U.S. and Canadian driver's licenses was put up for sale on a dark web marketplace. The company detected unauthorized access to its cloud platform on September 1, 2026. The compromised data includes full names and government-issued ID numbers, posing a severe risk of identity theft.
- [Ransomware Data Exfiltration Surges 275%, Zscaler Reports](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/zscaler-report-ransomware-data-exfiltration-surges-275-percent/) (informational)
  A new report from Zscaler ThreatLabz highlights a dramatic shift in ransomware tactics, with the volume of exfiltrated data increasing by over 275% year-over-year to nearly 900 terabytes. This indicates a strategic move from encryption-focused attacks to data theft for extortion. The report also notes that attackers are increasingly targeting employees in privileged roles and using generative AI to accelerate their operations. The freight & logistics and utilities sectors saw the largest growth in attacks.
- [Denmark's National Population Register Breached, 8.8M Records Exposed](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/denmark-national-population-register-breached-exposing-8-8-million/) (high)
  Danish authorities have disclosed a massive data breach affecting the country's Central Person Register (CPR), exposing the personal information of 8.8 million people. The breach was not caused by a software vulnerability but by attackers misusing the legitimate credentials of a private company authorized to access the database. The compromised data includes names, addresses, and the highly sensitive 10-digit CPR numbers, creating a significant risk of identity theft and fraud for a large portion of the Danish population. An investigation is underway, and the third-party's access has been revoked.
- [FBI Arrests Suspected ShinyHunters Hacker in FBIJobs.gov Breach](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/fbi-arrests-suspected-shinyhunters-member-in-fbi-jobs-portal-breach/) (medium)
  The FBI has arrested a Canadian cybersecurity expert in Pennsylvania, suspected of being a co-conspirator with the ShinyHunters hacking group. The arrest is linked to the September 2026 breach of the FBI's own job application portal, FBIJobs.gov. The ShinyHunters group had claimed responsibility for the attack, which they attributed to a contractor's failure to patch a third-party platform. The arrest marks a significant development in the multi-country investigation into the notorious extortion-focused hacking collective.
- [Allies Expose Chinese Hacking Contractor 'Integrity Technology Group'](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/us-uk-issue-alert-on-chinese-hacking-contractor-integrity-technology-group/) (high)
  An international coalition of cybersecurity agencies, including those from the U.S. and U.K., has issued a joint advisory on Integrity Technology Group (ITG), a sanctioned Chinese company. The alert details how ITG provides hacking tools and services for state-sponsored espionage campaigns, acting as a contractor for threat groups like Flax Typhoon. The advisory exposes ITG's TTPs, including the use of custom tools like 'MicroScan' and 'EBurst' to target critical infrastructure and government entities worldwide.
- [Cisco and SonicWall Flaws Under Active Exploit; CISA Issues KEVs](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisco-and-sonicwall-zero-day-vulnerabilities-actively-exploited/) (critical)
  Cisco and SonicWall are patching critical, actively exploited vulnerabilities in widely deployed enterprise products. Cisco fixed an authentication bypass (CVE-2026-76504, CVSS 9.8) in its Catalyst SD-WAN Manager. SonicWall patched a maximum-severity SSRF bug (CVE-2026-102255, CVSS 10.0) in its SMA 1000 series appliances. CISA has added both to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, mandating urgent patching for federal agencies.
- [OT Security Digest: Flaws in Cisco, ASUS, TP-Link, Satel Devices](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ot-security-digest-critical-flaws-in-cisco-asus-tp-link-satel-devices/) (medium)
  This operational technology security roundup for October 10, 2026, details several critical vulnerabilities. Cisco patched a remote code execution flaw (CVE-2026-76465, CVSS 9.8) in its Nexus switches. ASUS fixed critical code-execution bugs (CVSS 9.3) in its routers. Additionally, high-severity vulnerabilities were addressed in TP-Link Tapo cameras and Satel industrial dataloggers, highlighting widespread risks in connected devices.
