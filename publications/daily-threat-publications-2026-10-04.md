## Articles in this publication
- [Technical University of Denmark Breach Exposes Data of 200,000](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/technical-university-of-denmark-data-breach-exposes-200000-users/) (high)
  The Technical University of Denmark (DTU) has suffered a major data breach after attackers compromised its central identity management system, DTUBasen. The incident may affect up to 200,000 current and former students, employees, and partners, with attackers exfiltrating a large volume of data stretching back to 2003. Exposed information includes highly sensitive Danish civil registration (CPR) numbers, full names, addresses, and contact details, placing affected individuals at a high risk of identity theft and fraud. DTU has notified authorities and is urging users to take protective measures.
- [South Korean Financial Sector Hit by Coordinated Cyberattacks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/south-korean-financial-sector-hit-by-coordinated-cyberattacks/) (high)
  Multiple South Korean financial institutions, including major banks like Shinhan Bank and KB Kookmin Bank, have been targeted in a widespread and coordinated cyber campaign. The attacks resulted in the breach of customer and employee data, prompting President Lee Jae Myung to order a full investigation. Authorities suspect the attackers may have used artificial intelligence and targeted weaker, externally-connected systems like loan-agent websites to bypass the core, isolated banking networks. Attack traffic was traced to multiple countries, suggesting a sophisticated, large-scale operation.
- [Epic Systems Halts Development to Fix MyChart Security Flaws](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/epic-systems-halts-mychart-development-to-fix-security-flaws/) (high)
  Epic Systems, a dominant vendor in electronic health records (EHR), has taken the highly unusual step of halting most product development for approximately six weeks. The pause is to allow its teams to focus on remediating significant cybersecurity flaws discovered in its MyChart patient portal, which is used to manage the records of over 320 million patients. The vulnerabilities, identified by a frontier AI model from Anthropic, could potentially allow unauthorized access to sensitive patient medical records without being logged, posing a severe risk to data privacy.
- [Fortra Patches Critical Flaws in BoKS Privileged Access Manager](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/fortra-patches-critical-vulnerabilities-in-boks-pam/) (critical)
  Fortra has released security updates for its Core Privileged Access Manager (BoKS) platform, addressing eight vulnerabilities, three of which are critical. The most severe flaw, CVE-2026-79901 (CVSS 9.9), allows for a full authentication bypass. Other critical bugs include a command injection vulnerability (CVE-2026-79898, CVSS 9.1) leading to root-level command execution, and a stack buffer overflow (CVE-2026-12627, CVSS 9.8) that could cause arbitrary code execution. Fortra is not aware of any active exploitation and urges customers to apply the patches immediately.
- [Suspected ShinyHunters Hacker 'Rey' Detained in Jordan](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/shinyhunters-suspect-rey-detained-in-jordan-cooperating-with-fbi/) (medium)
  A suspected key member of the prolific ShinyHunters hacking and data extortion group has been detained in Jordan and is reportedly cooperating with the U.S. Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI). The individual, identified as Saif al-Din Khader, who allegedly used the alias "Rey," is said to be helping law enforcement identify other members of the cybercrime collective. The detention is a major development following ShinyHunters' recent high-profile claim of breaching FBI systems and stealing a massive trove of sensitive data. The group's dark web leak site has since gone offline.
- [U.S. Senate Passes Bipartisan Healthcare Cybersecurity Act](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/us-senate-passes-bipartisan-healthcare-cybersecurity-act/) (informational)
  The U.S. Senate has unanimously passed the Health Care Cybersecurity and Resiliency Act, a bipartisan bill aimed at strengthening the cyber defenses of the nation's healthcare sector. The legislation authorizes grants to help providers, especially those in rural areas, improve their security capabilities. It also mandates cybersecurity training, improves coordination between HHS and CISA, and seeks to modernize HIPAA security rules to include measures like encryption and multi-factor authentication. The bill now moves to the House of Representatives for consideration.
