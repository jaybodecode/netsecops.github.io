## Articles in this publication
- [U.S. Agencies Issue Urgent Warning on Industrial Refrigeration Cyberattacks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/us-agencies-warn-of-cyberattacks-targeting-industrial-refrigeration-systems/) (high)
  Five U.S. federal agencies, including CISA and the FBI, have issued a joint warning about an ongoing global cyber threat targeting industrial refrigeration and cold chain systems. The advisory follows recent attacks in Europe where hackers compromised internet-exposed controllers, causing food spoilage and equipment damage. The agencies urge organizations to secure their operational technology (OT) by removing devices from the public internet and applying patches.
- [French Bank Crédit Mutuel Arkéa Hit by Cyberattack Enabling $133M+ Crypto Theft](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/french-bank-credit-mutuel-arkea-hit-by-cyberattack-enabling-133m-crypto-theft/) (critical)
  French banking group Crédit Mutuel Arkéa has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack that facilitated fraudulent transactions, leading to a massive cryptocurrency theft. On-chain investigator ZachXBT traced the incident, alleging that over $133 million in crypto was stolen from the bank's accounts on exchanges like Kraken and Crypto.com. The bank stated the attackers gained access via a service provider's platform and that around €100 million was missing.
- [CISA Adds Actively Exploited SonicWall MFA Bypass Flaw to KEV Catalog](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-warns-of-actively-exploited-sonicwall-ssl-vpn-mfa-bypass-flaw/) (critical)
  The U.S. CISA has added a critical SonicWall SSL-VPN vulnerability, CVE-2026-19868, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, confirming active attacks. The flaw, with a CVSS score of 7.5, allows an attacker with valid credentials for a secondary authentication method to bypass SAML and multi-factor authentication (MFA). Federal agencies are mandated to patch by November 20, 2026, and all organizations using affected SonicWall devices are urged to update immediately.
- [York University Discloses Breach of Data from Online Forms](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/york-university-discloses-data-breach-affecting-online-form-users-since-2007/) (medium)
  York University in Toronto is investigating a cybersecurity incident discovered on October 25, 2026, which resulted in the theft of personal data. The breach affects individuals who submitted information through various online forms, including residence applications, between 2007 and October 2026. For a small number of victims, more sensitive data like government ID or financial details were exposed. The university is notifying affected individuals and offering credit monitoring services.
- [AI Hacking Framework 'ARTEX' Linked to South Korean Bank Heists](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ai-hacking-framework-artex-linked-to-south-korean-bank-breaches/) (high)
  An investigation into an AI-powered hacking framework named ARTEX has uncovered exposed server infrastructure and direct links to recent cyberattacks against South Korean banks. The findings, which emerged on November 7, 2026, demonstrate the tangible threat of weaponized AI tools in the financial sector. The ARTEX framework appears to automate multiple stages of an attack, from reconnaissance to exploitation, showcasing the evolving capabilities of AI-driven cyber threats.
- [Who Gives A Crap Data Exposed in Marketing Vendor Breach](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/who-gives-a-crap-customer-data-exposed-in-third-party-marketing-breach/) (medium)
  The eco-friendly toilet paper company Who Gives A Crap has notified customers of a data breach originating from a compromised US-based marketing software provider. The incident, which occurred between October 30 and November 1, 2026, exposed personal information of customers in Australia, the UK, and the US. According to the company, payment details, passwords, and order information were not affected. The breach highlights the ongoing risks associated with supply chain security.
- [VC Firms Raise $750M for Cybersecurity to Fight AI Threats](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/venture-capital-firms-raise-750m-for-cybersecurity-to-combat-ai-threats/) (informational)
  Venture capital firms are injecting nearly $750 million into new cybersecurity funds, signaling strong investor confidence in startups built to counter AI-powered cyberattacks. Brightmind Partners is raising a $250 million fund, while Ballistic Ventures is targeting $490 million across two funds. This significant investment highlights the cybersecurity sector's resilience and growth, driven by the urgent need for innovative solutions to defend against increasingly sophisticated and automated threats.
- [Microsoft Patches Paint RCE Flaw via Microsoft Store Update](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/microsoft-patches-paint-rce-vulnerability-cve-2026-83844-via-store-update/) (high)
  Microsoft has patched an important-rated remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability, CVE-2026-83844, in the Windows 11 Paint application. The flaw, an out-of-bounds read with a CVSS score of 8.1, could allow an unauthenticated attacker to execute code over a network without user interaction. Unusually, the patch is delivered via a Microsoft Store update, not through the standard Windows Update process. Users must manually update the Paint app to version 11.2609.401.0 or later to be protected.
