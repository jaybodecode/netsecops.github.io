UNM Health Services Disrupted by Cyberattack, Extortion Group Claims Data Theft

The University of New Mexico (UNM) Health system experienced a significant cyberattack on October 26, 2026, causing major disruptions to clinical operations, including the diversion of ambulances and cancellation of surgeries. A new extortion group calling itself "BYOD" has claimed responsibility, alleging the theft of a massive trove of sensitive patient, student, and employee data. While UNM Health has since restored critical systems, the incident highlights the severe operational risks facing the healthcare sector.

CISA Adds Exploited Google Brotli and Adobe Commerce Flaws to KEV

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added two actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. The flaws include a critical out-of-bounds write in Google's Brotli library, dubbed Node2Shell (CVE-2026-111497), and a file upload vulnerability in Adobe Commerce/Magento (CVE-2026-48356). Federal agencies are now mandated to patch by November 10, 2026.

AI-Powered Hacking Tools Like CyberStrikeAI Used in Japan Attacks

Security researchers have uncovered three distinct cyberattack campaigns targeting Japanese organizations that demonstrate the increasing operational use of artificial intelligence in offensive operations. One notable campaign leveraged an open-source AI penetration testing framework named CyberStrikeAI, which used a swarm of 44 AI agents to automate the entire attack lifecycle, from reconnaissance to data exfiltration, against at least 19 organizations.

GMO Internet Group Offers $1M Bounty After Refusing Ransom Demand

Following a breach of its 'Onamae.com Desktop Cloud' service, Japanese tech giant GMO Internet Group has refused to pay a ransom demand of approximately $50 million. Instead, the company has launched a $1 million 'GMO Cybercrime Bounty' program to identify and lead to the arrest of the attackers. GMO is also offering compensation to affected customers and has actively worked to take down the hackers' data leak site.

White House Launches 'Project Lifeline 250' to Secure Rural Hospitals

The White House, through the Office of the National Cyber Director (ONCD), has launched 'Project Lifeline 250,' a six-month pilot program designed to bolster the cybersecurity of underserved rural hospitals. Kicking off in South Carolina, the initiative is a public-private partnership providing free cybersecurity hardware, services, and support from technology companies like Microsoft, Cisco, and Zscaler to facilities that often lack the resources to defend against modern cyber threats.

AI Security Startup Upwind Raises $400M, Valuation Soars to $3.8B

Upwind Security, a startup specializing in cloud and AI application security, has closed a $400 million Series C funding round, pushing its valuation to $3.8 billion. The round, led by Bessemer Venture Partners, reflects surging investor interest and confidence in platforms designed to secure complex cloud environments and the rapidly growing deployment of AI models and agents.

Citrix Zero-Days and Oracle PeopleSoft Flaw Under Active Exploit

Update:New research from the Zero Day Initiative details that CVE-2026-35273 is not a single flaw but a complex chain of three vulnerabilities. It combines a Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF) in the Integration Gateway, a default trust misconfiguration in the Management Hub (PSEMHUB) allowing local loopback trust, and a third bug for arbitrary code execution. This chain enables unauthenticated RCE. Affected versions include PeopleTools 8.61 and 8.62. A critical workaround involves editing the `allowedhosts.properties` file to remove `127.0.0.1` and `localhost` entries, breaking the trust relationship exploited by the chain.

PHP2Shell: Critical Flaws Trigger Emergency Patches, Mass Scans

Update:A China-linked APT group is actively exploiting PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619) in combination with a Linux kernel privilege escalation flaw, Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300), to gain root access on internet-facing firewalls. The campaign involves reconnaissance using FOFA, leading to the compromise of 25 firewalls, including one belonging to a U.S. government-affiliated organization. Attackers deploy PHP webshells and establish reverse SSH tunnels for persistence, indicating a highly targeted and sophisticated operation with espionage motives. This represents a significant escalation in the threat landscape for PHP2Shell.
