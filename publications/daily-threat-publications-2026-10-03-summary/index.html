Today New Articles

Critical RCE Vulnerability (CVSS 9.8) Affects Siemens PLCs

A critical remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability with a CVSS score of 9.8 has been disclosed in multiple Siemens Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs), including the widely used S7 series. The flaw poses a severe risk to industrial control systems, potentially allowing attackers to disrupt physical processes. Siemens has issued firmware updates and urges immediate patching.

New Zealand NCSC Report Names China as Top Cyber Threat

New Zealand's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has officially identified China as the most 'persistent and capable' state-sponsored cyber threat to the nation. An annual report reveals that state-backed actors were linked to 86 nationally significant cyber incidents in the past year, targeting critical sectors like government, healthcare, and IT as part of long-term espionage campaigns.

Booba Project Ransomware Claims Attack on NY Healthcare Provider

The 'Booba Project' ransomware group has claimed responsibility for a cyberattack against Associated Gastroenterologists of Central New York, a healthcare provider. The group alleges it has stolen 70 gigabytes of data, which could include highly sensitive patient Protected Health Information (PHI) and employee data. The provider has reportedly filed a data breach notification, and legal firms are investigating for a potential class-action lawsuit.

Foreign Hackers Breach Two Colorado Water Utilities, Manipulate Systems

Update:New intelligence indicates a significant escalation in cyberattacks targeting U.S. water and wastewater systems, with a strategic shift from financial extortion to operational disruption. Phishing emails have been identified as a primary initial access vector, allowing state-sponsored actors, including those linked to Iran, to compromise IT networks and pivot to OT environments. The EPA reports a 'several-fold increase' in incidents across states like Minnesota, Colorado, Georgia, and Michigan, highlighting widespread vulnerabilities such as default passwords and lack of MFA. This trend signifies a grave and increasing threat to public health and critical infrastructure.

Citrix Patches Two Critical NetScaler Zero-Days Under Active Attack

Update:CISA has reinforced its alert regarding the actively exploited Citrix NetScaler zero-days (CVE-2026-88771, CVE-2026-88772), providing additional guidance for detection and remediation. New hunting hints include monitoring unusual traffic on UDP port 443 for DTLS exploitation and crashes of the `nsppe` process. Detection methods now emphasize utilizing vendor tools and considering memory/filesystem analysis for high-value systems. Remediation steps are further detailed with links to MITRE ATT&CK mitigations, reinforcing the urgency for patching and compromise hunting.

Industry Leaders Warn AI-Powered Attacks Are Outpacing Defenses

Update:Microsoft's 2026 Digital Defense Report, released October 1, 2026, provides concrete data reinforcing warnings about AI-powered attacks. The report highlights that AI is reducing attack lifecycles from days to minutes, with phishing incidents rising from 7% to 23% and public-facing application exploits from 15% to 24%. It cites the 'JadePuffer' campaign as an example of fully autonomous AI attacks, emphasizing the urgent need for AI-powered defense to counter machine-speed threats and the erosion of the defender's time advantage. The report also details targeted industries including Government, IT, and Research/Academia, with the US being the most targeted country.

Critical Fortinet FortiMail Zero-Day Exploited for RCE

Update:Fortinet has clarified that the FortiMail 7.2.x branch will not receive patches for CVE-2026-104286, mandating users on these versions to upgrade to a newer, supported release. The vulnerability is further detailed with specific CWEs: CWE-22 (path traversal) and CWE-158 (improper neutralization of a NULL byte). New actionable hunting hints include monitoring for POST requests to `/api/v1/apps/`, searching for files named `new` or `out.cgi`, and using a command line pattern `find / -name "*new*" -newermt "2026-09-30"` to identify newly created malicious files on the system.

KillSec Ransomware Group Dismantled in International Takedown

Update:This update provides significant new technical insights into the KillSec ransomware group's operations, detailing specific MITRE ATT&CK techniques likely employed for initial access, execution, persistence, defense evasion, exfiltration, and impact. It also includes detailed hunting hints such as command-line patterns (`vssadmin.exe delete shadows`), process names (`rclone.exe`, `megasync.exe`), and event IDs (4720) for detecting similar activity. Furthermore, the article reports the arrest of Fouad Eltibrizi (alias "Archduke"), a Dutch national facing extradition to the U.S. on hacking and extortion charges, indicating a key role in the group's activities. The group's operation as a data broker, selling stolen data for up to $500,000, is also highlighted.
