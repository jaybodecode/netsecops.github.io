Russian Zero-Click Exploits, Iranian ICS Attacks, and Critical Zero-Day Patches

Publication Date: July 23, 2026

Summary

This intelligence brief for July 23, 2026, covers a major Russian state-sponsored espionage campaign using a zero-click Zimbra exploit to target Western organizations. U.S. agencies have also expanded warnings about Iranian-affiliated actors targeting industrial control systems from Siemens and Schneider Electric. Additionally, critical zero-day vulnerabilities in Check Point and Microsoft SharePoint products are under active attack, prompting CISA to mandate urgent patching. Other major developments include a large-scale data breach at Paidwork, a new ransomware strain from the Chaos group, and a GAO report on duplicative federal cyber regulations.
