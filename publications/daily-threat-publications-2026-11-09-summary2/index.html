N0N Extortion Group Claims Breach of 830M Expedia Loyalty Records

The data extortion group N0N has listed Expedia Group on its dark web leak site, claiming to have exfiltrated 830 million records from the company's loyalty program. The group alleges the data includes customer emails, loyalty IDs, and booking values. However, as of November 9, 2026, N0N has not provided any proof to substantiate its claims. Expedia Group has not issued a public statement or filed a material incident report regarding the alleged data exposure. Security analysts advise caution, as such claims are sometimes exaggerated or fabricated.

Bitter APT Targets South Asia with New C++/Rust PapRAT Backdoor

The cyber espionage group Bitter (APT-C-08) is conducting a new campaign targeting organizations in South Asia with a previously undocumented remote access trojan (RAT) named PapRAT. The malware, which exists in both C++ and Rust versions, is delivered through complex infection chains involving malicious LNK files, VHDX disk images, and WebDAV servers. PapRAT's capabilities include command execution, file manipulation, and screen capture, demonstrating the threat actor's evolving sophistication and use of living-off-the-land techniques.

Insight Credit Union Data Breach Exposes SSNs and Financial Data

Insight Credit Union, a financial institution based in central Florida, has disclosed a data breach that exposed sensitive customer information, including names, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, and financial account details. The credit union began notifying affected individuals and filed reports with state attorneys general on November 6, 2026. In response, attorneys are investigating a potential class-action lawsuit to seek compensation for affected members and compel stronger security measures.

Dutch Regulator Warns Financial Firms of Growing AI and Cyber Risks

The Dutch Authority for the Financial Markets (AFM) has issued a report warning that financial service providers in the Netherlands face increasing cyber risks from the adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) and heavy reliance on third-party IT services. Published on November 9, 2026, the report calls for firms to implement responsible AI policies and improve IT risk management, stressing that companies retain ultimate responsibility for data protection even when services are outsourced.

Marquis Software Ransomware Attack Breaches 670,000 Bank Customers

A ransomware attack on Marquis Software, a U.S. technology vendor for community banks, has resulted in a significant data breach affecting over 670,000 banking customers. The incident, reported on November 8, 2026, highlights the critical supply chain risks facing the financial sector. The attack on Marquis, which provides transaction-management tools, serves as a stark reminder of how a single compromised vendor can have a cascading impact on numerous financial institutions and their clients.

Critical GitLab RCE Flaw (CVE-2026-97229) Actively Exploited

A critical, unauthenticated remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability in GitLab (CVE-2026-97229) is being actively exploited in the wild. The flaw allows attackers to run arbitrary code by submitting a crafted regular expression in a CI/CD configuration file. Due to evidence of active exploitation, CISA has added the vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, mandating federal agencies to patch. Administrators of affected GitLab CE/EE versions are urged to update immediately.

MediaLab Ventures Discloses Data Breach Exposing SSNs

MediaLab Ventures, the technology holding company for brands like Imgur and Kik, has reported a data breach that exposed the first and last names and Social Security numbers of an undisclosed number of individuals. The company filed a notice with the Massachusetts attorney general on November 6, 2026, and is offering 24 months of complimentary credit monitoring and identity theft protection services to those affected by the security incident.
