Today New Articles

Cisco Patches High-Severity RCE Flaw in UCS Central Software

Cisco has released a security update for a high-severity vulnerability, CVE-2026-76506, in its UCS Central Software. The flaw, which has a CVSS score of 7.6, could allow an authenticated, remote attacker to achieve remote code execution (RCE), write arbitrary files, and disclose information. No workarounds are available, and administrators are urged to apply the patch immediately.

NTT Docomo Confirms Data Breach Potentially Affecting 300,000 Customers

Japanese telecommunications giant NTT Docomo has confirmed a major data breach resulting from unauthorized external access. The attack, detected on November 4, 2026, may have compromised the personal information of up to 300,000 customers. Exposed data includes names, addresses, dates of birth, phone numbers, and in some cases, bank account details. The company has suspended its 'My docomo' customer portal while it investigates.

Qilin Ransomware Gang Leaks Burnaby School District Data on Dark Web

The Qilin ransomware group has published sensitive data stolen from the Burnaby School District in British Columbia, Canada, following a ransomware attack in September 2026. The district confirmed the data leak, which includes personal information of students, parents, and staff, such as medical records, learning assessments, and disciplinary files. The district is offering credit monitoring to the approximately 27,000 affected students and their families.

French Bank Crédit Mutuel Arkéa Hit by Cyberattack Enabling $133M+ Crypto Theft

French banking group Crédit Mutuel Arkéa has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack that facilitated fraudulent transactions, leading to a massive cryptocurrency theft. On-chain investigator ZachXBT traced the incident, alleging that over $133 million in crypto was stolen from the bank's accounts on exchanges like Kraken and Crypto.com. The bank stated the attackers gained access via a service provider's platform and that around €100 million was missing.

Hundreds of French Supermarket Refrigeration Controllers Exposed Online After Hack

Update:Five U.S. federal agencies (CISA, FBI, HHS, USDA, DOT) have issued a joint advisory on November 5, 2026, warning of an ongoing global cyber threat targeting industrial refrigeration and cold chain systems. This advisory follows recent attacks in France, Belgium, and Sweden, where internet-exposed Danfoss AK-SM 800A and Copeland XWEB Pro controllers were compromised, leading to food spoilage and equipment damage. The agencies highlight potential similar malfunctions at U.S. military commissaries under investigation, indicating a broader scope. They urge critical infrastructure operators to immediately secure OT environments by removing devices from the public internet, changing default credentials, and applying patches.

UK NCSC: AI-Powered Ransomware on Critical Infrastructure Likely by 2028

Update:The NCSC's 2026 annual review provides further granularity on the 'highly likely' prediction, specifying a 75-95% probability of AI-powered ransomware targeting CNI by 2028. It details AI's role as a 'force multiplier' for social engineering, vulnerability discovery, and malware development, with nation-states like China and Russia expected to leverage it. The report also revealed NCSC handled 337 incidents, 80 nationally significant, in the past year. Compliance guidance now explicitly includes MITRE IDs for patch management and access control, and criticizes 'tech on the cheap', urging organizations to address security debt and master foundational cybersecurity.
