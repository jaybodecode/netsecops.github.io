Cisco and SonicWall Flaws Under Active Exploit; CISA Issues KEVs

Cisco and SonicWall are patching critical, actively exploited vulnerabilities in widely deployed enterprise products. Cisco fixed an authentication bypass (CVE-2026-76504, CVSS 9.8) in its Catalyst SD-WAN Manager. SonicWall patched a maximum-severity SSRF bug (CVE-2026-102255, CVSS 10.0) in its SMA 1000 series appliances. CISA has added both to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, mandating urgent patching for federal agencies.

OT Security Digest: Flaws in Cisco, ASUS, TP-Link, Satel Devices

This operational technology security roundup for October 10, 2026, details several critical vulnerabilities. Cisco patched a remote code execution flaw (CVE-2026-76465, CVSS 9.8) in its Nexus switches. ASUS fixed critical code-execution bugs (CVSS 9.3) in its routers. Additionally, high-severity vulnerabilities were addressed in TP-Link Tapo cameras and Satel industrial dataloggers, highlighting widespread risks in connected devices.

Denmark's National Population Register Breached, 8.8M Records Exposed

Update:New details confirm the Danish CPR data breach was due to extreme negligence by third-party IT provider, Pays ApS. It was discovered that at least three user accounts, including a privileged administrator account, were secured with the notoriously weak password '123456'. This allowed an attacker to gain access to the CPR system for an extended period of 21 days, beginning September 10, 2026, systematically exfiltrating sensitive citizen data. This revelation significantly escalates concerns regarding the foundational security practices of vendors with access to critical national infrastructure and underscores the severe national security implications of such a compromise.

FBI Arrests Suspected ShinyHunters Hacker in FBIJobs.gov Breach

Update:Edward 'Ed' Dubrovsky, a prominent Canadian cybersecurity executive and co-founder of ransomware negotiation firm CYPFER, was arrested on October 8, 2026, in Pennsylvania on federal charges of conspiracy to commit extortion. This arrest is directly linked to the FBI's ongoing investigation into the ShinyHunters hacking group and the September 2026 breach of the FBIJobs.gov portal. Dubrovsky's arrest, following his career advising companies on cyber extortion, raises significant questions about the integrity of the incident response industry and the potential for professionals to cross ethical and legal lines. The case has been transferred to the Eastern District of Texas, and the charges highlight the complex interplay between legitimate cybersecurity services and criminal activities.

Allies Expose Chinese Hacking Contractor 'Integrity Technology Group'

Update:New details emerged regarding the US Department of Justice and FBI's disruption of Flax Typhoon. The operation specifically seized seven domains controlling the Microscan and FishHub hacking tools. It was revealed that Microscan utilized a Mirai-infected IoT botnet for widespread vulnerability scanning, while FishHub served as a dedicated spearphishing platform. Furthermore, the threat actor employed SoftEther VPN for establishing persistent access to compromised networks. CISA has also added five vulnerabilities exploited by Flax Typhoon to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, emphasizing the critical need for patching.
