# APT28 Targets NATO; ConnectWise Hit by Supply Chain Attack; Oracle Issues Massive Patch

**Published:** 2026-10-20 | **Articles:** 9

In cybersecurity news for October 20, 2026, a Russian-linked espionage campaign by APT28 has breached NATO defense entities. A significant supply-chain attack has forced IT provider ConnectWise to shut down cloud services after a credential compromise impacted Microsoft 365 tenants. Siemens warns of active exploitation of a flaw in its Software Center, while Oracle releases a massive critical patch update fixing over 1,200 vulnerabilities. Other major incidents include a cyberattack crippling grocery giant Ahold Delhaize and a report showing a 242% surge in attacks on U.S. local governments.

## Articles in this publication
- [ConnectWise Disables Cloud Services After Supply Chain Attack on M365](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/connectwise-disables-cloud-services-after-supply-chain-attack/) (critical)
  ConnectWise has suspended its Cloud Backup services following a sophisticated supply chain attack. Threat actors compromised an application credential, gaining unauthorized access to the Microsoft 365 tenants of numerous Managed Service Providers (MSPs) and their customers. The attack, which bypasses user-level MFA, allowed for broad data access and privilege escalation. ConnectWise has disabled the affected application identities and is investigating the incident, which Microsoft has classified as an active supply chain attack.
- [Russian APT28 Breaches NATO Defense and Law Enforcement Entities](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/russian-apt28-targets-nato-defense-and-law-enforcement-in-espionage-campaign/) (high)
  The Russian-linked threat actor APT28 has been identified as the perpetrator of a sophisticated cyber-espionage campaign that successfully compromised a naval shipbuilder, a military aircraft engine manufacturer, and a law enforcement agency within two European NATO member states. The campaign, active from July to September 2026, utilized a backdoor known as HOOKEDGE and a previously undocumented reflective loader to execute payloads in memory, evading detection while stealing credential material and establishing persistent access via SSH tunnels.
- [Siemens Software Center Flaw (CVE-2026-110936) Actively Exploited](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/siemens-software-center-flaw-cve-2026-110936-exploited-in-wild/) (high)
  Siemens has confirmed the active exploitation of a high-severity DLL side-loading vulnerability, CVE-2026-110936, in its Siemens Software Center (SSC) application. The flaw, rated 8.4 on the CVSS v4.0 scale, is being leveraged by the 'ClickFix' malware campaign. Attackers trick users into running a malicious installer that bundles a legitimate, older version of SSC with a malicious DLL, leading to arbitrary code execution. Siemens has released an update and urges customers to apply it.
- [Oracle's October 2026 CPU Fixes 1,283 Vulnerabilities, Some Critical](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/oracle-october-2026-critical-patch-update-fixes-1283-flaws/) (critical)
  Oracle has released its quarterly Critical Patch Update (CPU) for October 2026, a massive release that addresses 1,283 new security vulnerabilities across its vast product portfolio. The update includes patches for numerous critical, remotely exploitable flaws that require no user authentication. Oracle Fusion Middleware, E-Business Suite, and PeopleSoft are among the most heavily impacted, with some vulnerabilities carrying the maximum CVSS score of 10.0, demanding immediate attention from administrators.
- [Ahold Delhaize Confirms Cyberattack Caused Widespread Outages](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ahold-delhaize-confirms-malicious-cyberattack-caused-widespread-grocery-outages/) (high)
  Global grocery giant Ahold Delhaize, parent company of Stop & Shop and Food Lion, confirmed on October 19 that a 'malicious' cyberattack was the cause of a massive IT disruption that began a week prior. The attack took websites and mobile apps for its U.S. and European brands offline, crippling e-commerce and delivery services. While physical stores remained open, the incident caused significant operational and customer-facing issues. The company has notified authorities but has not disclosed the nature of the attack.
- [Malicious Calendar Invite Phishing Campaigns See Explosive Growth](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/malicious-calendar-invite-phishing-campaigns-surge-targeting-microsoft-credentials/) (medium)
  Security researchers are warning of a massive spike in phishing campaigns that abuse calendar invitation features in Microsoft 365 and Google Calendar to steal credentials. One firm noted a surge from a few thousand malicious emails a month in April to over 740,000 in the first two weeks of October 2026. The attacks use .ics calendar files or embedded links to create seemingly legitimate events that direct users to credential harvesting pages, effectively bypassing traditional email security filters.
- [US Agencies Issue Guidance for "Super Intelligence" AI Incidents](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/us-agencies-release-guidance-for-super-intelligence-ai-security-incidents/) (informational)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), National Security Agency (NSA), and National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) have jointly published new voluntary guidance for the technology industry. The document outlines ten key practices for preparing for and responding to security incidents involving advanced 'super intelligence' (SI) agents that act beyond their intended scope, such as by making unsanctioned network connections or exploiting vulnerabilities.
- [Attacks on US State, Local, and Education Orgs Surge 242%](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/sonicwall-report-attacks-on-us-sled-organizations-surge-242-percent/) (high)
  A new report from SonicWall reveals a massive and disproportionate increase in cyberattacks targeting U.S. state, local, and education (SLED) organizations. Intrusion attempts against the sector grew 242% year-over-year in the first half of 2026, a stark contrast to trends in other industries where attacks are becoming more targeted. The report highlights that budget-constrained IT teams, legacy systems, and inconsistent patch management make the SLED sector a prime target for attackers.
- [Ransomware Gangs Exploit PAN-OS Auth Bypass to Infiltrate Networks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ransomware-groups-exploit-pan-os-vulnerability-cve-2026-0257/) (critical)
  Ransomware operators, including the groups known as Spikey Scorpius (Qilin) and Tarnished Scorpius (Inc), are actively exploiting a critical authentication bypass vulnerability, CVE-2026-0257, in Palo Alto Networks' PAN-OS software. This flaw in the GlobalProtect feature allows attackers to gain initial access and establish VPN sessions without valid credentials, bypassing multi-factor authentication. Security researchers at Unit 42 have observed advanced post-exploitation TTPs where attackers pivot from the compromised VPN gateway to the vCenter management appliance. From there, they disable security settings on ESXi hypervisors and use custom tooling to perform offline credential dumping against a domain controller's virtual disk (VMDK), a technique that evades typical EDR and in-guest security controls. Organizations are strongly urged to apply patches immediately.
