Ransomware Attacks Hit All-Time High in August 2026

Global ransomware attacks reached a new peak in August 2026, with a record 997 incidents reported, a 23% increase from July. According to data from Comparitech, this averages to 32 attacks per day. The utility sector was hit particularly hard, with attacks doubling, while the healthcare sector saw a 30% rise. The Qilin and The Gentlemen ransomware gangs were the most prolific, collectively responsible for over a quarter of all attacks. The Clop group also resurfaced, linking attacks to a vulnerability in PTC Windchill.

Mathspace Breach Affects 1M Users via Metabase Flaw

The Australian education platform Mathspace has disclosed a data breach affecting over one million users. Attackers gained access to an internal reporting database by exploiting CVE-2026-72898, a known SQL injection vulnerability in a self-hosted instance of the open-source business intelligence tool, Metabase. The compromised data includes user names, email addresses, and location information. The company stated that more sensitive data like passwords and academic records were not impacted.

Pro-Ukraine 'Hacking Cat' Group Deploys Wiper Malware

The pro-Ukrainian hacktivist group 'Hacking Cat' has reportedly escalated its cyber operations against Russian organizations by deploying custom-built malware, including destructive data wipers. The group's stated goal is to disrupt Russian military logistics. This shift from simple disruption or data leaks to the use of destructive malware marks a significant tactical evolution. The incident also highlights the challenge of attribution, as researchers note an overlap in tools used by Hacking Cat and other pro-Ukrainian groups, and the group itself has disputed some public attributions.

Unit 42 Develops Model to Map Cloud Identities via Behavioral Clustering

Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 has published research on a new behavioral clustering model that uses unsupervised machine learning to analyze cloud audit logs. By examining over 40,000 identities in AWS environments, the model, which leverages UMAP and HDBSCAN algorithms, successfully maps entities to their functional roles, such as administrators or DevOps tools. A key outcome of this research is the ability to translate these complex behavioral patterns into lightweight, portable SQL queries. This allows organizations to implement continuous, scalable threat detection to identify anomalous activities and masquerading threats without the overhead of a persistent machine learning pipeline.

EU Cyber Resilience Act's 24-Hour Reporting Mandate Starts Sept 11

Update:The EU Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) has officially launched its Single Reporting Platform, fulfilling the mandate of the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA). As of September 11, 2026, manufacturers of products with digital elements must use this portal to report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe security incidents within 24 hours. This operationalizes the CRA's reporting obligations, providing a centralized system for compliance. Additionally, open-source software stewards will be subject to these rules starting in late 2027.

Vishing Campaign 'PREY-0058' Targets Execs for M365 Data Theft

Update:A new variant of social engineering attacks targeting Microsoft cloud users has emerged, leveraging calls and SMS messages to personal phones. Attackers impersonate IT support, urging victims to 'update' their passkey, MFA, or SSO settings on convincing phishing sites. This leads to credential harvesting, account takeover, and data exfiltration, similar to previous campaigns. The campaign, observed since May 2026, broadens the scope beyond executives to general Microsoft cloud service users, highlighting evolving lures and communication channels for credential theft.

Microsoft's Record September Patch Tuesday Fixes 974 Vulnerabilities

Update:Further analysis of Microsoft's September Patch Tuesday reveals over 20 unauthenticated Remote Code Execution (RCE) vulnerabilities, expanding beyond the previously noted DNS Server RCE. New critical RCEs include CVE-2026-69579 (MSMQ), CVE-2026-69845 & CVE-2026-72979 (DHCP), CVE-2026-73009 (SSTP), and notably, CVE-2026-69676 (Kerberos), posing a direct threat to Active Directory. The update also provides enhanced hunting hints (e.g., MoUsoCoreWorker.exe monitoring, specific event IDs for DNS/DHCP/MSMQ/Kerberos) and new workarounds like disabling non-essential services or restricting network access.

CISA adds five actively exploited flaws to its KEV catalog

Update:The CISA KEV catalog update for JFrog Artifactory now lists CVE-2026-42018 (CVSS 7.5) as an Improper Authentication vulnerability leading to sensitive data leakage, replacing the previously noted CVE-2026-82329. The update also provides enhanced detection and response guidance, including D3FEND mappings for log analysis and network monitoring, to help organizations identify and mitigate exploitation attempts across all listed vulnerabilities.

GitLab CVSS 10.0 vulnerability exploited one day after disclosure

Update:Further details on the actively exploited GitLab path traversal vulnerability (CVE-2026-85706) have emerged. The Hong Kong Computer Emergency Response Team Coordination Centre (HKCERT) has also issued an alert confirming widespread exploitation. New cyber observables and hunting hints are provided, including specific log sources like `gitlab-workhorse/current` and `production_json.log`, and target files such as `/etc/gitlab/gitlab.rb`. A detailed SIEM detection rule (YAML) is included to identify exploitation attempts, alongside guidance on EDR monitoring for suspicious process activity. Mitigation strategies are further elaborated with D3FEND mappings, emphasizing immediate patching, network access restriction, and configuration hardening.
