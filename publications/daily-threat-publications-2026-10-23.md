# Ransomware, Zero-Days, and Supply Chain Risks Dominate Cybersecurity News

**Published:** 2026-10-23 | **Articles:** 8

**Critical Vulnerabilities and Exploitation:**

- **[UPDATE] Advisory Warns of Prolific SafePay Ransomware Targeting Critical Infrastructure**: Further analysis reveals SafePay ransomware employs double extortion, exfiltrating data before encryption using tools like 7-Zip and RClone. Initial access now specifically includes exploitation of Citrix and Fortinet products, with attackers using Mullvad VPN for anonymity and PsExec for lateral movement.
- **[NEW] Arista Patches Exploited VeloCloud Orchestrator Zero-Day**: Arista Networks has released emergency patches for CVE-2026-114625, a critical SQL injection vulnerability in its VeloCloud Orchestrator that allows for privilege escalation and is being actively exploited. Successful exploitation grants an authenticated user superuser control over SD-WAN infrastructure.
- **[NEW] Critical Splunk Enterprise Flaw Allows System Compromise**: A critical improper access control vulnerability, CVE-2026-76299, in Splunk Enterprise (CVSS 9.9) allows a low-privileged network attacker to compromise systems without user interaction, impacting confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

**New Threats and Incidents:**

- **[NEW] Capcom Data Breach Exposes Data of Over 11 Million Users**: Japanese gaming company Capcom confirmed a data breach of its CAPCOM ID service, impacting over 11.7 million customer accounts with unauthorized access to email addresses, IP addresses, and login information. Financial data was not compromised.
- **[NEW] Ransomware Attack Forces NY School District to Cancel Classes**: The Oneonta City School District in New York canceled classes for two days due to a ransomware attack affecting five schools and approximately 1,700 students. Investigations are ongoing with law enforcement.
- **[NEW] New 'GlassWorm' Malware Spreads Via VS Code Extensions**: A new self-propagating malware named GlassWorm targets developers by hiding in malicious Visual Studio Code extensions. It functions as a worm and credential stealer, exfiltrating browser logins, developer tokens, and cryptocurrency wallet data, posing a supply chain risk.

**Industry Trends and Law Enforcement Actions:**

- **[UPDATE] Kroll Report: 69% of Orgs Breached in Past Year, Most via Third Parties**: Updated analysis shows only 7% of breaches originated in the tech sector, yet these incidents caused 79% of all individuals impacted by third-party compromises. The report expands on regulatory implications and vendor risk management, highlighting fourth-party risks and contractual obligations.
- **[NEW] INTERPOL Disrupts 10,909 Malicious Networks, Arrests 1,462**: Operation Serengeti III, a global law enforcement action led by INTERPOL, disrupted 10,909 malicious networks and led to 1,462 arrests across 21 countries. The operation recovered over $105 million in illicit funds, with estimated losses linked to dismantled networks exceeding $385 million.

## Articles in this publication
- [Advisory Warns of Prolific SafePay Ransomware Targeting Critical Infrastructure](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/joint-advisory-warns-of-highly-active-safepay-ransomware/) (high)
  CISA, the FBI, and international partners issued a joint advisory on the SafePay ransomware group, one of the most active operations of 2026. Since August 2024, the group has compromised approximately 600 organizations, including critical infrastructure sectors, across the Americas, Europe, and Australia. The advisory details the gang's common TTPs, which include credential theft, exploiting known vulnerabilities, and phishing. Agencies urge organizations to implement MFA, patch vulnerabilities, and maintain offline backups to defend against this widespread threat.
- [Kroll Report: 69% of Orgs Breached in Past Year, Most via Third Parties](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/kroll-report-finds-69-percent-of-orgs-breached-in-past-year/) (informational)
  Kroll's 2026 Data Breach Outlook report reveals that 69% of organizations experienced at least one data breach in the past year, with 34% suffering multiple incidents. The report highlights the growing threat of supply chain risk, finding that breaches originating from third-party technology providers had the widest impact, affecting 69% of all individuals impacted in Kroll's incident response cases. A notable disparity exists in risk perception, with IT leaders more concerned about vendor breaches than C-suite executives.
- [Capcom Data Breach Exposes Data of Over 11 Million Users](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/capcom-data-breach-exposes-data-of-11-million-users/) (high)
  Japanese gaming company Capcom has confirmed a significant data breach of its CAPCOM ID membership service, impacting over 11.7 million customer accounts. The incident, detected on October 19, 2026, resulted in the unauthorized access and theft of user data including email addresses, IP addresses, and login information. While financial data was not compromised, Capcom is warning all users to be vigilant against phishing attempts. The company has notified authorities and is in the process of contacting affected individuals.
- [Arista Patches Exploited VeloCloud Orchestrator Zero-Day](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/arista-patches-actively-exploited-velocloud-orchestrator-zero-day-cve-2026-114625/) (critical)
  Arista Networks has released emergency patches for a critical zero-day vulnerability, CVE-2026-114625, in its VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO) on-premises solution. The flaw, a SQL injection that allows for privilege escalation, is being actively exploited in the wild. Successful exploitation allows an authenticated user to gain superuser control over the SD-WAN infrastructure. Arista has identified malicious IPs and urges customers to patch immediately.
- [Ransomware Attack Forces NY School District to Cancel Classes](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/oneonta-new-york-school-district-cancels-classes-after-ransomware-attack/) (medium)
  The Oneonta City School District in New York canceled classes for two days following a ransomware attack discovered on its network. The attack has affected five schools and around 1,700 students. The district's technology team is working with local, state, and federal authorities to investigate the incident, contain the damage, and restore systems. While classes are canceled, athletic events are proceeding as scheduled.
- [INTERPOL Disrupts 10,909 Malicious Networks, Arrests 1,462](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/interpol-operation-serengeti-iii-disrupts-10000-cybercrime-networks/) (informational)
  A global law enforcement action led by INTERPOL, codenamed Operation Serengeti III, has resulted in the disruption of 10,909 malicious networks and the arrest of 1,462 suspects across 21 countries. The operation, which ran from June to August 2026, targeted a wide range of cybercrime activities and successfully recovered over $105 million in illicit funds. The total financial losses linked to the dismantled networks are estimated to exceed $385 million.
- [New 'GlassWorm' Malware Spreads Via VS Code Extensions](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/glassworm-malware-spreads-through-malicious-vs-code-extensions/) (high)
  A new self-propagating malware called GlassWorm is targeting developers by hiding in malicious Visual Studio (VS) Code extensions. The malware acts as both a worm and a credential stealer, designed to exfiltrate saved browser logins, developer tokens (e.g., for GitHub, AWS), and cryptocurrency wallet data. Its worm-like capabilities allow it to spread to other systems, posing a significant supply chain risk if it compromises developer machines with access to sensitive code repositories.
- [Critical Splunk Enterprise Flaw Allows System Compromise](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/splunk-enterprise-vulnerability-cve-2026-76299-allows-system-compromise/) (critical)
  A critical improper access control vulnerability, CVE-2026-76299, has been discovered in Splunk Enterprise, carrying a CVSS score of 9.9. The flaw allows a low-privileged network attacker to compromise affected systems with no user interaction required. A successful exploit could have a high impact on the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the Splunk instance. Cisco, which discovered the flaw internally, has released security hardening updates and strongly urges users to patch immediately.
