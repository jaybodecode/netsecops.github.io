Warlock Ransomware Hits Critical Sectors via Old SharePoint Flaws

The China-linked Warlock ransomware group has breached at least four organizations by exploiting a year-old set of Microsoft SharePoint vulnerabilities known as 'ToolShell'. The campaign, active in the two months preceding October 1, 2026, targeted a water utility, a telecom provider, a government body, and a university across Europe, Africa, and Latin America. After gaining access via unpatched on-premises SharePoint servers, the attackers deploy web shells and use 'bring your own vulnerable driver' (BYOVD) techniques to disable security software before deploying the Warlock ransomware across the network.

Ledger Wallets Hit by Supply Chain Attack; Over $90M in Crypto Stolen

A sophisticated supply chain attack targeting Ledger hardware wallets has resulted in the theft of over $92 million in cryptocurrency from 311 wallets. The attack involved physical tampering with devices sold through CryptoBilis, an authorized reseller in Southeast Asia. Ledger confirmed that at least one compromised device contained an unauthorized hardware implant. This method bypasses the core security model of hardware wallets by compromising the device before it reaches the customer. Ledger has advised customers who purchased from the reseller to cease using the devices and transfer funds immediately.

iRhythm Notifies Over 360,000 Patients of Data Breach from June

Medical device maker iRhythm Technologies is notifying over 360,000 patients that their personal and medical data was stolen in a data breach that occurred in June 2026. The incident involved unauthorized access to business applications hosted by a third party. Attackers accessed and exfiltrated data between June 3 and June 8, then contacted iRhythm on June 9 with a ransom demand. The compromised data includes patient names, addresses, dates of birth, insurance information, and device serial numbers. Notifications to affected individuals began on October 2, 2026, nearly four months after the breach.

New Critical RCE Flaw Found in Patched Citrix NetScaler Appliances

Citrix has disclosed another critical vulnerability, CVE-2026-107406, in its NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances, rated 9.5 CVSS. This memory overflow flaw can lead to unauthenticated remote code execution (RCE). Alarmingly, the vulnerability affects not only older builds but also recent versions that were patched in September 2026 to fix other zero-day flaws. Exposure depends on the appliance's configuration as a SAML identity provider (IdP). While there are no known exploits in the wild, Citrix urges customers to apply the new patches immediately to prevent compromise.

Iranian Actor Targets Iraqi Infrastructure with "Blinder Tunnel"

Update:The Iranian 'Blinder Tunnel' campaign has escalated, successfully breaching a government cloud environment in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, exfiltrating over 1GB of data, and compromising an Israeli security professional. Attackers are now employing additional social engineering tactics, including fake LinkedIn personas and counterfeit government portals (e.g., Kuwait Ministry of Foreign Affairs), alongside their previous Dubai Airports impersonation. A new key tool identified is 'StarkMeet,' a trojanized video meeting application used to deploy backdoors, complementing the previously known ShelbyLoader V2 malware.

Cisco and SonicWall Flaws Under Active Exploit; CISA Issues KEVs

Update:New reports confirm widespread active exploitation of SonicWall SMA 1000 SSRF (CVE-2026-102255) just 48 hours after patches were released on October 6, 2026. Security researchers detected 135 distinct exploit attempts originating from U.S.-based IP addresses by October 8. The attacks involve crafted HTTP OPTIONS requests, and the flaw can be chained for full device takeover and remote code execution. Organizations are urged to apply patches immediately, as hundreds of appliances remain exposed. Specific affected versions prior to `12.5.0-03082` and `12.4.3-03670` are targeted.
