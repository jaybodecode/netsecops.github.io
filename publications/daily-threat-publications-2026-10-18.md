# AI Fuels Ransomware, Zero-Days Exploited, and Supply Chain Attacks

**Published:** 2026-10-18 | **Articles:** 8

**Critical Vulnerabilities Under Active Exploitation**:

*   **AI Exploited in 84% of Ransomware Attacks**: A new report highlights that 84% of ransomware attacks leverage AI, with 40% now employing 'triple extortion' tactics that include direct threats against employees and their families. This escalates pressure on organizations beyond technical defenses.
*   **China-Linked Group Exploits BeyondTrust Zero-Day**: CISA has added CVE-2026-102764, a BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day exploited by Armory Castle, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. The flaw, with a CVSS score of 9.8, is used for espionage, with attackers modifying system files and using stolen credentials for lateral movement.
*   **Plex Media Server Exploit Chain Deploys Cryptomining Malware**: Threat actors are actively exploiting a chain of four vulnerabilities in unpatched Plex Media Server software (versions prior to 1.43.3.10828) to achieve unauthenticated remote code execution and deploy cryptomining malware.
*   **High-Severity SQL Injection Flaw in Modern Events Calendar**: A high-severity SQL injection vulnerability (CVE-2026-96534, CVSS 7.7) has been disclosed in Modern Events Calendar Lite and Pro WordPress plugins (versions prior to 7.37.0), allowing authenticated attackers to exfiltrate sensitive database information.

**Supply Chain and Data Breach Incidents**:

*   **ConnectWise Cloud Backup Supply Chain Attack Hits M365 Tenants**: ConnectWise has suspended its Cloud Backup service and disabled seven application identities following a supply chain attack that began on October 13, 2026. The attack leveraged a compromised service account credential with Delegated Admin Privileges (DAP) to access 173 Microsoft 365 tenants.
*   **Millions of California Students' Data Exposed in Testing Provider Breach**: Further investigation into the ETS data breach reveals that a compromised login credential accessed an SFTP site, exposing student dates of birth and Unique Statewide Student Identifiers (SSID). Data dating back to 2006 was also compromised.
*   **Cyber-Physical Attack Hits 700 French Supermarkets via Refrigerators**: Hundreds of affected Danfoss and Copeland refrigeration controllers remain directly exposed to the public internet, indicating that the root cause of the initial cyber-physical attack has not been remediated, leaving systems vulnerable to further exploitation.

**Regulatory and Legal Developments**:

*   **Health Network and IT Firm to Pay $2.7M for Breach of 888,000 Patient Records**: Boston Children's Health Physicians (BCHP) and its IT provider, XTIUM, have agreed to a $2.7 million settlement over a 2024 data breach that exposed the sensitive personal and medical data of nearly 888,000 pediatric patients, including Social Security numbers and financial records.

## Articles in this publication
- [AI Exploited in 84% of Ransomware Attacks, Report Finds](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ai-exploited-in-84-percent-of-ransomware-attacks-report-finds/) (high)
  A new report from Semperis finds that Artificial Intelligence (AI) was a factor in 84% of ransomware attacks against surveyed organizations. Alarmingly, 44% of these incidents involved attackers weaponizing the victim's own AI systems. The report also highlights a rise in physical threats and psychological intimidation against employees to compel ransom payments, with identity systems like Active Directory remaining a primary target.
- [China-Linked Group Exploits BeyondTrust Zero-Day in Espionage Attacks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/china-linked-hackers-exploit-beyondtrust-zero-day-in-espionage-campaign/) (critical)
  A China-linked cyber espionage group known as Armory Castle (UNC5221) has been exploiting a critical zero-day vulnerability, CVE-2026-102764, in BeyondTrust Remote Support appliances. The flaw, with a CVSS score of 9.3, allows unauthenticated attackers to gain root-level control. The campaign, which began in late August 2026, involved deploying custom malware like the SAWFLY web shell and BARECLAW tunneler to achieve persistence, escalate privileges, and exfiltrate credentials from the device's secrets database. The attackers also wiped logs to cover their tracks. BeyondTrust has released patches and urges self-hosted customers to update immediately, rebuild compromised appliances from clean images, and rotate all associated credentials. The campaign targeted legal, technology, and government sectors, consistent with intelligence collection motives.
- [Cyber-Physical Attack Hits 700 French Supermarkets via Refrigerators](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/french-supermarkets-hit-by-cyber-physical-attack-on-refrigeration-systems/) (high)
  A large-scale cyber-physical attack has crippled over 700 supermarkets across France, including major chains like Coopérative U and Carrefour Market. Attackers remotely manipulated Danfoss refrigeration controllers, causing them to overheat to temperatures as high as 50°C (122°F). This act of sabotage resulted in massive spoilage of fresh and frozen food, operational shutdowns, and even small fires. The attack exploited vulnerabilities in obsolete equipment that was no longer receiving security updates. The incident, which has also affected supply chains in several other European countries and the US, highlights the severe real-world consequences of unsecured Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and IoT devices in critical infrastructure.
- [ConnectWise Cloud Backup Supply Chain Attack Hits M365 Tenants](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/connectwise-cloud-backup-supply-chain-attack-compromises-m365-tenants/) (high)
  An active supply chain attack is leveraging a compromised Microsoft 365 credential for the ConnectWise Cloud Backup application (AppId: 506e08f9-6e9a-4da4-942b-a099a03644ae). Threat actors are abusing the app's high-privilege OAuth permissions to access customer M365 environments, read mailboxes and files, and attempt to create backdoors. The activity was observed originating from the IP address 144.172.64.128.
- [Millions of California Students' Data Exposed in Testing Provider Breach](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/california-student-data-breach-exposes-millions-of-records/) (high)
  A major data breach at ETS, California's standardized testing provider, has exposed the personal information of millions of current and former public school students. The breach, which occurred over several months via a compromised login credential, exposed names, addresses, demographic details, and disability records. Data dates back to 2023, affecting far more than the state's current 5.7 million students. Free identity protection services are being offered.
- [Health Network and IT Firm to Pay $2.7M for Breach of 888,000 Patient Records](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/pediatric-health-network-xtium-pay-2-7m-data-breach-settlement/) (high)
  Boston Children's Health Physicians (BCHP) and its IT service provider, XTIUM, have agreed to a $2.7 million settlement with the attorneys general of New York and Connecticut. The settlement resolves investigations into a 2024 data breach originating at XTIUM that exposed the sensitive personal and medical data of nearly 888,000 pediatric patients. The compromised information included Social Security numbers, medical details, and financial records. The companies are also required to implement stronger data security measures.
- [Plex Media Server Exploit Chain Deploys Cryptomining Malware in the Wild](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/plex-media-server-vulnerabilities-exploited-for-cryptomining/) (high)
  Threat actors are actively exploiting a chain of four vulnerabilities in unpatched Plex Media Server software to achieve unauthenticated remote code execution (RCE). The exploit, publicly available as 'Plex2Shell', is being used to install cryptocurrency mining malware on compromised servers. The flaws affect versions prior to 1.43.3.10828, and users, especially those on NAS devices, are urged to update immediately. Honeypot activity confirmed active scanning and exploitation from multiple IP addresses.
- [High-Severity SQL Injection Flaw in Modern Events Calendar WordPress Plugin](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/wordpress-plugin-modern-events-calendar-sql-injection-vulnerability/) (high)
  A high-severity SQL injection vulnerability, CVE-2026-96534, has been disclosed in the popular Modern Events Calendar Lite and Pro WordPress plugins. The flaw, rated 7.7 on the CVSS scale, allows an authenticated attacker with low-level permissions, such as a Subscriber, to execute malicious SQL queries. A successful exploit could lead to the exfiltration of sensitive information from the website's database. All versions prior to 7.37.0 are affected, and administrators are urged to update immediately.
