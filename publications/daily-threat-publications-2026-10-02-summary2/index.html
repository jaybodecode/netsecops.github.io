Critical Fortinet FortiMail Zero-Day Exploited for RCE

Fortinet has disclosed a critical zero-day vulnerability, CVE-2026-104286, in its FortiMail secure email gateway. The flaw, which has a CVSS score of 9.8 and is under active exploitation, allows an unauthenticated attacker to write arbitrary files, leading to remote code execution. CISA has added the vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, mandating urgent action from federal agencies. While patches are pending, Fortinet has provided immediate workarounds, including disabling the IBE feature or restricting web access to the management interface.

China-Aligned Group TA419 Targets U.S. AI Policy Experts

A China-aligned cyber-espionage group, tracked as TA419 by Proofpoint, is conducting sophisticated phishing campaigns targeting U.S. experts in artificial intelligence policy. The attackers use elaborate impersonations of prominent officials and AI company employees to build rapport before luring targets to adversary-in-the-middle (AitM) phishing sites. The goal is to steal cloud account credentials, including passwords, MFA codes, and session cookies, to gather intelligence on U.S. AI strategy.

Ransomware Data Exfiltration Surges 275%, Zscaler Reports

A new report from Zscaler ThreatLabz highlights a dramatic shift in ransomware tactics, with the volume of exfiltrated data increasing by over 275% year-over-year to nearly 900 terabytes. This indicates a strategic move from encryption-focused attacks to data theft for extortion. The report also notes that attackers are increasingly targeting employees in privileged roles and using generative AI to accelerate their operations. The freight & logistics and utilities sectors saw the largest growth in attacks.

Citrix Patches Two Critical NetScaler Zero-Days Under Active Attack

Update:New intelligence reveals that active exploitation of Citrix NetScaler zero-days (CVE-2026-88771, CVE-2026-88772) began as early as September 3, 2026, over three weeks before public disclosure. Multiple threat actors, including suspected state-sponsored groups, are leveraging these vulnerabilities for mass exploitation. Beyond webshells, attackers are now deploying novel tunneler malware for persistent access. The scope of impact is significant, affecting dozens of organizations across North America and Europe in government, finance, energy, and healthcare sectors, with an estimated 20,000 NetScaler instances exposed online. Organizations must not only patch but also conduct thorough post-compromise investigations for these advanced threats.

Pentagon Reveals Breach; Kiteworks Halts Systems on Threat Intel

Update:The U.S. Department of Defense has confirmed that the DMDC data breach, which occurred from October 2025 to July 2026, exposed the personal records of over 3 million individuals. This includes 2.76 million living and 294,000 deceased military and civilian personnel and their families. Attackers maintained undetected access for nine months, exploiting a vulnerability in a file-sharing system. The compromised data is highly sensitive, including full names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, contact information, and military service details, significantly increasing the risk of identity theft and targeting by foreign intelligence services. DoD began sending notifications on September 18, 2026.

Cisco Patches Actively Exploited SD-WAN Auth Bypass Zero-Day

Update:The new report specifies that Cisco has released patched software versions 26.2.1 and 26.1.2.1 to address the critical authentication bypass vulnerability, CVE-2026-76504, in its Catalyst SD-WAN Manager. Additionally, Cisco has confirmed that its own cloud-hosted and managed SD-WAN deployments have already been patched. This provides more concrete remediation details for organizations to apply the necessary updates immediately, reinforcing the urgent need to upgrade to these specific fixed versions.

Europol Dismantles KillSec Ransomware; Teenager Suspected Leader

Update:Further details on the KillSec ransomware group takedown reveal that five central servers were seized, providing more insight into the operation's scale. The investigation also clarified roles within the group, identifying an 18-year-old developer alongside the 16-year-old administrator. KillSec, active since 2024, primarily gained initial access through exploiting software vulnerabilities and poorly secured cloud storage, targeting professional services, technology, healthcare, and government sectors, with victims mainly in the US and India. Additional hunting hints for similar ransomware activity include monitoring for large data transfers, credential abuse, and disabled security tools.

Google Report: AI Accelerates Vulnerability Discovery & Exploitation

Update:The latest Google GTIG report reveals that AI-assisted vulnerability discovery yields more severe flaws, with 50% leading to Remote Code Execution (RCE), nearly double the rate of other methods. A critical example, CVE-2026-1731 in BeyondTrust, was exploited within four days of disclosure. Furthermore, the report identifies a significant new attack surface: AI systems themselves. Over 1,500 AI-related CVEs were disclosed in 2026, impacting orchestration frameworks like Flowise, Langflow, vLLM, and Ollama, underscoring the need for securing the AI supply chain.
