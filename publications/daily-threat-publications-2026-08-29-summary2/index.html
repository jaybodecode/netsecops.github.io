Summary

This cybersecurity brief for August 29, 2026, covers multiple critical incidents. PaperCut is urging immediate patching for a zero-day vulnerability chain under active exploitation that allows for remote code execution. ServiceNow has addressed three separate CVSS 10.0 vulnerabilities in its platform. Major data breaches have been disclosed by McKesson, Manchester Airports Group, and Baylor Genetics, collectively affecting millions. Meanwhile, the U.S. ATF confirmed a major cyberattack claimed by the Qilin ransomware group, and the city of Berlin is refusing to pay a ransom to the Rhysida group after a massive data theft.
