# Ransomware Surges, Critical Exploits Mandate Urgent Patching

**Published:** 2026-10-27 | **Articles:** 7

**Critical Vulnerabilities Under Active Exploitation:**

*   **Arista Patches Exploited VeloCloud Orchestrator Zero-Day (CVE-2026-114625):** Arista has released patches for a critical zero-day vulnerability in its VeloCloud Orchestrator, now listed on CISA's KEV catalog. This SQL injection flaw, with a CVSS score of 9.9, allows for privilege escalation and has been actively exploited, necessitating immediate patching by federal agencies by October 26.
*   **PHP2Shell Flaws Actively Exploited, Triggering Global Scramble:** CISA has issued a directive for federal agencies to patch PHP2Shell vulnerabilities by October 29, 2026, as millions of services remain vulnerable. Exploitation has been observed, leading to confirmed remote code execution and cookie exfiltration, with slow global patch adoption posing a widespread risk.

**New Threats and Advisories:**

*   **Ransomware Attacks Surge 50% in Q3 2026, Fueled by Gang Rivalry:** Ransomware attacks saw a 50% increase in Q3 2026, driven by a shift towards data-theft-only extortion models by groups like The Gentlemen and Qilin. This tactic, particularly impacting SMBs and manufacturing in North America, prioritizes reputational damage and intellectual property loss over operational disruption. Detection strategies now focus on DLP, Network Traffic Analysis, and UEBA.
*   **New Ransomware Group 'Emperador' Claims Attack on AB InBev:** A new ransomware group, 'Emperador,' has claimed responsibility for a data breach at Anheuser-Busch InBev, alleging the exfiltration of 3.9 GB of sensitive data, including Azure blob storage assets and PKI certificates. The group is demanding a $1 million ransom, though these claims remain unverified.

**Policy & Industry Notes:**

*   **Senator Warren Blasts Financial Regulators' Weaker Cyber Rules:** Senator Elizabeth Warren has criticized the Federal Reserve, FDIC, and OCC for recent policy changes that she argues weaken cybersecurity enforcement for banks. She contends that a narrower definition of 'unsafe or unsound' practices creates a high bar for regulatory action, potentially leaving the U.S. banking system vulnerable to AI-enabled threats.
*   **ODVA's CIP Security Spec for EtherNet/IP Earns IEC 62443 Certification:** ODVA's CIP Security specification for EtherNet/IP networks has achieved IEC 62443-4-2 certification at Security Level Capability 3 (SL-C 3). This certification provides a verified security baseline for industrial control systems, expected to simplify the development and certification of secure products for manufacturers and aid compliance with regulations like the EU's Cyber Resilience Act.
*   **New NERC Rule for Low-Impact Systems Creates Audit & Compliance Risks:** The NERC CIP-003-9 standard, effective since April 2026, is presenting compliance challenges for the North American energy sector regarding vendor remote access to low-impact Bulk Electric System (BES) cyber systems. A key concern is the discrepancy between the three-year recordkeeping requirement and NERC's potential six-year audit scope, risking non-compliance penalties for older activities.

- [Ransomware Attacks Surge 50% in Q3 2026, Fueled by Gang Rivalry](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ransomware-attacks-surge-50-percent-in-q3-2026-fueled-by-gang-rivalry/) (high)

The surge is largely driven by intense competition between the two most prolific gangs, "The Gentlemen" and "Qilin," which were collectively responsible for 746 attacks. The report suggests the market has stabilized after previous law enforcement takedowns, entering a "new era" of escalating activity as groups vie for dominance in the cybercriminal underground.
- [Arista Patches Exploited VeloCloud Orchestrator Zero-Day](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/arista-patches-actively-exploited-velocloud-orchestrator-zero-day-cve-2026-114625/) (critical)
  Arista Networks has released emergency patches for a critical zero-day vulnerability, CVE-2026-114625, in its VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO) on-premises solution. The flaw, a SQL injection that allows for privilege escalation, is being actively exploited in the wild. Successful exploitation allows an authenticated user to gain superuser control over the SD-WAN infrastructure. Arista has identified malicious IPs and urges customers to patch immediately.
- [PHP2Shell Flaws Actively Exploited, Triggering Global Scramble](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/php2shell-vulnerabilities-cve-2026-109618-cve-2026-109619-trigger-global-patch-scramble/) (critical)
  Two critical unauthenticated remote code execution (RCE) vulnerabilities in PHP, dubbed 'PHP2Shell' and tracked as CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, are under active, widespread exploitation. Affecting an estimated 70% of websites, the flaws were exploited before patches were officially released, leading to emergency advisories from the NSA and CISA. The vulnerabilities, which stem from memory corruption issues in the PHP engine, allow attackers to take full control of servers. Organizations globally have taken systems offline as a precaution while a frantic race to apply patches is underway. CISA has added both CVEs to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog.
- [Senator Warren Blasts Financial Regulators' Weaker Cyber Rules](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/senator-warren-blasts-financial-regulators-for-weakening-cyber-enforcement/) (informational)
  Senator Elizabeth Warren has sent letters to the Federal Reserve, FDIC, and OCC, urging them to rescind recent policy changes that she argues weaken bank examiners' ability to enforce cybersecurity standards. Warren contends that a new, narrower definition of 'unsafe or unsound' practices creates a dangerously high bar for regulatory action, leaving the U.S. banking system vulnerable to emerging threats, particularly those enabled by artificial intelligence. She warns that this policy shift could allow cybersecurity vulnerabilities to fester until a major attack occurs.
- [ODVA's CIP Security Spec for EtherNet/IP Earns IEC 62443 Certification](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/odva-cip-security-specification-for-ethernet-ip-earns-iec-62443-certification/) (informational)
  In a major step for industrial cybersecurity, the standards body ODVA announced its CIP Security specification for EtherNet/IP networks has been certified by TÜV Rheinland against the IEC 62443-4-2 standard. The specification achieved Security Level Capability 3 (SL-C 3), designed to protect against skilled industrial control system attackers. This certification provides a verified security baseline that is expected to significantly streamline the process for industrial device manufacturers to develop and certify their own secure-by-design products, helping them meet emerging regulations like the EU's Cyber Resilience Act.
- [New NERC Rule for Low-Impact Systems Creates Audit & Compliance Risks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/new-nerc-cip-003-9-standard-creates-audit-and-compliance-risks/) (medium)
  The NERC CIP-003-9 standard, effective since April 2026, is creating significant compliance and audit challenges for the North American energy sector. The rule imposes new, enforceable controls for vendor remote access to low-impact Bulk Electric System (BES) cyber systems, a category that previously lacked a minimum security baseline. A key issue is a discrepancy in recordkeeping requirements: entities must retain evidence for three years, but NERC audits can span a six-year period, potentially leaving operators unable to prove compliance for older activities and facing steep penalties.
- [New Ransomware Group 'Emperador' Claims Attack on AB InBev](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/new-ransomware-group-emperador-claims-attack-on-brewer-ab-inbev/) (high)
  A new and aggressive ransomware group, 'Emperador,' has claimed responsibility for a data breach at Anheuser-Busch InBev, the world's largest brewing company. In a post on their dark web leak site, the group alleges it exfiltrated 3.9 GB of sensitive data, including Azure blob storage assets, corporate emails, and internal PKI certificates. Emperador is demanding a $1 million ransom to prevent the public release of the stolen information. The claims remain unverified, and AB InBev has not yet issued a statement.
