# New Zero-Day, Ransomware Activity, and Exploited Vulnerabilities Highlighted

**Published:** 2026-11-20 | **Articles:** 8

**Critical Vulnerabilities and Exploitation:**

*   **New Windows "FastUnlock" Zero-Day Allows Lock Screen Bypass:** A newly disclosed zero-day vulnerability in Windows, "FastUnlock," enables attackers with physical access to bypass the lock screen and execute programs by exploiting the Narrator accessibility feature and Microsoft account recovery options. This bypass is effective even on some BitLocker-encrypted systems with TPM auto-unlock.
*   **CISA Adds Four Actively Exploited Flaws to KEV Catalog:** The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added four actively exploited vulnerabilities affecting Synology, BerriAI, Apache, and Vanderbilt University products to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. These flaws are being used for ransomware deployment and credential theft, with federal agencies facing November and December 2026 deadlines for patching.

**Ransomware and Information Stealing Threats:**

*   **[UPDATE] Ransomware Gangs Qilin and Barracuda Claim New Corporate Victims:** A recent Intel 471 report confirms the manufacturing sector as the primary target for ransomware in 2026, with Qilin, The Gentlemen, and Akira noted as active groups. Initial access brokers facilitate breaches through vulnerability exploitation and brute-force attacks, with IT/OT convergence increasing risk to legacy systems.
*   **[NEW] "Black Lotus" Emerges as New File-Encrypting Ransomware:** A new file-encrypting ransomware strain named "Black Lotus" has been discovered on underground forums, targeting Windows systems. It encrypts files, appends a ".black_lotus" extension, and leaves a ransom note, suggesting targeting of corporate networks.
*   **[NEW] Ransomware Groups Claim New Victims in Healthcare, Tech, and Mfg:** Multiple ransomware and extortion groups reported new victims on November 19, 2026, including LockBit targeting City International Hospital in Vietnam. Other groups like INC_RANSOM, KRYBIT, and DireWolf listed victims in software, manufacturing, and hospitality sectors.
*   **[NEW] Researchers Analyze "VeeraStealer," a New Info-Stealing Malware:** "VeeraStealer," a new information-stealing malware for Windows, has been analyzed. It harvests sensitive data like browser credentials and Wi-Fi details, employing multiple persistence techniques before exfiltrating data to a command-and-control server.

**Service Disruptions and Targeted Attacks:**

*   **[UPDATE] N-able Confirms Server Breach Caused Email Security Service Outage:** N-able has confirmed a cyberattack on a single web server in their hosted cloud environment caused a service disruption. The compromised server was taken offline, and extensive credential resets were initiated, though core email filtering services remained operational with no evidence of customer data compromise.
*   **[NEW] Attacks Targeting Taiwanese Websites Triple in Recent Weeks:** Security firm Wordfence reported a tripling of cyberattacks targeting Taiwanese websites since late October 2026, with a single IP address linked to over 246,000 blocked requests. The campaign appears to be a large-scale reconnaissance effort scanning for vulnerabilities related to malicious file uploads and SQL injection.

## Articles in this publication
- [Ransomware Gangs Qilin and Barracuda Claim New Corporate Victims](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ransomware-gangs-qilin-and-barracuda-claim-new-victims/) (high)
  The ransomware landscape remains highly active, with the Qilin and Barracuda groups claiming new victims on November 1, 2026. The Qilin gang, one of 2026's most prolific groups, added U.S.-based company Danser to its list of victims. Meanwhile, the Barracuda ransomware group announced it had breached EVINOKS, a Turkish technology and manufacturing firm. Barracuda threatened to release the stolen data from EVINOKS for free on its dark web leak site. These incidents are part of a broader surge in ransomware attacks, which saw a 45% increase in the first half of 2026, with the manufacturing sector being the most frequent target.
- [N-able Confirms Server Breach Caused Email Security Service Outage](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/n-able-cloud-server-breach-disrupts-global-email-security-services/) (high)
  N-able has confirmed a security incident involving unauthorized access to a server supporting its Mail Assure and SpamExperts hosted cloud platforms. The breach, which began on November 16, 2026, led to a multi-day service disruption across the Americas, Europe, and APAC. As a precaution, N-able disabled management interfaces, APIs, and quarantine access. While core email filtering remained operational, administrators were unable to manage the services. An investigation is ongoing to determine the attack vector and full impact, though N-able has stated there is currently no evidence of customer data compromise. This incident is reportedly separate from a recent vulnerability in its N-central RMM product.
- [New Windows "FastUnlock" Zero-Day Allows Lock Screen Bypass](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/windows-fastunlock-zero-day-vulnerability-allows-lock-screen-bypass/) (medium)
  A security researcher has disclosed an unpatched zero-day vulnerability in Windows, dubbed "FastUnlock," that allows an attacker with physical access to a locked PC to execute programs. The technique exploits the Narrator accessibility feature in conjunction with Microsoft account recovery options. The bypass is effective even on some systems with BitLocker full-disk encryption enabled, specifically when a TPM is configured for automatic unlocking at boot.
- [CISA Adds Four Actively Exploited Flaws to KEV Catalog](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-adds-four-actively-exploited-vulnerabilities-to-kev-catalog/) (critical)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added four actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. The critical flaws affect products from Synology, BerriAI, Apache, and Vanderbilt University, with some being used to deploy ransomware or steal credentials. CISA has set deadlines in November and December 2026 for federal agencies to apply patches, urging all organizations to prioritize remediation.
- ["Black Lotus" Emerges as New File-Encrypting Ransomware](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/black-lotus-new-file-encrypting-ransomware-targets-windows/) (medium)
  Security researchers at CYFIRMA have discovered a new file-encrypting ransomware strain named "Black Lotus." The malware, found on underground forums, targets the Windows operating system. Upon execution, it encrypts files, appends a ".black_lotus" extension, and creates a ransom note named "how2recovery.txt." The language in the note suggests that the threat actors are targeting corporate networks for wider impact.
- [Researchers Analyze "VeeraStealer," a New Info-Stealing Malware](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/veerastealer-new-multi-stage-infostealer-malware-analyzed/) (medium)
  A new information-stealing malware named "VeeraStealer" has been analyzed by security researchers. Targeting Windows systems, this multi-stage malware is designed to harvest sensitive data, including browser credentials and saved Wi-Fi details. To ensure its survival on a compromised machine, VeeraStealer employs multiple persistence techniques, such as creating scheduled tasks and modifying the Windows Run registry key, before exfiltrating the stolen data to a command-and-control server.
- [Attacks Targeting Taiwanese Websites Triple in Recent Weeks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cyberattacks-targeting-taiwanese-websites-triple-in-november-2026/) (medium)
  Security firm Wordfence has reported a dramatic tripling in the number of cyberattacks targeting Taiwanese websites with the ".tw" domain since late October 2026. A single IP address, 45.38.60.254, has been identified as the source of over 246,000 blocked attack requests. The campaign appears to be a large-scale reconnaissance effort, systematically scanning for known vulnerabilities related to malicious file uploads and SQL injection, particularly on WordPress sites.
- [Ransomware Groups Claim New Victims in Healthcare, Tech, and Mfg](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/lockbit-inc-ransom-and-krybit-claim-new-victims-in-multiple-sectors/) (high)
  On November 19, 2026, multiple ransomware and extortion groups disclosed a new wave of victims across various sectors. The notorious LockBit group claimed an attack on City International Hospital in Vietnam. Other threat actors, including INC_RANSOM, KRYBIT, and DireWolf, listed victims in the software, manufacturing, and hospitality industries, highlighting the persistent and widespread nature of the ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) ecosystem.
