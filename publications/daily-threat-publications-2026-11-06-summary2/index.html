OpenAI's AI Agent Autonomously Finds and Patches 948 Linux Flaws

OpenAI has developed an autonomous AI pipeline that discovered, exploited, and generated patches for 948 local privilege escalation (LPE) vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel. The five-stage system, which cost $1.2 million in API credits, highlights the transformative potential of AI for both offensive and defensive cybersecurity operations. The project's success, building on earlier research, demonstrates a dramatic acceleration in vulnerability discovery, with the AI identifying novel exploit techniques and outpacing manual human analysis.

CISA Adds Actively Exploited SonicWall MFA Bypass Flaw to KEV Catalog

The U.S. CISA has added a critical SonicWall SSL-VPN vulnerability, CVE-2026-19868, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, confirming active attacks. The flaw, with a CVSS score of 7.5, allows an attacker with valid credentials for a secondary authentication method to bypass SAML and multi-factor authentication (MFA). Federal agencies are mandated to patch by November 20, 2026, and all organizations using affected SonicWall devices are urged to update immediately.

New 'Has' Ransomware and 'LunexLoader' Malware Emerge

Researchers have identified two new emerging threats: 'Has' ransomware and the 'LunexLoader' malware. 'Has' is a new variant of the Dharma ransomware family that uses double extortion tactics, encrypting files and threatening to leak exfiltrated data. 'LunexLoader' is a stealthy, modular malware loader delivered via phishing, designed for sandbox evasion and deploying secondary payloads. These threats highlight the continued evolution of ransomware and the sophisticated, multi-stage nature of modern malware attacks.

Critical Google Kaniko Flaw (CVE-2026-105769) Leaks Azure Credentials

A critical vulnerability, CVE-2026-105769, with a CVSS score of 9.3, has been discovered in Google's popular open-source kaniko tool. The flaw allows a maliciously crafted, lookalike registry hostname to trick kaniko into sending the build's Azure access token to an attacker-controlled server. This could lead to the complete compromise of an organization's Azure environment. All users of kaniko are urged to upgrade to the patched version 1.25.20 immediately.

Microsoft & Partners Disrupt Major Phishing-as-a-Service Operation

Update:New Phishing-as-a-Service (PhaaS) operations, including SkyW4lker and Mir0Auth, are actively exploiting Microsoft's device-code authentication flow to bypass MFA and steal access tokens from U.S. Microsoft 365 users. These campaigns, detailed in new reports, leverage malicious links and PDFs to trick users into authorizing attacker-controlled sessions, leading to severe impacts like data breaches and business email compromise. The attacks highlight the persistent threat of device-code phishing despite previous takedowns, with new technical details on the attack chain and hunting hints for detection.

UK NCSC: AI-Powered Ransomware to Hit Critical Infrastructure by 2028

Update:The NCSC report further clarifies that AI's primary threat lies in automating and accelerating the exploitation of 'long-tolerated' weaknesses, such as end-of-life systems, delayed patching, and insecure configurations. The availability of open-source AI tools is expected to lower the barrier to entry for less sophisticated criminals, making advanced tactics more widespread. To counter this, organizations are urged to adopt AI-powered defense mechanisms, including automated patching (referencing D3FEND's Software Update) and AI/ML-driven security tools, as reactive security postures will be overwhelmed by the speed and scale of AI-driven attacks.

European Supermarkets Hit by Coordinated Freezer Cyberattacks

Update:New information reveals that the coordinated cyberattacks on European refrigeration systems also impacted facilities of France's national blood service, reportedly via a compromised supplier. This significantly broadens the incident's scope to critical public health infrastructure, raising the severity. In response, Singapore's Cyber Security Agency (CSA) issued an advisory on November 6, 2026, urging organizations to secure remotely accessible OT control systems, highlighting increased global concern.
