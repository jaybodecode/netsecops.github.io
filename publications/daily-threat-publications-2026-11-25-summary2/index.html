CISA Adds Actively Exploited Drupal, Optimizely, and AntiCsrf Flaws to KEV

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added three vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, citing evidence of active exploitation. The flaws include a remote code execution vulnerability in Drupal Core (CVE-2026-132165), a critical deserialization flaw in Idunno.AntiCsrf (CVE-2026-131914), and a state control issue in Optimizely Web Experimentation (CVE-2026-132224). Federal agencies are now under a strict deadline to remediate these high-risk vulnerabilities.

AI-Assisted Campaign by GreyNoise13 Hits 22 Orgs via Check Point Flaws

A threat actor tracked as GreyNoise13 (GT13) has compromised at least 22 organizations globally in a sophisticated, AI-assisted hacking campaign targeting Check Point Security Management servers since mid-October 2026. The attackers are leveraging a chain of critical vulnerabilities for initial access, including flaws in Check Point, Citrix, and Ivanti products, impacting critical sectors like energy, finance, and government across eight countries.

Massive Data Leak Exposes Two Billion Credentials from Infostealer Ops

A cybersecurity researcher has discovered a massive, non-password-protected database on the public internet containing approximately two billion credentials. The discovery, made by Jeremiah Fowler and reported by ExpressVPN, is believed to be an aggregation of data harvested from widespread infostealer malware campaigns. The exposed data includes a vast collection of usernames and passwords in plaintext, posing a severe risk of account takeovers and credential stuffing attacks.

Two Critical GeoServer Flaws Actively Exploited for RCE

Threat actors are actively exploiting two critical vulnerabilities in the open-source GeoServer platform (CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129243), chaining them together to achieve unauthenticated remote code execution. The exploitation was observed in the wild by security firm watchTowr just one day after patches were released, prompting urgent warnings for administrators to update their instances or restrict access immediately.

CISA Adds 10 More Exploited Flaws to KEV from Danfoss, HPE, Mitel, Vates

On November 25, 2026, CISA added ten more vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, signaling active exploitation in the wild. The newly added flaws impact a diverse range of products from vendors including Danfoss, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), Mitel, and Vates. Federal agencies are mandated under Binding Operational Directive (BOD) 26-04 to prioritize patching these high-risk vulnerabilities.

NGINX HTTP/2 Use-After-Free Flaw (CVE-2026-123633) Allows DoS, RCE

A critical use-after-free vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-123633, has been discovered in the NGINX HTTP/2 implementation. The flaw can be triggered by an unauthenticated attacker sending a specially crafted, fragmented HTTP/2 request to a vulnerable server configured as a reverse proxy. Successful exploitation can cause a denial of service (DoS) by crashing the worker process. Researchers have also demonstrated that remote code execution (RCE) is possible by chaining the vulnerability with a separate memory disclosure bug, particularly when Address Space Layout Randomization (ASLR) is disabled or bypassed. NGINX has released patches, and administrators are urged to upgrade affected systems immediately.

AI-Powered Hacking Tools Like CyberStrikeAI Used in Japan Attacks

Update:The UK's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has issued a warning regarding a significant increase in AI-assisted cyberattacks since September 2026. Threat actors are leveraging agentic offensive frameworks, such as ARTEX, to automate the discovery and exploitation of common security weaknesses. These include exposed credentials, misconfigurations, and API flaws, allowing attacks to proceed from discovery to exploitation in minutes. This NCSC alert reinforces the growing operational use of AI in cyber warfare, highlighting the speed and scale at which basic security hygiene failures can now be weaponized globally.

FBI Warns of ARTEX, an AI-Powered Tool for Autonomous Cyberattacks

Update:Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 has confirmed over ten global campaigns (July-September 2026) utilizing 'agentic AI attackers' – autonomous AI agents, often LLMs, to automate and accelerate intrusions. These agents operate at machine speed, compressing attack timelines from weeks to hours. Crucially, Unit 42 identified unique behavioral fingerprints for detection: inhuman command velocity (median inter-command gap of 1.4 seconds), structured command headers (e.g., `===[Title]===`), and embedded conversational reasoning comments within scripts. These specific indicators provide new, actionable intelligence for defenders to identify and counter these advanced, AI-driven threats.

Ransomware Attacks Shatter Records in Q3 2026

Update:The latest report from Check Point Research provides additional context on the record-breaking Q3 2026 ransomware activity.
