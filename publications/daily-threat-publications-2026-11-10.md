# Cyber-Physical Attacks Escalate, Major Data Breaches Impact Millions

**Published:** 2026-11-10 | **Articles:** 8

**Critical Vulnerabilities and Active Exploitation:**

*   **Google Patches Eighth Actively Exploited Chrome Zero-Day This Year:** Google has released an emergency update for Chrome (version 157.0.8103.50/.51) to fix a high-severity use-after-free vulnerability in its XML component, CVE-2026-125350. This marks the eighth actively exploited Chrome zero-day patched in 2026, highlighting the ongoing threat of browser exploits.
*   **SAP Patches Four Critical Flaws with Maximum 10.0 CVSS Score:** SAP's November Security Patch Day addresses 35 vulnerabilities, including four critical flaws (ICM2SHELL and PROXYGATE) with a CVSS score of 10.0. These vulnerabilities, while not yet confirmed as exploited in the wild, allow for remote code execution on critical SAP systems and require immediate patching.

**Major Data Breaches and Incidents:**

*   **[UPDATE] Cyber-Physical Attack Hits 700 French Supermarkets via Refrigerators:** The French government has attributed cyber-physical attacks on supermarkets to Iran's IRGC, specifically the 'Shahid Kaveh' unit. The attacks, which caused fires and data loss, have expanded to other European countries and critical sectors like blood banks, prompting France to seek EU sanctions.
*   **Vanderbilt University Hit by 'BYOD' Ransomware, Over 1M Records at Risk:** Vanderbilt University is experiencing a ransomware attack by the 'BYOD' group, potentially exposing the sensitive data of over one million students and staff. The incident has disrupted campus systems and led to file encryption.
*   **Ten Philippine Government Agencies Report Data Breaches:** The Philippines' National Privacy Commission has confirmed data breach notifications from ten government agencies, including the Department of Justice and DICT. The scope and impact of these breaches on sensitive government data are currently under assessment.
*   **Nitori Data Breach May Expose Records of 37.62 Million Customers:** Japanese retailer Nitori Holdings has reported a data breach that may have compromised the personal records of up to 37.62 million customers, including email and home addresses. Financial information was not affected, but customers are advised to be vigilant against phishing.
*   **CIRO Reaches $8.3M Settlement for 2025 Data Breach Affecting 750k:** The Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO) has proposed an $8.3 million settlement for a class-action lawsuit stemming from a 2025 phishing-induced data breach. The incident compromised the personal and financial data of approximately 750,000 Canadians.
*   **Children's Foundation Data Breach Occurs via Third-Party IT Provider:** The Children's Foundation experienced a data breach through its third-party IT provider, Highland Shores Children's Aid Society. Unauthorized access to the provider's network in July may have exposed the names and contact information of volunteers, donors, and applicants.

## Articles in this publication
- [Cyber-Physical Attack Hits 700 French Supermarkets via Refrigerators](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/french-supermarkets-hit-by-cyber-physical-attack-on-refrigeration-systems/) (high)
  A large-scale cyber-physical attack has crippled over 700 supermarkets across France, including major chains like Coopérative U and Carrefour Market. Attackers remotely manipulated Danfoss refrigeration controllers, causing them to overheat to temperatures as high as 50°C (122°F). This act of sabotage resulted in massive spoilage of fresh and frozen food, operational shutdowns, and even small fires. The attack exploited vulnerabilities in obsolete equipment that was no longer receiving security updates. The incident, which has also affected supply chains in several other European countries and the US, highlights the severe real-world consequences of unsecured Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and IoT devices in critical infrastructure.
- [Google Patches Eighth Actively Exploited Chrome Zero-Day This Year](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/google-patches-eighth-actively-exploited-chrome-zero-day-of-2026/) (critical)
  Google has released an emergency security update for Chrome to address a high-severity use-after-free vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-125350. The flaw, located in the XML component, is confirmed to be actively exploited in the wild, marking the eighth Chrome zero-day patched in 2026. The update, version 157.0.8103.50/.51, also fixes seven other high-severity issues. Users are urged to update their browsers immediately to mitigate the risk of arbitrary code execution.
- [Vanderbilt University Hit by 'BYOD' Ransomware, Over 1M Records at Risk](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/vanderbilt-university-hit-by-byod-ransomware-attack/) (high)
  Vanderbilt University has confirmed a major cybersecurity incident, identified as a ransomware attack by the 'BYOD' group. The attackers claim to have stolen sensitive data, including Social Security numbers, for over one million students and staff. The university's IT department instructed campus members to disconnect from Wi-Fi as it works to contain the breach, which has disrupted campus systems and encrypted files with a '.byod' extension.
- [Ten Philippine Government Agencies Report Data Breaches](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ten-philippine-government-agencies-report-data-breaches/) (high)
  The Philippines' National Privacy Commission (NPC) has confirmed that ten government agencies, including the Department of Justice and the Department of Information and Communications Technology, have filed data breach notifications. The NPC is now assessing the scope and nature of the incidents, which raise serious concerns about the security of sensitive government-held data. The full extent of the breaches and the number of affected individuals have not yet been disclosed.
- [SAP Patches Four Critical Flaws with Maximum 10.0 CVSS Score](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/sap-patches-four-maximum-severity-vulnerabilities-cvss-10/) (critical)
  SAP's November Security Patch Day includes fixes for 35 vulnerabilities, four of which are rated with the maximum CVSS score of 10.0. These critical flaws, discovered by security firm Onapsis and named ICM2SHELL and PROXYGATE, could allow an unauthenticated attacker to achieve remote code execution on affected SAP NetWeaver, Web Dispatcher, and Gateway systems. While not yet exploited in the wild, a working exploit has been developed, and customers are urged to patch immediately.
- [Nitori Data Breach May Expose Records of 37.62 Million Customers](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/japanese-retailer-nitori-reports-data-breach-affecting-37-million-customers/) (high)
  Japanese home furnishings giant Nitori Holdings has announced a major data breach that may have exposed the personal records of up to 37.62 million customers. The breach, which occurred in October due to unauthorized access, compromised customer email and home addresses. Nitori has confirmed that financial information like credit card details was not affected but is warning customers to be vigilant for phishing attempts.
- [CIRO Reaches $8.3M Settlement for 2025 Data Breach Affecting 750k](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/canadian-regulator-ciro-reaches-8-million-settlement-for-2025-data-breach/) (medium)
  The Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO) has agreed to a proposed $8.3 million national settlement for a class-action lawsuit following a 2025 data breach. The incident, which originated from a phishing attack, compromised the personal and financial data of approximately 750,000 Canadians, including Social Insurance Numbers and investment account details. The settlement, which still requires court approval, aims to compensate affected individuals.
- [Children's Foundation Data Breach Occurs via Third-Party IT Provider](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/the-childrens-foundation-discloses-data-breach-via-it-provider/) (medium)
  The Children's Foundation has reported a data breach that occurred through a third-party IT service provider, Highland Shores Children's Aid Society. An unauthorized party accessed the provider's network in July, encrypted systems, and may have exposed the personal information of the foundation's volunteers, donors, and program applicants. The compromised data is limited to names and contact information; no financial data was affected.
