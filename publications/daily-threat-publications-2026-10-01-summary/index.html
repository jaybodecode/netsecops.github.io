Today New Articles

Cisco Patches Actively Exploited SD-WAN Auth Bypass Zero-Day

Cisco has patched a critical authentication bypass zero-day (CVE-2026-76504, CVSS 9.8) in its Catalyst SD-WAN Manager. The flaw, caused by improper URI handling, allows remote unauthenticated attackers to gain admin access. CISA has added it to the KEV catalog, urging immediate patching as there are no workarounds.

Dutch Security Institute Hacked via AI-Powered Zammad Zero-Days

The Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure (DIVD) was compromised by what it describes as an "agentic AI-powered attack." The sophisticated intrusion chained two zero-day vulnerabilities in the Zammad helpdesk software (CVE-2026-102489 and CVE-2026-102490) to achieve remote code execution and full system control, leading to a data breach.

Europol Dismantles KillSec Ransomware; Teenager Suspected Leader

An international law enforcement operation, codenamed Operation KillSwitch, has dismantled the infrastructure of the KillSec ransomware group. Led by German authorities and supported by Europol, the operation seized the group's darknet leak site and secured 110 TB of stolen data. A 16-year-old is suspected of being the group's main operator.

Russian APT Star Blizzard Evolves Phishing with 'RedFlick' Technique

The Russian APT group Star Blizzard (FSB Centre 18) has adopted a new, more efficient malware delivery technique called "RedFlick" to deploy its CosmicPulse backdoor. The new method streamlines phishing campaigns against Ukraine-linked targets, including NGOs and journalists, by requiring only a single user interaction to initiate the infection chain.

Data Breaches at Healthcare & Professional Services Firms Disclosed

Several US-based organizations, including Modoc Medical Center and Blanchard Training & Development, have recently disclosed data breaches that occurred earlier in 2026. The public notifications reveal the exposure of sensitive data, including personal, financial, and protected health information (PHI) affecting thousands of individuals across the healthcare, legal, and professional services sectors.

Gartner Advises CISOs to Prioritize AI Risks, Preemptive Security

Technology research firm Gartner has released new guidance for CISOs, urging them to prioritize managing AI-related risks, budget for preemptive security capabilities, and treat all frontier AI models as a potential insider risk. The advice, published on September 30, comes as AI-augmented attacks accelerate, shrinking response times for defenders.

Citrix Patches Two Critical NetScaler Zero-Days Under Active Attack

Update:Further analysis of the Citrix NetScaler zero-day (CVE-2026-88772) reveals that suspected state-sponsored actors initiated exploitation as early as September 3, 2026, three weeks prior to public disclosure. These sophisticated threat actors deployed specific custom malware, including the WHIPSHOT web shell for persistent access and the SLAPSHOT tunneling tool for lateral movement. The campaign specifically targeted high-value organizations in government and financial sectors across North America and Europe. This new information underscores the advanced nature of the attacks and the extended period of compromise for affected entities. Additionally, a temporary mitigation of disabling the DTLS feature is now suggested if immediate patching is not feasible, though patching remains the primary solution.

Citrix NetScaler Zero-Days Actively Exploited in the Wild

Update:New analysis of the NetScaler zero-day vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-88771, CVE-2026-88772) provides critical technical insights. Attackers are using two distinct methods: a crafted DTLS packet for RCE/DoS and a three-stage log poisoning technique to deploy a sophisticated PHP web shell (`nsg64.deb`). The web shell features RC4-encrypted C2 and abuses SUID binaries for privilege escalation. Exploitation has been active since at least August 21, 2026. Specific IOCs, including IP addresses, file hashes, and log patterns, are now available to aid detection and response efforts. MITRE ATT&CK techniques have also been mapped.

Industry Leaders Warn AI-Powered Attacks Are Outpacing Defenses

Update:A new report from Google's Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) on October 1, 2026, quantifies the impact of AI on cybersecurity, revealing a more than 100% increase in disclosed vulnerabilities and a 241% rise in high-risk disclosures in the first eight months of 2026. The report highlights AI's role in accelerating the exploitation of N-day vulnerabilities, with average monthly exploitations rising from 10.5 to 18. Network edge and security appliances are identified as prime targets, significantly compressing the 'patch gap' and increasing risk for organizations.
