# Active Exploits, Urgent Advisories, and New Ransomware Claims

**Published:** 2026-09-26 | **Articles:** 8

**Critical Vulnerabilities Under Active Exploitation**:

*   **ShinyHunters Bypasses WAFs in Renewed Oracle PeopleSoft Attacks**: The threat actor group ShinyHunters is actively exploiting a critical Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability (CVE-2026-35273) using a URL-encoding trick to bypass Web Application Firewalls. This campaign has led to the deployment of a new backdoor, SIDEEYE, and the use of MeshCentral for persistence, with over 100 organizations notified of their risk.
*   **CISA Adds SharePoint, MikroTik Bugs to Known Exploited List**: CISA has added CVE-2026-65660, a remote code execution flaw in Microsoft SharePoint, and CVE-2026-67279, a workflow bypass in MikroTik RouterOS, to its KEV catalog due to active exploitation. Federal agencies are mandated to patch these vulnerabilities by September 28, 2026.

**New Threats and Advisories**:

*   **Kiteworks Urges Shutdown After Warning of Imminent Cyberattack**: Secure file-transfer vendor Kiteworks has issued an unprecedented advisory urging customers to shut down systems for a 6-9 hour window over the weekend, citing credible threat intelligence about a potential imminent attack. The move is preventative, with no known compromises at this time.
*   **Microsoft Details Destructive Cloud Tactics of JADEPUFFER**: Microsoft has detailed a destructive campaign by Storm-3168 (JADEPUFFER) in Microsoft Azure, where compromised service principals were used for automated attacks. This 'agentic ransomware' group executed rapid, destructive actions within minutes, aiming to cripple victims and prevent recovery.

**Ransomware Activity and Claims**:

*   **Qilin Ransomware Claims Attack on U.S. Manufacturer All Tech**: The Qilin ransomware group has claimed an attack on U.S.-based precision machining company All Tech Machine & Engineering, Inc., alleging the theft of internal data as part of a double-extortion strategy.
*   **ZaWoo Ransomware Claims Attack on French Accounting Firm**: The ZaWoo ransomware group has listed Agiliance, a French accounting and business advisory firm, on its data leak site, claiming a compromise on August 18 and potentially exposing sensitive financial and client data.
*   **KRYBIT Ransomware Claims Attack on National Airline of Tanzania**: The KRYBIT ransomware group has claimed an attack on Air Tanzania Company Limited (ATCL), the national airline of Tanzania, alleging data theft and threatening to release it, highlighting risks to critical transportation infrastructure.
*   **Charles & Keith Confirms Cyberattack After Ransomware Listing**: Singaporean fashion brand Charles & Keith Group has confirmed a cybersecurity incident affecting some servers, following its listing on the data leak site of the ransomware group 'TheGentlemen'. The company is investigating the extent of the data impact while operations continue normally.

## Articles in this publication
- [ShinyHunters Bypasses WAFs in Renewed Oracle PeopleSoft Attacks](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/shinyhunters-bypasses-firewalls-in-renewed-oracle-peoplesoft-attacks/) (critical)
  The threat actor group ShinyHunters (UNC6240) has resumed mass exploitation of a critical Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability, CVE-2026-35273. The new campaign uses a URL-encoding trick to bypass Web Application Firewall (WAF) rules previously implemented as a mitigation. Google has notified over 100 organizations of their risk, with dozens already compromised. The attackers are deploying a new backdoor called SIDEEYE and using the legitimate remote management tool MeshCentral for persistence, expanding their targeting from universities to healthcare, technology, and government sectors.
- [Kiteworks Urges Shutdown After Warning of Imminent Cyberattack](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/kiteworks-issues-urgent-shutdown-advisory-after-federal-threat-warning/) (high)
  Secure file-transfer vendor Kiteworks sent an urgent advisory to customers, urging them to shut down their systems for a 6-9 hour window over the weekend. This unprecedented recommendation followed the receipt of "credible threat intelligence from federal intelligence authorities" about a potential imminent attack. The company stated the move was preventative, as it is not aware of any current compromises. However, the advisory raised concerns about a potential zero-day vulnerability, prompting Kiteworks to advise a coordinated global downtime as a precaution.
- [CISA Adds SharePoint, MikroTik Bugs to Known Exploited List](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/cisa-adds-actively-exploited-sharepoint-and-mikrotik-bugs-to-kev-catalog/) (high)
  The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added two new vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, indicating active attacks. The first, CVE-2026-65660, is a remote code execution flaw in Microsoft SharePoint with an 8.8 CVSS score. The second, CVE-2026-67279, is a medium-severity workflow bypass in MikroTik RouterOS that can be chained with another bug for full administrative control. Federal agencies are mandated to patch these vulnerabilities by September 28, 2026.
- [Microsoft Details Destructive Cloud Tactics of JADEPUFFER](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/microsoft-details-cloud-destruction-tactics-of-jadepuffer-ransomware-group/) (critical)
  Microsoft has detailed a destructive cloud campaign in Microsoft Azure by the threat actor Storm-3168 (JADEPUFFER). This 'agentic ransomware' group used compromised service principals for automated attacks. After a 15-hour reconnaissance phase, the actor executed a destructive phase lasting just seven minutes, deleting storage accounts, an Azure Key Vault, and attempting to wipe recovery backups. The attack highlights a shift towards highly automated, rapid-destruction cloud attacks that aim to cripple victims and prevent recovery.
- [Qilin Ransomware Claims Attack on U.S. Manufacturer All Tech](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/qilin-ransomware-group-claims-attack-on-us-manufacturer-all-tech/) (high)
  The Qilin ransomware group has listed All Tech Machine & Engineering, Inc., a U.S.-based precision machining company, as a victim on its dark web data leak site. The claim, which appeared around September 24, 2026, alleges the theft of internal data. This is part of the group's double-extortion strategy to pressure victims into paying a ransom. All Tech has not publicly confirmed the incident, which highlights the ongoing threat of ransomware to the manufacturing sector.
- [ZaWoo Ransomware Claims Attack on French Accounting Firm](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/french-accounting-firm-agiliance-targeted-by-zawoo-ransomware/) (high)
  The ZaWoo ransomware group has listed Agiliance, a French accounting and business advisory firm, on its data leak site. The post, which appeared on September 24, 2026, claims the firm was compromised on August 18. Agiliance, which comprises eight accounting practices, has not confirmed the incident. A breach could expose sensitive financial and client data, posing a significant downstream risk for the businesses and individuals it serves in France.
- [KRYBIT Ransomware Claims Attack on National Airline of Tanzania](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/national-airline-of-tanzania-claimed-as-victim-by-krybit-ransomware/) (high)
  The KRYBIT ransomware group has listed Air Tanzania Company Limited (ATCL), the national airline of Tanzania, as a victim on its dark web leak site. The claim, discovered around September 24-25, 2026, suggests the group has stolen data and is threatening to release it. Air Tanzania has not confirmed the attack, but the incident highlights the significant threat ransomware poses to critical transportation infrastructure, with potential risks to passenger data and flight operations.
- [Charles & Keith Confirms Cyberattack After Ransomware Listing](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/charles-keith-confirms-cyberattack-after-ransomware-group-listing/) (high)
  Singaporean fashion brand Charles & Keith Group has confirmed it was hit by a cybersecurity incident that affected some of its servers. The confirmation, issued on September 24, 2026, came after the company was listed on the data leak site of the ransomware group known as 'TheGentlemen'. The company has reported the incident to authorities, engaged cybersecurity experts, and stated that its retail and online operations continue normally while they investigate the extent of the data impact.
