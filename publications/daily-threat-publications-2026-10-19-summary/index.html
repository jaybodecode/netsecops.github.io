OpenCode Breach Exposes OpenAI, Anthropic API Keys, Leads to Fraud

The open-source AI coding agent OpenCode has disclosed a security breach that exposed user API keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The incident, which affected 1.4% of its Console users, resulted from unauthorized access to a legacy system. Attackers used the stolen keys in a 'distillation attack,' running up significant fraudulent charges on user accounts to likely train their own AI models. OpenCode has notified affected users and advised them to revoke and replace the compromised keys.

Helix Ransomware Group Claims Attack on American Campus

The Helix ransomware group has claimed responsibility for a cyberattack on American Campus, a major US-based student housing provider with an estimated annual revenue of $1 billion. The group posted the claim on its dark web leak site on October 19, 2026, threatening to release exfiltrated data within hours. This attack is part of a recent wave of high-profile targets claimed by the Helix group, employing a double extortion strategy.

Gîtes de France Breach Data Added to Have I Been Pwned

Data from a May 2026 breach at the French holiday rental network Gîtes de France has been added to the Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) notification service. The incident, attributed to the hacker 'ChimeraZ,' exposed the personal and booking details of hundreds of thousands of customers. The indexed dataset includes 117,574 unique email addresses, along with names, phone numbers, and physical addresses, putting affected individuals at high risk of targeted phishing scams.

Microsoft Remains Top Impersonated Brand in Q3 2026 Phishing Attacks

According to a Q3 2026 report from Check Point Research, Microsoft continues to be the most impersonated brand in phishing attacks, accounting for 33% of all such attempts. This is a significant jump from the previous quarter and far exceeds other commonly spoofed brands like Apple and Google. The report also highlights a new trend of attackers impersonating AI and developer platforms, such as Anthropic's Claude and Google's Antigravity, to steal credentials, payment details, and achieve remote code execution.

Burger King Japan App Breach Exposes 11.18 Million Member Records

Update:New information clarifies that user passwords were not compromised in the Burger King Japan app data breach, as they were not stored on the affected system. The update also provides more specific potential attack techniques (TTPs) such as SQL injection and exploitation of public-facing applications. Additionally, enhanced mitigation strategies for organizations, including Database Activity Monitoring and Network Egress Filtering, are detailed. The exposed data now explicitly includes 'Prefecture of residence' as part of the PII.

China-Linked Group Exploits BeyondTrust Zero-Day in Espionage Attacks

Update:The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has issued a critical alert, adding CVE-2026-102764 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. This mandates U.S. federal agencies to patch the BeyondTrust Remote Support flaw immediately and strongly advises all organizations to do the same. The vulnerability, an authentication bypass, continues to be actively exploited as a zero-day by the China-linked ARMORY CASTLE group since August 2026, for initial access, privilege escalation, and deploying custom malware like SAWFLY and BARECLAW. BeyondTrust has released patches (BT26-05-RS) for self-hosted versions 26.2.3 and earlier, urging updates to 26.2.4 or later.

Millions of California Students' Data Exposed in Breach at Testing Provider

Update:New reports indicate that class-action lawsuits have been filed against Educational Testing Service (ETS) following the data breach. Further details on the exposed student data now include demographic information such as race and ethnicity, program participation status (e.g., English learner), and statewide assessment results, expanding on previously known categories. The breach was specifically detected on September 25, 2026. Technical analysis also highlights additional MITRE ATT&CK techniques involved, including T1552.001 (Data from Information Repositories) and T1567.002 (Exfiltration Over SFTP).
