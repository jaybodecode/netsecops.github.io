Google Disrupts 1.3M-Device XCPE Botnet, Bans Chinese Manufacturer

Google announced the disruption of the XCPE botnet, a network of over 1.3 million compromised Android TV set-top boxes and other IoT devices. Google's Threat Intelligence Group attributed the botnet's creation and operation to the device manufacturer, Videostrong Technology Co., which allegedly pre-installed the malware in the device firmware for financial gain. The botnet was used for ad fraud and as a residential proxy service. In response, Google has taken down C2 servers and banned Videostrong from the Android ecosystem.

UK NCSC: AI-Powered Ransomware on Critical Infrastructure Likely by 2028

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has warned that an AI-powered ransomware attack targeting the nation's critical infrastructure is 'highly likely' by 2028. In its annual 'State of the Cyber Nation' report, the GCHQ division stated that AI will not invent new attack types but will dramatically increase the speed and scale at which existing vulnerabilities can be exploited. The NCSC anticipates an 'unprecedented rise' in cyber incidents and urges organizations to focus on fundamental cybersecurity hygiene to defend against these accelerated threats.

Android's November Patch Fixes Critical Zero-Click RCE, 'SMS of Death'

Google and Samsung have rolled out the November 2026 Android security update, addressing over 200 vulnerabilities. The patch fixes several critical flaws, including CVE-2026-58868, a zero-click remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability in the Android Framework that requires no user interaction to exploit. Another critical fix, CVE-2026-58882, resolves the 'SMS of Death' bug that could allow an attacker to remotely crash devices via a text message. The update covers Android versions 14 through 17 and includes specific patches for Samsung Galaxy devices.

New 'VOLTA' Infostealer Uses AI to Prioritize and Rank Stolen Data

A new Windows information-stealing malware, 'VOLTA-AUTODOXER,' has been discovered using large language models (LLMs) like Anthropic's Claude and Google's Gemini to analyze and rank stolen data for monetization. The malware exfiltrates a wide array of data, including browser credentials, crypto wallets, and session tokens. It then feeds this data to an LLM with a prompt to act as a 'forensic intelligence analyst,' automating the process of identifying the most financially valuable victims. This marks a significant evolution in infostealer capabilities.

Revolve Data Breach Exposes Payment Card Info of Over 197,000 Customers

Online fashion retailer Revolve Group Inc. has disclosed a data breach that occurred on September 11, 2026, affecting at least 197,072 customers. An unauthorized actor used 'automated means' to access the company's e-commerce platform and steal customer names, addresses, and saved payment card information, including card numbers and expiration dates. The company began notifying affected individuals on October 29 and has reported the incident to law enforcement.

CISA Launches 2026 Critical Infrastructure Security & Resilience Month

Update:The 2026 Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month campaign is themed 'Resolve to be Resilient.' CISA emphasizes strengthening defenses against physical and cyber threats, including those amplified by geopolitical instability, in addition to artificial intelligence. The agency is promoting the Infrastructure Resilience Planning Framework (IRPF) as a key resource for organizations to identify critical assets, understand risks, and develop robust plans. Furthermore, CISA encourages engagement with Information Sharing and Analysis Centers (ISACs) to stay informed on current threats and best practices.
