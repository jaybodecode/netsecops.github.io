APT29 Targets iOS & Android Users in 'Poisoned Source' Campaign

CRITICAL

APT29 Uses Watering Holes, Supply Chain Attack on Mobile Users

The Russian SVR-linked threat group APT29 (aka Midnight Blizzard, Cozy Bear) has been identified as the actor behind a large-scale intelligence-gathering campaign targeting iOS and Android users. According to research from Sekoia, the 'Poisoned Source' operation used multiple vectors, including watering hole attacks on high-profile websites and a supply chain compromise of the Ukrainian web analytics firm Plerdy. The attackers injected malicious code to fingerprint devices and deliver n-day exploit chains, including the DarkSword kit for iOS, to victims primarily in Ukraine and the United States.

Barracuda Warns of Critical Flaws Allowing Root Access in ESG

CRITICAL

Barracuda ESG Flaws Allow Unauthenticated Root Access

Barracuda Networks is urging customers to immediately patch two vulnerabilities in its Email Security Gateway (ESG) appliances. The flaws, CVE-2026-116606 and CVE-2026-111560, can be chained together by an unauthenticated attacker to bypass authorization and execute commands with root privileges. An updated advisory clarified that administrative privileges are not required for exploitation, significantly increasing the threat level. This follows a history of ESG appliances being targeted by sophisticated threat actors for espionage.

Chinese APT 'SlippyNunchucks' Hijacks Sogou App for Malware

HIGH

SlippyNunchucks APT Abuses Sogou App via DNS Hijacking

The China-aligned threat group 'SlippyNunchucks' is conducting a surveillance campaign by abusing the popular Sogou Input Method editor, developed by Tencent. According to ESET Research, the group uses DNS hijacking to exploit an insecure HTTP communication flaw (CVE-2026-120422) in the application's vocabulary promotion mechanism. This allows them to deliver their 'DarkVigil' malware to targets in Hong Kong, Thailand, Japan, and other countries in Asia and Europe. Tencent has since released a patch to migrate the affected communication to HTTPS.

'Black October' Attacks Steal API Keys from Nine AI Dev Platforms

HIGH

Black October: Coordinated Attacks on AI Developer Platforms

Update:

The ongoing campaign targeting AI developer platforms has expanded, now affecting over a dozen entities including Chainstack, WorkOS, Trigger.dev, and Brev. Threat actors are utilizing AI-assisted offensive tools, notably the open-source Artex framework, to automate intrusions. Initial access vectors are more diverse than previously reported, including compromised credentials, vulnerable third-party dependencies, and zero-day exploits like CVE-2026-102489 and CVE-2026-102490 in the Zammad ticketing system. Case studies for Chainstack and WorkOS breaches provide further insight into the evolving tactics.

European Supermarkets Suffer Second Wave of Refrigeration Cyberattacks

HIGH

Supermarket Refrigeration Systems Hacked Across Europe

Update:

New details confirm the cyberattacks on European supermarket refrigeration systems also impacted Finland, Luxembourg, and the Netherlands, expanding the known scope beyond the initial seven countries. The Coop chain has been identified as an additional victim. Authorities are now framing the incident as a potential act of hybrid warfare targeting critical food supply infrastructure, elevating the perceived threat level. UK NCSC: AI-Powered Ransomware on Critical Infrastructure Likely by 2028

INFORMATIONAL

NCSC Warns of AI-Ransomware Threat to UK CNI

Update:

The NCSC's annual report provides further details on the AI-powered ransomware threat to UK CNI. CEO Richard Horne emphasized the severe societal disruption possible, warning of potential food shortages or power outages. The report explicitly criticizes organizations for technical debt and reliance on end-of-life systems, which create fertile ground for AI-driven attacks. In response, the UK government announced £60 million for a national Cyber Shield and £70 million for a National Exposure and Real-Time Vulnerability (NERV) Centre. The NCSC also highlighted that CNI operators are subject to NIS Regulations, with potential fines for failing to implement appropriate security measures.

Android's November Patch Fixes Critical Zero-Click RCE, 'SMS of Death'

CRITICAL

Android November 2026 Update Patches 200+ Flaws

Update:

The November 2026 Android security update details have been further clarified. The 2026-11-01 patch level addresses 119 vulnerabilities in core Android OS components like Framework and System, including the critical CVE-2026-58868. The 2026-11-05 patch level covers 97 vulnerabilities in vendor-specific components from Arm, Imagination Technologies, MediaTek, Unisoc, and Qualcomm. New detection methods, such as checking the security patch level via `getprop ro.build.version.security_patch`, and comprehensive remediation steps have been provided to help users and organizations secure their devices.
