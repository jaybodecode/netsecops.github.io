# Cloud Breaches, Critical Vulnerabilities, and Ransomware Attacks Dominate Cybersecurity News

**Published:** 2026-10-26 | **Articles:** 8

**Critical Vulnerabilities and Exploitation:**

- **PHP2Shell Flaws Actively Exploited (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619)**: These critical RCE vulnerabilities, with a CVSS score of 9.2, are being actively exploited. PHP versions 8.1 and older are End-of-Life and unpatchable, necessitating immediate decommissioning or upgrade. Mitigation strategies include network segmentation and reducing the attack surface.
- **GitLab Patches Critical CVSS 10.0 Unauthenticated RCE Flaw (CVE-2026-110264)**: GitLab has released patches for a critical unauthenticated RCE vulnerability. New cyber observables are available to aid detection, including monitoring web server logs and GitLab processes for anomalous behavior.
- **Oracle's October CPU Delivers Record 1,295 Security Patches**: Oracle's latest Critical Patch Update addresses 1,295 vulnerabilities, including six critical flaws with a CVSS score of 10.0 that allow unauthenticated remote code execution. Products like Oracle Access Manager and Oracle SOA Suite are among those impacted.

**Cloud Service Compromises and Supply Chain Attacks:**

- **[UPDATE] SoftBank Cloud Unit Crippled by Ransomware, 495 Orgs Affected**: IDC Frontier, SoftBank's cloud unit, experienced a ransomware attack. While large-scale data exfiltration is not evident, attackers accessed and viewed registration records for 48 customers, including login IDs and hashed passwords.
- **[UPDATE] ConnectWise Disables Cloud Services After Supply Chain Attack on M365**: Microsoft has attributed the ConnectWise Cloud Backup supply chain attack to Storm-0940, which used compromised application credentials to access 173 M365 customer and partner environments. Attackers attempted to establish persistence by adding new credentials to other trusted applications.
- **[UPDATE] LiteLLM Supply Chain Attack Steals Cloud and AI Service Keys**: Attackers compromised LiteLLM's release pipeline using stolen GitHub CLI OAuth tokens. The malicious package modified Python startup hooks for persistence and included a remote access trojan (RAT) backdoor.

**New Threats and Data Breaches:**

- **[NEW] Dairy Queen Operator Fourteen Foods Discloses Data Breach**: Fourteen Foods, an operator of Dairy Queen restaurants, disclosed a data breach exposing sensitive personal information, including names and Social Security numbers. The breach occurred in February 2026, with impacted individuals notified eight months later.
- **[NEW] Panzer Ransomware Group Claims Attack on IT Firm Yodingenierie**: The Panzer ransomware group has claimed responsibility for a cyberattack on Yodingenierie, an IT and engineering company. The breach was discovered on October 25, 2026, highlighting the continued global reach of ransomware operations.

## Articles in this publication
- [SoftBank Cloud Unit Crippled by Ransomware, 495 Orgs Affected](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/softbank-subsidiary-idcf-crippled-by-ransomware-impacting-495-organizations/) (critical)
  A catastrophic ransomware attack has struck IDC Frontier (IDCF), a Japanese cloud subsidiary of SoftBank, disrupting services for 495 corporate and government clients. The attack on its IDCF Cloud platform's East Japan Region 1 resulted in a widespread shutdown of virtual servers. The company has warned customers that their data may be unrecoverable, forcing them to rebuild systems from their own backups. The incident has caused a significant supply chain crisis, affecting logistics, transportation, and public services across Japan.
- [ConnectWise Disables Cloud Services After Supply Chain Attack on M365](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/connectwise-disables-cloud-services-after-supply-chain-attack/) (critical)
  ConnectWise has suspended its Cloud Backup services following a sophisticated supply chain attack. Threat actors compromised an application credential, gaining unauthorized access to the Microsoft 365 tenants of numerous Managed Service Providers (MSPs) and their customers. The attack, which bypasses user-level MFA, allowed for broad data access and privilege escalation. ConnectWise has disabled the affected application identities and is investigating the incident, which Microsoft has classified as an active supply chain attack.
- [LiteLLM Supply Chain Attack Steals Cloud and AI Service Keys](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/litellm-hit-by-supply-chain-attack-stealing-cloud-credentials/) (high)
  The popular AI gateway tool LiteLLM has suffered its second supply chain attack in a year. On October 21, 2026, a malicious version, v1.107.0, was published to the PyPI and Docker Hub registries. The compromised package contained malware designed to harvest sensitive credentials, including cloud logins, developer secrets, and API keys for AI services. All users who may have installed the malicious version are urged to rotate all credentials immediately.
- [PHP2Shell Flaws Actively Exploited, Triggering Global Scramble](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/php2shell-vulnerabilities-cve-2026-109618-cve-2026-109619-trigger-global-patch-scramble/) (critical)
  Two critical unauthenticated remote code execution (RCE) vulnerabilities in PHP, dubbed 'PHP2Shell' and tracked as CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, are under active, widespread exploitation. Affecting an estimated 70% of websites, the flaws were exploited before patches were officially released, leading to emergency advisories from the NSA and CISA. The vulnerabilities, which stem from memory corruption issues in the PHP engine, allow attackers to take full control of servers. Organizations globally have taken systems offline as a precaution while a frantic race to apply patches is underway. CISA has added both CVEs to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog.
- [Oracle's October CPU Delivers Record 1,295 Security Patches](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/oracle-october-2026-cpu-delivers-record-1295-security-patches/) (high)
  Oracle has released its October 2026 Critical Patch Update (CPU), a massive security release containing 1,295 new patches for 1,209 unique vulnerabilities. The update spans 33 product families, including widely used enterprise software like Oracle Fusion Middleware, E-Business Suite, MySQL, and Java SE. Among the fixes, 95 are for critical vulnerabilities, many of which could permit unauthenticated remote code execution. Oracle strongly urges customers to apply these patches without delay to protect against potential exploitation.
- [GitLab Patches Critical CVSS 10.0 Unauthenticated RCE Flaw](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/gitlab-patches-critical-cvss-10-unauthenticated-rce-vulnerability-cve-2026-110264/) (critical)
  GitLab has released an emergency patch for CVE-2026-110264, a critical integer overflow vulnerability with a CVSS score of 10.0. The flaw allows an unauthenticated attacker to achieve remote code execution on affected self-managed GitLab Community and Enterprise Edition servers. The vulnerability impacts a wide range of versions dating back to 7.0. GitLab.com instances are not affected, but all users of self-managed installations are urged to update to version 19.5.1 immediately to prevent a full server compromise.
- [Dairy Queen Operator Fourteen Foods Discloses Data Breach](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/fourteen-foods-data-breach-exposes-social-security-numbers/) (medium)
  Fourteen Foods, a large operator of Dairy Queen Grill & Chill restaurants, has disclosed a data breach that exposed sensitive personal information, including names and Social Security numbers. The breach occurred in February 2026 when an unauthorized party accessed the company's network. Impacted individuals were notified in October 2026, eight months after the initial intrusion was detected, highlighting significant delays in the incident response and notification process.
- [Panzer Ransomware Group Claims Attack on IT Firm Yodingenierie](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/panzer-ransomware-group-claims-attack-on-yodingenierie/) (high)
  The Panzer ransomware group has claimed responsibility for a cyberattack on Yodingenierie, an IT and engineering company based in Benin and a member of the pan-African NED&CO Group. The breach was discovered on October 25, 2026. This incident highlights the continued global reach of ransomware gangs, targeting organizations of all sizes and sectors, including in developing regions.
