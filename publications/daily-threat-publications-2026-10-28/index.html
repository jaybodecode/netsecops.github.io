CRITICAL

CISA Adds Brotli & Adobe Flaws to KEV Catalog

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added two actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. The flaws include a critical out-of-bounds write in Google's Brotli library, dubbed Node2Shell (CVE-2026-111497), and a file upload vulnerability in Adobe Commerce/Magento (CVE-2026-48356). Federal agencies are now mandated to patch by November 10, 2026.

PHP2Shell: Critical Flaws Trigger Emergency Patches, Mass Scans

CRITICAL

PHP2Shell Flaws Spark Emergency Patches and Mass Exploitation

Update:

A China-linked APT group is actively exploiting PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619) in combination with a Linux kernel privilege escalation flaw, Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300), to gain root access on internet-facing firewalls. The campaign involves reconnaissance using FOFA, leading to the compromise of 25 firewalls, including one belonging to a U.S. government-affiliated organization. Attackers deploy PHP webshells and establish reverse SSH tunnels for persistence, indicating a highly targeted and sophisticated operation with espionage motives. This represents a significant escalation in the threat landscape for PHP2Shell.

Citrix Zero-Days and Oracle PeopleSoft Flaw Under Active Exploit

CRITICAL

Threat Advisory: Citrix & Oracle Flaws Actively Exploited

Update:

New research from the Zero Day Initiative details that CVE-2026-35273 is not a single flaw but a complex chain of three vulnerabilities. It combines a Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF) in the Integration Gateway, a default trust misconfiguration in the Management Hub (PSEMHUB) allowing local loopback trust, and a third bug for arbitrary code execution. This chain enables unauthenticated RCE. Affected versions include PeopleTools 8.61 and 8.62. A critical workaround involves editing the `allowedhosts.properties` file to remove `127.0.0.1` and `localhost` entries, breaking the trust relationship exploited by the chain.
