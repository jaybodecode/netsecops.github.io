# CISA Issues KEV Alerts for Actively Exploited Zero-Days in Microsoft Exchange and Cisco SD-WAN

**Published:** 2026-05-17 | **Articles:** 12

This cybersecurity brief for May 16-17, 2026, covers a series of critical vulnerabilities and high-profile cyberattacks. Key events include the active exploitation of zero-day flaws in Microsoft Exchange (CVE-2026-42897) and Cisco SD-WAN (CVE-2026-20182), both added to CISA's KEV catalog. Additionally, two unpatched Windows zero-days were publicly disclosed, a major supply chain attack compromised the TanStack ecosystem affecting OpenAI, and ed-tech giant Instructure confirmed paying a ransom to the ShinyHunters group after a massive data breach. These incidents highlight escalating threats to enterprise infrastructure, software supply chains, and educational institutions.

## Articles in this publication
- [ShinyHunters Claims Massive Canvas Breach, Disrupting 275 Million Users at 9,000 Institutions](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/shinyhunters-claims-massive-canvas-breach-affecting-9000-institutions/) (critical)
  The notorious hacking group ShinyHunters has claimed responsibility for a catastrophic data breach of the Canvas Learning Management System (LMS), owned by Instructure. The attack allegedly resulted in the theft of 3.65 TB of data belonging to 275 million users across nearly 9,000 educational institutions worldwide. The compromised data includes full names, email addresses, student IDs, and private messages, creating a severe risk of targeted phishing attacks. The incident caused widespread service outages during final exams and was compounded by a secondary attack where attackers defaced login portals with a public ransom note, escalating panic and disruption across the global education sector.
- [High-Severity Flaw in JetBrains TeamCity On-Premises Allows API Exposure (CVE-2026-44413)](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/jetbrains-teamcity-vulnerability-cve-2026-44413-exposes-api/) (high)
  JetBrains has disclosed and patched a high-severity, post-authentication vulnerability (CVE-2026-44413) in its TeamCity On-Premises CI/CD server. The flaw allows any authenticated user, regardless of their permissions, to expose parts of the server API to unauthorized external users. All versions up to 2025.11.4 are affected. JetBrains has released version 2026.1 to fix the issue and has also provided a security patch plugin for users who cannot upgrade immediately. TeamCity Cloud instances are not affected.
- [New 'Mini Shai-Hulud' Worm Hits npm & PyPI in Major Supply Chain Attack](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/mini-shai-hulud-worm-compromises-over-170-npm-and-pypi-packages/) (critical)
  A sophisticated, self-propagating worm dubbed 'Mini Shai-Hulud' has compromised over 170 packages across the npm and PyPI repositories. Attributed to the threat group TeamPCP, the attack hijacked the release pipelines of major projects like TanStack, Mistral AI, and UiPath. The worm steals a wide range of developer credentials and then uses them to publish new infected packages, further spreading the compromise. A critical vulnerability, CVE-2026-45321, has been assigned to the TanStack compromise.
- [OpenAI Launches 'Daybreak' to Automate Vulnerability Hunting with AI](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/openai-launches-daybreak-to-automate-cyber-defense/) (informational)
  OpenAI has launched 'Daybreak,' a new cybersecurity initiative that uses its advanced AI models, including the new GPT-5.5-Cyber, to help organizations automatically find, validate, and remediate software vulnerabilities. Announced on May 12, 2026, the platform is a direct response to Anthropic's 'Project Glasswing,' marking a new front in the competition to apply frontier AI to solve complex cybersecurity challenges.
- [New 'Fragnesia' Linux Flaw (CVE-2026-46300) Allows Root Access; PoC Exploit Released](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/fragnesia-linux-kernel-vulnerability-cve-2026-46300-allows-root-access/) (critical)
  A new high-severity local privilege escalation (LPE) vulnerability in the Linux kernel, dubbed 'Fragnesia' (CVE-2026-46300), has been publicly disclosed along with a working proof-of-concept exploit. The flaw exists in the kernel's XFRM ESP-in-TCP subsystem and allows an unprivileged local user to corrupt the page cache and overwrite read-only files, leading to root access. Fragnesia is an extension of the recent 'Dirty Frag' family of bugs and reportedly bypasses the patches released just days ago. The immediate public release of the PoC puts unpatched systems at significant risk, forcing administrators into a race to apply mitigations or vendor patches.
- [Researcher Drops Two Windows Zero-Days, 'YellowKey' and 'GreenPlasma,' Exposing BitLocker and Escalating Privileges](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/researcher-drops-two-unpatched-windows-zero-day-exploits-yellowkey-and-greenplasma/) (critical)
  A security researcher has publicly released proof-of-concept exploits for two unpatched Windows zero-day vulnerabilities, dubbed 'YellowKey' and 'GreenPlasma'. The first, YellowKey, is a critical BitLocker encryption bypass affecting Windows 11 and Server editions, while GreenPlasma is a local privilege escalation flaw. The researcher released the exploits out of frustration with Microsoft's bug reporting process, creating a significant risk as the PoCs are now public and reportedly being used in attacks.
- [Microsoft Exchange Zero-Day CVE-2026-42897 Under Active Exploitation, Mitigation Urged](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/microsoft-exchange-zero-day-cve-2026-42897-under-active-exploitation/) (critical)
  Microsoft has confirmed a new zero-day vulnerability, CVE-2026-42897, in on-premises Exchange Servers is being actively exploited in the wild. The flaw, a cross-site scripting (XSS) and spoofing issue affecting Exchange Server 2016, 2019, and Subscription Edition, allows a remote, unauthenticated attacker to perform spoofing attacks. Microsoft has released mitigation guidance and strongly recommends enabling the Exchange Emergency Mitigation Service (EEMS) as a security update is not yet available. This incident highlights the ongoing risk associated with on-premises email infrastructure and the necessity for administrators to apply temporary fixes promptly.
- [Cisco Warns of Actively Exploited Critical Auth Bypass Flaw in SD-WAN](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/critical-cisco-sd-wan-flaw-cve-2026-20182-actively-exploited/) (critical)
  Cisco has issued an urgent warning about a critical authentication bypass vulnerability, CVE-2026-20182, in its Catalyst SD-WAN products. The flaw, which has a maximum CVSS severity score of 10.0, is being actively exploited in the wild. It allows a remote, unauthenticated attacker to gain administrative privileges on affected controllers and managers, potentially leading to full network compromise. Attackers have been observed adding SSH keys and modifying network configurations. The vulnerability affects all deployment types, and Cisco urges customers to apply available patches immediately.
- [Funnel Builder WordPress Plugin Flaw Actively Exploited to Skim Payments from 40,000+ Stores](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/critical-flaw-in-funnel-builder-wordpress-plugin-actively-exploited/) (critical)
  A critical, unauthenticated vulnerability in the Funnel Builder WordPress plugin is under active exploitation, affecting over 40,000 e-commerce sites using WooCommerce. Attackers are injecting malicious JavaScript that acts as a payment skimmer, stealing customer credit card numbers and other sensitive data from checkout pages. The developer, FunnelKit, has released a patch (version 3.15.0.3) and urges immediate updates.
- [European Hospitals Now See Cyberattacks as Direct Threats to Patient Care, Not Just Data](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/european-hospital-cyber-risk-shifts-to-patient-care/) (informational)
  A Black Book Research survey of 284 European hospital executives reveals a significant shift in the perception of cyber risk. Attacks are no longer seen merely as IT or data privacy issues but as direct threats to patient care and safety. The study found 82% of leaders have extreme concerns about attacks, yet confidence in maintaining safe operations plummets from 59% at 24 hours post-attack to just 14% after 72 hours, highlighting a critical resilience gap.
- [UK's HMRC Taps Quantexa AI to Dismantle Cyber-Enabled Tax Fraud Rings](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/uk-hmrc-deploys-quantexa-ai-to-combat-tax-fraud/) (informational)
  The UK's HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) has awarded a ten-year, £175 million contract to British AI firm Quantexa. The partnership will use Quantexa's AI-driven "decision intelligence" platform to analyze vast datasets, aiming to better detect sophisticated tax fraud, identify errors, and uncover hidden criminal networks. The move represents a major government investment in AI for combating financial crime.
- [AI Drives Investment and Uncertainty in Cyber Insurance Market](https://cyber.netsecops.io/articles/ai-growing-impact-on-cyber-insurance-and-digital-risk/) (informational)
  The cyber insurance market is being profoundly reshaped by artificial intelligence. A Q1 2026 report from Gallagher Re shows 95% of InsurTech funding ($1.63B) targeted AI-focused firms. Simultaneously, a 978% surge in generative AI-related litigation has prompted major insurers like Chubb and Travelers to introduce broad AI liability exclusions in standard commercial policies, creating a complex and uncertain landscape for managing digital risk.
